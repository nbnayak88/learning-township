An approved transfer is not permission to transact

HR01 — Workforce Foundations · GICS07 Financials · OmniCapital Financial Group Ltd. IIL — Industry Immersion Lab · TOS — The Origin Story · 120-minute Sprint · Edition 2026-10-31 Mission: Architecting Experiences for a Better World. Published educational edition; no enrollment, certification or public access implied.

1. One learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one workforce transfer that preserves identity and makes regulated-role authority explicit. One persona-led story · One Decision Build Card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use only synthetic data; no tenant or production action is required.

3. Origins and process evolution

These closed windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniCapital corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; paper, spreadsheets, ERP and cloud practices can coexist through the present.

Teaching window Process response and persistent question
1970–1984 Personnel cards and local branch rosters record who works where; approvals travel separately.
1985–1994 Personnel databases and payroll identifiers reduce re-entry but identity duplicates persist.
1995–2004 Integrated HR/ERP adds positions and dated transactions; permission remains a separate decision.
2005–2014 Web self-service and shared services speed transfers but distribute responsibility across systems.
2015–2025 Cloud HCM and rules connect records to identity and learning; missing evidence still needs a human owner.

Counterexample: digitizing an incomplete record makes it easier to retrieve, not more trustworthy. Which decision was the original record intended to support?

4. One persona-led story

Nadia Fernandes (OCF-P07), Group CHRO, receives an urgent request to move synthetic worker FIN-P100 from branch support into a payments-control team. Ravi Patel (OCF-P14), Payments and Treasury Operations Lead, needs cut-off coverage. The approved job change is effective on 1 November 2026; the receiving role’s authorization record is absent. Omar Haddad (OCF-P10), CISO and Resilience Lead, asks who may release sensitive access.

Choose before revealing: Hold the entire transfer, grant all permissions with the HR approval, or allow the employment change while separately holding sensitive duties? Record your first reason on the card.

Evidence reveal: The synthetic move is within one legal employer. The employment approval is valid, but learning completion contains no evidence of role authorization. Nadia separates the dated HR transaction from the held duties; Ravi requests an approved supervised alternative. If a legal employer changes, this assumed transaction must be redesigned.

Revise your decision: A complete HR record supports an employment decision; it cannot prove licensing, competence, access approval or successful provisioning. Preserve an accessible human route to challenge errors. Financial service continuity matters, but an urgent cut-off does not create authority. Customer KYC and employee readiness are different processes; do not copy customer identity checks into HR without purpose and policy review.

5. Application responsibilities and human authority

Frame the business need first; applications implement the chosen operating model. The following is a proposed teaching architecture. Confirm named delegates, country policy, legal basis and actual product scope before implementation.

Application responsibility Accountable data owner Human decision right
Core HR; Employee Central example HR owns person, employment, position and job records Delegated HR approver authorizes the employment change; Nadia sponsors the model.
Learning and credential register Learning owner / designated credential steward owns current evidence Completion is evidence, not automatic permission for regulated activity.
Role authorization and IAM Receiving business authority owns duties; Security owns access execution Ravi’s designated role approver and authorized Security staff decide within policy; neither HR nor AI bypasses them.

Core HR owns workforce facts; domain owners own performance, learning, rewards, time or payroll facts as applicable. IAM executes access policy; integration transports and reconciles states; analytics reads authorized data and does not become its owner. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder, but cannot approve consequential employment, regulated duties, payments or access. An interface acknowledgment is not proof of the business outcome. SAP examples illustrate responsibilities, not guaranteed features or universal localization.

7. One Decision Build Card

Synthetic input: FIN-P100 / FIN-E100; old branch-support assignment valid through 31 October; new payments-control assignment effective 1 November; approval recorded 30 October; role authorization missing; IAM acknowledgment unknown. These are synthetic facts, not an enterprise staffing baseline.

Keep all work in this one card. Capture: business problem; initial and revised choice with reason; entity/identity/date semantics; authoritative source and accountable owner; human approval boundary; one justified local variant with purpose/minimum data/viewers; known versus unknown downstream state; exception owner and approved fallback; all three test observations and evidence references. Missing evidence stays blank or unknown, never silently becomes approval.

Compare one alternative: a manual controlled handoff may be preferable to automation when exception ownership or recovery is absent. State the evidence needed to change your mind. Avoid collecting real screening, health, payroll, customer or conduct records in this learning card.

8. Exactly three tabletop tests

Test Input / reveal Expected proposed behavior Evidence to record
OCF-HR01-T01 — Before and on effective date Inspect the same person on 31 October and 1 November. Preserve identity and history; show the approved new assignment only on its effective date; duties remain separately held. Two dated views, approval reference and exception owner.
OCF-HR01-T02 — Completion without authority Reveal a completed course but no authorization. Keep sensitive work held; route to designated role authority; explain permitted alternative duties. Evidence distinction, named decision role and worker notice.
OCF-HR01-T03 — Unconfirmed downstream state IAM is unavailable after HR accepts the change. Record HR accepted and IAM unknown separately; use approved escalation; do not grant extra access or declare end-to-end success. Per-system states, recovery owner and reconciliation condition.

For each test, record input → expected → observed → revision/evidence → Pass, Revise or Blocked. All observed results are initially unfilled. Publication does not execute any tabletop, system test, approval or operational transaction. Acceptance requires a defensible card and all three observed tabletop results with revisions; bypassing a protected human decision requires redesign.

10. Separate AI-driven exploration: 2026–2050

Scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps.

Scenario window Question to explore
2026–2030 — Assist Could approved-source assistance explain evidence gaps and draft next steps without approving the decision?
2031–2040 — Connect Could separately authorized services coordinate this handoff while keeping unknown states and local rights visible?
2041–2050 — Adapt Could reviewed feedback propose reversible process changes while people retain consequential authority?

Competing future: fragmented systems, uneven access, policy restrictions and manual review persist; a simpler controlled workflow outperforms orchestration. Falsifiable hypothesis question: Would better data availability reduce transfer exceptions if local approval capacity remains unchanged? Define an observation that would disprove your claim—for example fewer steps but more unresolved exceptions or worse worker outcomes. Compare against a manual baseline before claiming value.

11. Reflect and move to Pre-Foundation

What evidence changed your first decision? Which authority did you initially assume? Who bears the exception burden? What would make you stop this design? Pre-Foundation handoff: Define person, employment, assignment, position, legal entity and effective date. Confirm the same-employer assumption and delegated authority before configuration. Continue the same semi-fiction through Pre-Foundation (concepts/data), Foundation (small working model), Essentials (end-to-end handoffs), Advanced (exceptions and resilience), and Ultimate (architect-level value realization, troubleshooting, lessons and evidence-based ROI). These are future learning handoffs, not published deeper editions or additional Origin Story formats.

12. Architecture alignment and separate axes

TOGAF: compare architecture options and document decisions. BIZBOK: connect capability and value. BABOK: identify needs, requirements and acceptance evidence. DAMA-DMBOK: assign ownership, definitions, quality and lineage. COBIT: distinguish accountability, controls and monitoring. Relevant ISO/IEC information-security principles: confidentiality, integrity, availability, access and controlled change. Alignment is educational; no certification, conformance or compliance is claimed.
