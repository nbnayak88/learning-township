### Concept 1: Strategize and Source: Categories and Sourcing (Day 2 V2)
*You cannot save on spend you have not organised.*  
Summary: Category management groups spend into manageable categories and sets a strategy for each: what to buy, from whom, at what risk and value. Sourcing then runs the events (RFx, auctions, negotiations) that turn the strategy into awarded business.

## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Energy and Industrials run large, project-based sourcing events; Consumer Staples runs high-volume, repeat category cycles.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Health Care and Financial Services need strict supplier qualification and regulatory terms; Utilities manages long-term service and maintenance contracts.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Real Estate and Communication Services buy many services and indirect items; Industrials has high direct-material volume.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Energy and Materials worry about concentration and geopolitical risk; Health Care worries about quality and regulatory standing.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Information Technology tracks cloud and software spend; Consumer Staples tracks commodity and price volatility.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Financial Services requires explainable AI and strong audit trails; Industrials integrates with plant and logistics systems.
