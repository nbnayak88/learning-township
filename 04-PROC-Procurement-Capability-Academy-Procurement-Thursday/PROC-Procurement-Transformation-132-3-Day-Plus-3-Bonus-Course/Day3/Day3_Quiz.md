## Concept check (short answer)
- What does Supplier Risk monitor?  
  *Answer: Financial, operational, geopolitical, ESG and cyber risk of suppliers*
- Which series cover Concept 4?  
  *Answer: AQC7 Ariba Supplier Risk and ARG9 GRC for Procurement*
- Why act on risk early?  
  *Answer: Early risk is a managed decision; late risk is a crisis*
- What does Spend Control Tower provide?  
  *Answer: A single view of spend, savings, compliance and supplier performance*
- Which series cover Concept 5?  
  *Answer: ASP8 Spend Control Tower and Analytics Cloud; AAN0 Analytics Cloud and Databricks*
- Why does spend visibility matter?  
  *Answer: Savings and priorities cannot be verified without it*
- What makes AI trustworthy on procurement data?  
  *Answer: Current integrated data plus governance and explainability*
- Which series cover Concept 6?  
  *Answer: AAI1-PROC Business AI, Joule, AI Agents; AIG2-PROC Integration Suite*
- What does the Integration Suite do for procurement?  
  *Answer: Connects ERP, finance, suppliers and data so information flows once*
