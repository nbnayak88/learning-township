**1. What is the concept?**
Spend Control Tower and SAP Analytics Cloud give a single view of spend, savings, compliance and supplier performance. Analytics Cloud with Databricks adds advanced analysis and prediction on procurement data. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Spend data scattered across systems hides duplicates, leakage and opportunity. Without trusted visibility, savings claims cannot be verified and priorities are set by anecdote.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A technology company classified its spend for the first time and found 30 percent of it with no contract. The dashboard turned an argument about priorities into a ranked list of opportunities. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: One trusted view of spend across the company; Verified savings and compliance tracking; Predictive insight on price, demand and risk; Procurement as a data-driven business partner. [on screen: four benefits]
