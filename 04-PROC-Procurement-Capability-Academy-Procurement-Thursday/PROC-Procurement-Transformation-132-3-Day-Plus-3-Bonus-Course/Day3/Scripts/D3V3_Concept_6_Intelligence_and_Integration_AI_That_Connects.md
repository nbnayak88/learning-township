**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and recommendations into sourcing, contracts and buying. The Integration Suite connects procurement with ERP, finance, suppliers and data platforms so information flows once. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI is only as good as the contracts, suppliers and spend it can reach, and only as safe as its governance. Integration provides the reach; governance and explainability provide the trust.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A procurement team used an assistant to summarise contract clauses, but only after integration gave it the current signed version of each contract. Before that, it quoted outdated terms; afterwards buyers relied on it daily. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster sourcing, contract review and supplier queries; Automated handoffs between procurement, finance and suppliers; Governed, auditable AI on commercial data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
