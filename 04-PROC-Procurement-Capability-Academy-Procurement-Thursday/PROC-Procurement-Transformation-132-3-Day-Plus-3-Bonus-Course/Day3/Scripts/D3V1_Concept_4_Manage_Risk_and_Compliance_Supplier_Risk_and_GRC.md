**1. What is the concept?**
Supplier Risk monitors financial, operational, geopolitical, ESG and cyber risk across your supplier base. GRC for Procurement defines policies, controls and segregation of duties so purchasing stays compliant and auditable. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
A single supplier failure, sanction breach or fraud can halt production and damage reputation. Risk found after the event is a crisis; risk found early is a managed decision.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. An energy company received an alert on a critical supplier's deteriorating finances months before it failed. A backup supplier was already qualified, and production never stopped. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Early warning on supplier risk; Policies and controls that prevent fraud and breaches; Audit-ready evidence of compliant purchasing; Resilience that lets you keep promises to customers. [on screen: four benefits]
