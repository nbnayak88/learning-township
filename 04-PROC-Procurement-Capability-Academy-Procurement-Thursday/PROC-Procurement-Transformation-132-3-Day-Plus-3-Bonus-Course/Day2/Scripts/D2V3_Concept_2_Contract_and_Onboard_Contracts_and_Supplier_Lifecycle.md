**1. What is the concept?**
Contracts manages the full contract lifecycle: authoring, negotiation, approval, obligations and renewal. Supplier Lifecycle and Performance onboards suppliers, keeps their data current and measures how well they deliver. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Value leaks after the award: missed volume discounts, expired terms, unmanaged suppliers and unrecorded performance. The contract and the supplier record are where promised savings are kept or lost.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A health care group discovered it was paying list price on products covered by negotiated contracts because buyers could not find the terms. A contract repository linked to purchasing recovered that leakage within a quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Contracts that are findable, compliant and enforceable; Faster, cleaner supplier onboarding; Visible supplier performance and obligations; Savings that survive after the award. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Health Care and Financial Services need strict supplier qualification and regulatory terms; Utilities manages long-term service and maintenance contracts.
