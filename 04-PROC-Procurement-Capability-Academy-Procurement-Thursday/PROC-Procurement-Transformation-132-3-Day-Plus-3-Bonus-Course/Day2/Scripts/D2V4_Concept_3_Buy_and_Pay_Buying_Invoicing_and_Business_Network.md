**1. What is the concept?**
Buying and Invoicing gives employees a guided, compliant way to requisition, order, receive and pay, with catalogues and approvals. SAP Business Network connects buyers and suppliers digitally for orders, confirmations, invoices and payments. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Maverick spend and manual paperwork bypass contracts and slow suppliers. If compliant buying is harder than going around the process, people go around it.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer discretionary company moved its suppliers to a network for orders and invoices. Invoice errors fell, early-payment discounts became possible, and buyers stopped chasing paper. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: High compliance with contracts and catalogues; Faster purchase-to-pay with fewer errors; Suppliers connected and paid on time; Better spend data for every other process. [on screen: four benefits]
