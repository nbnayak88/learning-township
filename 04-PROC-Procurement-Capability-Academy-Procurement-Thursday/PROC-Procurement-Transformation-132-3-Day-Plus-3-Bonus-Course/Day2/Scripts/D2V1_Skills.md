# Day 2 V1: Skills
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Ten skills. That is all that stands between you and architect-level procurement work.

Welcome to Day 2, and congratulations on completing Day 1! [pause]

Your goal from the Goal Card needs skills. Here are the top ten. [show slide]

1. **Spend and category analysis**: See spend by category, supplier and risk.
2. **Strategic sourcing and negotiation**: Run events that win value and terms.
3. **Fit-to-standard and process mapping**: Test requirements against standard and isolate gaps.
4. **Procurement business case and value storytelling**: Turn procurement change into CFO-ready value.
5. **Stakeholder and change leadership**: Align procurement, finance, legal, business and suppliers.
6. **Contract and supplier lifecycle literacy**: Know where value is kept or lost.
7. **Procurement analytics and KPI design**: Choose few metrics that drive decisions.
8. **Integration and data-flow design**: Design interfaces and master-data ownership.
9. **AI prompting and AI governance for Procurement**: Use AI well and safely on commercial data.
10. **Sector translation across 11 GICS sectors**: Adapt one architecture to any industry.

Rate yourself one to five on each. Circle your lowest two; we will build them through the six concepts.
