### Day 2 V2: Concept 1: Strategize and Source: Categories and Sourcing
*Duration: 12-15 min*

**Hook:** You cannot save on spend you have not organised.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Strategize and Source: Categories and Sourcing**. It anchors two product academies: ARI6 Ariba Category Management and APR1 Ariba Sourcing. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example ARI6-EN, ARI6-MA, ARI6-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Category management groups spend into manageable categories and sets a strategy for each: what to buy, from whom, at what risk and value. Sourcing then runs the events (RFx, auctions, negotiations) that turn the strategy into awarded business. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Buying by habit or by department leaves money on the table and risk unmanaged. Without a category strategy, sourcing becomes a price chase; without sourcing discipline, a good strategy never reaches the contract.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A materials company bought packaging through nine business units with nine supplier lists. One category strategy and one sourcing event consolidated the volume, improved terms and cut the supplier base in half, with no loss of service. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Clear category strategies tied to business needs; Competitive, transparent sourcing events; Measurable savings and better supplier terms; A repeatable rhythm the whole function can follow. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Energy and Industrials run large, project-based sourcing events; Consumer Staples runs high-volume, repeat category cycles.

### Day 2 V3: Concept 2: Contract and Onboard: Contracts and Supplier Lifecycle
*Duration: 12-15 min*

**Hook:** A great deal on paper is worth nothing if nobody follows it.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Contract and Onboard: Contracts and Supplier Lifecycle**. It anchors two product academies: ASN3 Ariba Contracts and ASR2 Ariba Supplier Lifecycle and Performance. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example ASN3-EN, ASN3-MA, ASN3-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Contracts manages the full contract lifecycle: authoring, negotiation, approval, obligations and renewal. Supplier Lifecycle and Performance onboards suppliers, keeps their data current and measures how well they deliver. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Value leaks after the award: missed volume discounts, expired terms, unmanaged suppliers and unrecorded performance. The contract and the supplier record are where promised savings are kept or lost.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A health care group discovered it was paying list price on products covered by negotiated contracts because buyers could not find the terms. A contract repository linked to purchasing recovered that leakage within a quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Contracts that are findable, compliant and enforceable; Faster, cleaner supplier onboarding; Visible supplier performance and obligations; Savings that survive after the award. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Health Care and Financial Services need strict supplier qualification and regulatory terms; Utilities manages long-term service and maintenance contracts.

### Day 2 V4: Concept 3: Buy and Pay: Buying, Invoicing and Business Network
*Duration: 12-15 min*

**Hook:** The easiest way to buy is the way people will actually follow.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Buy and Pay: Buying, Invoicing and Business Network**. It anchors two product academies: APO5 Ariba Buying and Invoicing and ASU4 SAP Business Network. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example APO5-EN, APO5-MA, APO5-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Buying and Invoicing gives employees a guided, compliant way to requisition, order, receive and pay, with catalogues and approvals. SAP Business Network connects buyers and suppliers digitally for orders, confirmations, invoices and payments. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Maverick spend and manual paperwork bypass contracts and slow suppliers. If compliant buying is harder than going around the process, people go around it.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer discretionary company moved its suppliers to a network for orders and invoices. Invoice errors fell, early-payment discounts became possible, and buyers stopped chasing paper. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: High compliance with contracts and catalogues; Faster purchase-to-pay with fewer errors; Suppliers connected and paid on time; Better spend data for every other process. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Real Estate and Communication Services buy many services and indirect items; Industrials has high direct-material volume.
