## Concept check (short answer)
- What does category management do?  
  *Answer: Groups spend into categories and sets a strategy for each*
- Which series anchor Concept 1?  
  *Answer: ARI6 Ariba Category Management and APR1 Ariba Sourcing*
- Why does sourcing need a category strategy?  
  *Answer: Without it sourcing becomes a price chase*
- What does Contracts manage?  
  *Answer: Authoring, negotiation, approval, obligations and renewal*
- Which series cover Concept 2?  
  *Answer: ASN3 Ariba Contracts and ASR2 Supplier Lifecycle and Performance*
- Where does value leak after award?  
  *Answer: Missed terms, expired contracts and unmanaged suppliers*
- What is maverick spend?  
  *Answer: Buying that bypasses contracts and the compliant process*
- Which series cover Concept 3?  
  *Answer: APO5 Ariba Buying and Invoicing and ASU4 SAP Business Network*
- What does Business Network connect?  
  *Answer: Buyers and suppliers for orders, confirmations, invoices and payments*
