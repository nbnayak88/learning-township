BEST PRACTICE

Design principles

  • EC as authoritative employee source
  • Listen at meaningful moments
  • Apply role-based access and confidentiality thresholds
  • Keep surveys short and localized
  • Assign action owners and close the feedback loop
  • Monitor integration and data retention
