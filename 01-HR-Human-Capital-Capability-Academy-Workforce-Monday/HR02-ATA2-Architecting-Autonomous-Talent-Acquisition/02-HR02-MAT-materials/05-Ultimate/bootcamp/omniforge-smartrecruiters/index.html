Are we assessing the work, or matching words?

Arjun Mehta (OMF-P05), Group CHRO, receives an urgent request to recruit a technician for recovered-feedstock operations at a cement plant. Elena Petrova (OMF-P07), Cement Plant Director, needs the person before the next operating cycle. Nikhil Sen (OMF-P16), Shift Supervisor and Workforce Representative, wants to know what the newcomer can do on arrival. Synthetic candidate MAT-C201 has relevant maintenance experience but lacks the exact job-title keywords in the screening template, and an automated recommendation ranks the application poorly.

Choose first: Send 'You are ready to start plant operations', delay every activity, or communicate the confirmed hiring status while naming the outstanding readiness steps?

Evidence reveal: An authorised reviewer examines a practical assessment against approved role criteria and the candidate progresses. The receiving team has not acknowledged the handoff and task-specific readiness is unconfirmed, so the message names only what is confirmed. The candidate has no personal smartphone, so onboarding cannot depend on one.

Counterexample: A dashboard showing 'initiated' proves a task started, not that the person can operate plant equipment safely.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionApproved in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionJob with approved criteria
  3. Assess and selectPractical assessment, reviewer decision
  4. OfferApproved and accepted
  5. Hire handoffCurrent start date reconciled
  6. OnboardingAccessible, device-independent route
  7. Plant readinessTask-specific confirmation by the receiving owner

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Requisition and selectionGroup CHRO and authorised reviewerApproved criteria; automated rank is an input only

Plant readinessCement Plant Director and shift supervisorTask-specific readiness confirmed by the receiving owner
Worker voiceWorkforce representativeAccessible, human-assisted route that does not rely on a personal device

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
