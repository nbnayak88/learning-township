# SAP Activate — Recruiting & Onboarding Capstone Challenge

**SuccessLabs Academy · HR02 Architecting Autonomous Talent Acquisition · Energy · Ultimate**

A 21-day capstone for the fictional OmniVerse Energy scenario. Use the activity plan to design and validate a SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting rollout. The source phases Recruiting first and treats Onboarding as a later phase.
