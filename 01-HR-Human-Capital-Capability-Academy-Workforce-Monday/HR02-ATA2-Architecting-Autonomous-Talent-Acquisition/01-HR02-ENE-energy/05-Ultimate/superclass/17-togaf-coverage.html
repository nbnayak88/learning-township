In brief. Proof of completeness: every TOGAF introduction concept, term, glossary entry, acronym and reference is mapped to a stream and to a place in this template.

This chapter proves coverage. It lists every core concept (TOGAF chapter 3), every defined term (chapter 4) and the three appendices (A referenced documents, B supplementary definitions, C acronyms), says where it is covered and which artefact carries it, and places it in the twelve streams.

Numbering note. Numbers follow the list supplied for this template: 18 core-concept topics (3.1 to 3.18) and 89 terms (4.1 to 4.89), including the term EA Landscape. Sub-sections (for example 3.5.1) are listed as extra rows. Some editions of the standard number items differently or also have a separate Architecture Repository topic; match on the title if numbers differ.

17.9 How to keep it complete

When TOGAF is updated, edit tools/togaf_data.py and tools/togaf_appendices.py (or the CSVs in data/), rebuild, and the Document, Deck and Workbook update together. The build check fails if a term has no stream or no location.
