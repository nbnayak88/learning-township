SAP Activate Case Study: OmniVerse Energy (Phases E to H)

In brief. A 21-day foundation sprint delivers the pilot slice on SAP Activate. Partner categories follow in regional waves using the same cadence.

23.1 What this case study shows and does not show

The sprint covers the pilot slice: one country, one role family, core hiring from requisition to offer, single sign-on, the HR core hire hand-off, job boards and e-signature. It does not claim that a 14-country rollout with all 28 partner categories completes in 21 days. Waves 2 to 4 add partners and regions using the same phases and gates.

23.3 Fit-to-standard

Standard hub capability is used first. Each gap is resolved in this order: configuration, marketplace connector, integration-hub adapter, extension. Anything outside standard needs a decision record.

23.6 Waves after the sprint

Wave 2 adds assessment, interviewing, checks, clearance and contract partners. Wave 3 adds analytics, planning and surveys. Wave 4 adds mobility, DEI, engagement and offboarding. Each wave reuses the patterns in Chapter 20 and the scorecard in Chapter 21.

23.7 Run

Release management, partner reviews, annual exit tests, adoption measures and the AI and privacy review cycle form the steady-state operating rhythm (Chapter 16).
