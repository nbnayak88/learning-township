In brief. Business, stakeholder, functional, non-functional and transition requirements, each testable and traced to a component.

8.1 How to read the register

IDs: BR business, SR stakeholder, FR functional, NF non-functional, TR transition. Priority uses MoSCoW. Status is Draft until baselined.
