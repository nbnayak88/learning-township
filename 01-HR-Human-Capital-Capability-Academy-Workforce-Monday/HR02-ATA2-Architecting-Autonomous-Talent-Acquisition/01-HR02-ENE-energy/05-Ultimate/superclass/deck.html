Architecture vision (TOGAF Preliminary and Phase A)

The problem and the vision

The problem

  • Three legacy hiring tools and spreadsheets across 14 countries
  • Different process and candidate experience in each country
  • Assessment, interview and check tools bought locally
  • Slow checks for safety-critical roles
  • AI use and candidate data not governed
  • No single view of the funnel
Vision

Hire for safety and skill, fast and fairly, on one platform that welcomes the best partners today and lets OmniVerse replace any of them tomorrow.

Business objectives

Seven objectives, each measurable

One platformOne process and one hiring data model
Faster hiringLess admin, shorter time to hire
Safe hiringChecks and clearance before day one
Vendor freedom28 categories behind capability interfaces
Fair AIAdvisory only, logged and tested
VisibilityFunnel, SLAs, KPIs in one place
GovernanceExit plans, audit trail, decision records
OutcomeFaster, safer, fair, replaceable

Architecture principles

Thirteen principles guide every decision

Business

  • One requisition, one candidate record
  • Process before tool
  • Candidate experience is a design input
  • Humans decide

Data

  • Candidate data is held in trust
  • Canonical hiring model
  • Summary, not raw sensitive detail
  • Every entity has an owner and steward

Application

  • Hub and spokes
  • Buy, configure, then extend
  • Capability interface, replaceable partner
  • Event-driven by default

Technology

  • Open standards
  • Zero trust for partners
  • Residency and sovereignty by design
  • Observable and exit-ready

Data governance

Ownership, stewardship, classification

Roles

  • Data owner: accountable per domain
  • Data steward: quality and definitions
  • AI and privacy board: monthly

Classification

  • Internal
  • Confidential: requisition, offer, candidate
  • Restricted: identity, checks, medical clearance

Lifecycle

  • Collect with consent and purpose
  • Retain by region table, then delete
  • Check detail stays with the provider
Technology architecture (TOGAF Phase D)

Security, resilience, hosting

Security

  • Enterprise SSO and SCIM for staff
  • Scoped OAuth 2.0 credentials per partner, in a vault
  • Signed, replay-protected webhooks
  • Immutable audit log
  • Contract tests per partner release

Resilience and hosting

  • Availability [99.5]%; recovery targets per component
  • Retry with back-off, dead-letter queue, daily reconciliation
  • Vendor cloud in the region set by the residency table
  • Exit plan tested yearly per partner category
