In brief. Forty-three building blocks in eleven layers. Each partner category is reached through a capability interface and an adapter, so any vendor can be replaced.

12.1 Architecture at a glance

12.2 Vendor-agnostic method

Each component has an ABB (what it must do) and SBB options (examples only). A component lists responsibilities, not product features. Choosing an SBB is a selection decision recorded in the decision register.

12.5 Application to capability

Every component supports at least one capability in Chapter 10 and realises at least one requirement in Chapter 8.

12.6 Hub choice and roadmap note

SmartRecruiters is the hiring hub because it connects to many partners and works with SuccessFactors and other HCM systems. SAP has stated an intent to replace the SuccessFactors Recruiting module over time; the architecture keeps the hub behind the capability interfaces so this direction can be followed without redesign.
