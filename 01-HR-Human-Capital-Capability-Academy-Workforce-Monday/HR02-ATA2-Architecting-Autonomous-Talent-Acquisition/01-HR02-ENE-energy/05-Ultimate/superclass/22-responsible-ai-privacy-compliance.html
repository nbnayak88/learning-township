Responsible AI, Privacy and Compliance (Requirements Management)

ADM phase All phases (Requirements Management)Lead streams AI, Security, DataSupporting Business, Domain, Industry9P4V V3 VictoryStandards TOGAF, DMBOK

In brief. AI advises and humans decide. Consent, minimum data, regional storage and tested retention apply to every partner.

22.1 AI use in hiring

AI appears in chat assistants, matching, screening support, interview summaries and partner tools. Several jurisdictions treat hiring AI as high risk. The design therefore keeps AI advisory, logs it, tests it and allows regional switches.

22.3 Candidate rights

Notice of AI use, a route to a human, explanation on request, access, correction and deletion, and accessible alternatives for assessments and video interviews.

22.5 Check results

Outcome and date only are kept in the hub. Detail stays with the provider. Adverse-action steps follow local law and always involve a human.

22.6 Partner privacy duties

Data-processing agreement, sub-processor list, region of processing, deletion on request and on exit, breach notice period, audit right.

22.7 Evidence

Consent log, AI register, bias test reports, retention job logs and exit test results are produced from the registers and the audit log for auditors.
