Speed without losing evidence

Arjun Malhotra (OVE-HR02a-P01), Global Talent Acquisition Product Owner, is pushed by Sofia Bennett (OVE-HR02a-P02), Renewables Growth President, to shorten selection for 200 project and engineering hires before construction begins. Kwesi Boateng (OVE-HR02a-P03), Offshore Operations Director, will not accept an uploaded certificate as proof of validated offshore credentials. Elena Markovic (OVE-HR02a-P04), Responsible AI and Privacy Counsel, asks which approved criteria an assistant used when it ranked grid engineer Noor Rahman (OVE-HR02a-P05) low.

Choose first: Remove waiting only, remove evidence gates too, or separate submission, validation and later site authorisation?

Evidence reveal: Administrative waiting is reduced by removing duplicate data entry and coordinating interviews. The offshore credential stays blocked until its owner validates it. Noor's application is reviewed against approved criteria, and a handoff the next system has not acknowledged is not reported as complete.

Counterexample: A faster time-to-fill can improve while credential evidence and fairness review are quietly dropped.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionApproved in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionR-ENE-001 / R-ENE-002 job
  3. Assess and selectScorecard and panel decision
  4. OfferApproved, sent, accepted
  5. Hire handoffPerson and employment matched
  6. OnboardingTasks and HR readiness
  7. Validation and authorisationCredential validation, then site authorisation

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Position and requisitionTalent acquisition ownerPosition-led requisition; criteria approved before applications open
Candidate evidence and selectionAccountable hiring panelPanel decides; an assistant may only rank as an input
Credential validationOffshore operations owner and validating authorityUploaded certificate is submission evidence; validation is a separate step
Employment factsHR steward (Employee Central)Core HR owns the employment record
Site authorisationSafety ownerNever inferred from the offer or from onboarding tasks

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.

Measure

Credential-validated hires divided by applicable candidates at the defined gate, reported with the time-to-fill definition (accepted-offer date minus approved-requisition date). Planned, unmeasured.
