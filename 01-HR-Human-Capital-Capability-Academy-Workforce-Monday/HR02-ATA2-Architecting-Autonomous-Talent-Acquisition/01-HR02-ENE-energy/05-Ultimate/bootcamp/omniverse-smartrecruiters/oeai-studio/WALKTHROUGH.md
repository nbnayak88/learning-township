# OEAI Studio — source inspection and adaptation

Inspected 26 November 2026. Source: https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/joule-studio/product-tour.html.

The source is a screenshot walkthrough, not a live tenant. Start, forward navigation, reverse chapter boundary, direct selection, Enlarge, completion and restart were exercised. Images sometimes loaded after their text; loading captures were rejected. Direct selection retained previous local position. Restart returned to the first chapter's previously viewed position in this session. OEAI deliberately restarts at position 1.

## OmniVerse grounding

Authoritative source: the bootcamp delivery pack and AI skill/prompt libraries linked in the application. AIU-OVE-01 is L1 Assist; accountable human OVE-R01. Permission: draft text for review. Prohibitions: publish, change position, set budget. Source skill and PRM-OVE-181/182 are included verbatim as owner-provided curriculum content. Source requirement references span multiple role families; do not claim all were originally written for the sample well-engineering position.

## Reuse blueprint

Keep tour data, views and navigation separate. Add the next agent only after its own scope, owner, inputs, allowed actions, prohibited actions and evidence are agreed. Do not activate all twelve agents by default.
