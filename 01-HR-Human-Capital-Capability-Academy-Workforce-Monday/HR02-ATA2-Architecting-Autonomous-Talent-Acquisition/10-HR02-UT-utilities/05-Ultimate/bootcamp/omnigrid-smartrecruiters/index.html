Urgency must not conceal missing prerequisites

Nadia Fernandes (OGU-P07), Group CHRO, works with Marcus Okoro (OGU-P08), Outage and Field Services Director, who requests a distribution technician for a modernisation crew. Nadia receives an impressive AI candidate summary and an urgent request to mark the vacancy filled, but the requisition has no verified position link and the accepted-offer checklist says nothing about local induction, PPE or technical authorisation.

Choose first: Accept the AI ranking, wait for every onboarding task before making an offer, or separate position approval, human selection and readiness gates?

Evidence reveal: The position is confirmed after an owner review and a structured, job-relevant assessment supports a human hiring decision. The candidate accepts, but the evidence packet shows induction booked and not completed. Nadia records offer acceptance and onboarding progress while Marcus keeps restricted work pending the delegated local decision.

Counterexample: A fast time-to-fill can improve while the recruit still lacks the prerequisites for field work.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionVerified link in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionSupported by owner review
  3. Assess and selectHuman scorecard
  4. OfferAccepted offer
  5. Hire handoffCandidate and employment correlated
  6. OnboardingNamed task owners
  7. Field authorisationDelegated local decision

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Position and demandPosition ownerUnsupported requisitions held or sent for a controlled exception
SelectionAccountable panelHuman scorecard; unexplained ranking is not sole evidence
Employment factsHR steward (Employee Central)Hiring and readiness are separate states
Field readinessOutage and field services director (delegated local decision)Induction, PPE and technical authorisation

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
