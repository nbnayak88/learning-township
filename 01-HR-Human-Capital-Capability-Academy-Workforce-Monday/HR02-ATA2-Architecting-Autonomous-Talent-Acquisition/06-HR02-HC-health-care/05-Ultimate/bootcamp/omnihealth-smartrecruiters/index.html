The offer is accepted; who owns day-one readiness?

Nina Fernandes (OHS-P06), Group CHRO, sees an accepted offer for a synthetic laboratory professional. Dr. Mei Lin (OHS-P11), Diagnostics and Imaging Director, wants a reliable start for the diagnostic service. The recruiter marks hiring complete, while onboarding lists credential review, learning and facility access under different owners, and the candidate receives a welcome that sounds like unconditional readiness.

Choose first: Treat acceptance as readiness, postpone every joining activity, or separate selection, employment entry and role-readiness gates?

Evidence reveal: The candidate completed employment documents, but the designated professional reviewer has not confirmed the scope and validity of the credential, and a name match alone cannot link the candidate to an existing worker record. Nina keeps the accepted-offer state and starts authorised administrative onboarding; the joining notice says which activities are permitted and who resolves the hold.

Counterexample: An urgent request does not manufacture approval; missing evidence remains unknown.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionApproved in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionRole with credential requirements
  3. Assess and selectPanel decision
  4. OfferAccepted offer
  5. Hire handoffRequest key, receiver status
  6. Administrative onboardingAuthorised tasks only
  7. Credential and role readinessDesignated reviewer decision

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
SelectionGroup CHRO and accountable panelHuman selection decision
Identity linkageHR identity stewardNo automatic merge; approved minimum evidence
Credential and role readinessDesignated professional reviewerScope and validity confirmed by the reviewer, not by the recruiter
Employment requestHR steward (Employee Central)Request key and receiver status reconciled before retry

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
