An accepted offer does not open a client environment

Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07), Group CHRO, wants a cloud engineer ready for a client project. Sofia Tan (OTS-P06), Platform Engineering Director, favours the highest automated match score. Omar Haddad (OTS-P10), CISO and Supply-Chain Security Lead, asks whether the assessment used permitted evidence and whether onboarding readiness includes privileged-access approval.

Choose first: Hire directly from the score, restart all assessments, or verify the approved position and evidence before a controlled handoff?

Evidence reveal: Synthetic candidate IT-C200 has accepted an offer, but the assessment contains an unverified skill inference and the hiring request lacks position approval. Nadia holds employment creation pending authorised resolution and invites the candidate to correct the evidence. Even after approval, a completed onboarding checklist will not authorise client privilege.

Counterexample: An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorisation.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionApproval confirmed
  2. RequisitionJob against approved position
  3. Assess and selectPermitted evidence, human review
  4. OfferAccepted offer
  5. Hire handoffHeld until position approved
  6. OnboardingTasks complete
  7. Client privilegeIndependent access approval

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Position approvalAccountable role in the requisition chainHold hire creation until resolved or rejected without hiding offer status
Evidence and selectionHiring panelPermitted evidence only; AI score is not final
Employment factsHR steward (Employee Central)Core HR owns the employment record
Client privilegeCISO and access ownerIndependent approval; distinct from task completion and employment start

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
