## Origins: the question that survives each era

These closed windows group process evolution for teaching; they are not universal invention or adoption dates. Adoption overlaps. Paper, spreadsheets and older systems can coexist through the present.
1970–1984 — Personnel teams use vacancy notices and paper applications
1985–1994 — Databases track applicants separately from employees
1995–2004 — Applicant tracking formalizes requisitions and interview stages
2005–2014 — Online recruiting connects offers and onboarding tasks
2015–2025 — Skills matching and digital preboarding add explainability and privacy questions
An accepted offer and a completed welcome task do not prove role readiness or privileged-access authorization.

## One story: choose, reveal, revise

Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07), Group CHRO, wants a cloud engineer ready for a client project. Sofia Tan (OTS-P06), Platform Engineering Director, favors the highest automated match score. Omar Haddad (OTS-P10), CISO and Supply-Chain Security Lead, asks whether the assessment used permitted evidence and whether onboarding readiness includes privileged-access approval.
Choose before reading on: Hire directly from the score, restart all assessments, or verify the approved position and evidence before a controlled handoff?
Evidence reveal: Synthetic candidate IT-C200 has accepted an offer, but the assessment contains an unverified skill inference and the hiring request lacks position approval. Nadia holds employment creation pending authorized resolution, invites the candidate to correct evidence, and defines a handoff from Recruiting to Core HR and Onboarding. Even after the position is approved, a completed onboarding checklist will not authorize client privilege.
Write what changed your decision and what remains unknown. An accessible explanation and a human challenge route must be available to the affected worker. An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorization.

## Application responsibility and human authority

Recruiting — Talent acquisition owns requisition and candidate assessment evidence. Hiring panel decides selection using verified criteria.
Core HR — HR owns employment and approved position records. Delegated HR authority approves hire creation.
Onboarding / learning — Onboarding owner coordinates tasks; learning owner records readiness evidence. Manager and assessors confirm each readiness item.
IAM / client access — Security owns approved entitlements. Client/delegated access authority grants privilege.
Business owners remain accountable; applications store and process the records. Discover the actual delegated approvers before implementation. Restrict client, candidate, pay and health information to its purpose and permitted viewers. Minimize replicated data and unnecessary compute rather than assuming automation is sustainable.
SAP examples in the preserved source are discussion options, not a technology selection or a claim that configuration has been validated. Start with the business need. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder; it cannot approve a consequential people decision, grant privilege or declare a payment complete.

## One build experiment: Decision Build Card

Synthetic inputs: Candidate IT-C200; requisition IT-R200; proposed position CLOUD-200; offer accepted; position approval missing; candidate evidence contested; onboarding tasks not started.
Complete these fields once:
1. People and business need: who benefits, what could go wrong and why this matters in IT services or engineering.
2. Initial choice and reason: compare the choices before seeing the reveal.
3. Evidence reveal and revised choice: state what changed, and one counterexample.
4. Identity, date and source owner: identify authoritative records, effective time, provenance and uncertain facts.
5. Application responsibility: separate record keeping, orchestration, calculation, analytics and access enforcement.
6. Human approval and protected action: name the decision role, limits and worker challenge route.
7. Minimum data and permitted viewers: state purpose, retention question, client isolation and accessible communication.
8. Alternative and local variant: defend a common core with a justified regional/client variant, or explain a different design.
9. Exception owner and recovery: define hold, escalation, retry/reconciliation and confirmation conditions.
10. Measure and missing evidence: Handoffs with approved position, verified selection and explicit readiness status / all proposed handoffs.
11. Pre-Foundation question: Define requisition, candidate, offer, employment and onboarding readiness; map the Recruiting-to-Onboarding data handoff.
For each test below record input → expected → observed paper result → evidence → revision. Blank observations remain blank. A complete card can still contain a poor decision.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

OTS-HR02-T01 — Position not approved
Expected proposed behavior: Hold hire creation; resolve approval or reject through the accountable role without hiding offer status.
Evidence to record: Record requisition, position status and candidate communication.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR02-T02 — Candidate challenges inferred skill
Expected proposed behavior: Recheck against job criteria using permitted evidence and an accessible human review; do not treat AI score as final.
Evidence to record: Record evidence correction and panel rationale.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR02-T03 — Checklist done, privilege absent
Expected proposed behavior: Keep client privilege held; distinguish task completion, employment start and access readiness.
Evidence to record: Record per-domain status and access-owner response.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.
Acceptance: one complete card, three recorded tabletop observations after any revisions, and an explanation of source ownership and human authority. A breached authority or privacy boundary requires redesign. Publishing these tests does not execute them.
