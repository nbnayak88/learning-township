Checks run

  • Workbook present with the 15 standard sheets and identical headers in all 12 use cases
  • Counts equal the standard in every sheet
  • IDs unique for personas, requirements, stories, tests and deliverables
  • Every requirement has exactly one story, at least one test, a traceability row and a build deliverable
  • Persona groups are exactly 16, 12 and 6
  • Every persona has at least 10 requirements
  • Every AI use case links to existing requirements and tests
  • No unfilled placeholders in any sheet or page
  • Requirements CSV equals the workbook sheet
  • Page and workbook counts agree
  • HTML is well formed, element ids unique, internal anchors, downloads and hub links resolve
  • LIME 12 links use ids that exist in the dashboard
  • Logo embedded; no Qualtrics, Notion or sister-case wording
  • Section order is the same on all 12 pages from requirements onward
  • Workbooks open and recalculate in LibreOffice
  • Pages render in headless Chrome with no script errors and a loaded logo

What this audit does not establish

  • Nothing is tenant-tested. Fit labels, integration behaviour and every deliverable status are proposals; test status is Not run and deliverable status is Not started.
  • Requirements, stories and tests are generated from persona cards, stated objections and sector facts through templates. They are a baseline for validation workshops and have had no subject-matter expert review.
  • Benefit clauses and acceptance criteria are standard for each of the ten stages, filled with the requirement's own sector facts. They are not individually written per requirement.
  • Recruiting roles and AI-driven roles are synthetic. OmniEsports has no persona-card file, so all 16 of its case personas are synthetic; one Industrials persona is a synthetic addition.
  • Effort and duration are not estimated beyond the 90-day Wave 0; later waves are to be baselined in Explore.
  • The live GitHub Pages site could not be reached from the build environment. Pages were checked from files and in headless Chrome, not on github.io.
  • Workbook formulas were recalculated in LibreOffice, not in desktop Excel.
  • Page narrative sections remain case-specific by design: OmniEsports centres on three recruitment surveys and five paper tests, the others on a story and three paper tests.
