The hiring decision

Arjun Rao, Global Talent Acquisition Product Owner, receives a service-growth hiring demand where speed matters to customer uptime. A screening pilot shows unequal progression and incomplete explanations. After synthetic candidate SYN-C201 accepts an offer, Priya Nair, Global Workforce Readiness Lead, sees a green onboarding dashboard because documents are complete, while Rafael Costa, Safety and Compliance Director, asks for current task prerequisites.

Choose first: Centralised recruiting with local readiness ownership, site-owned hiring, or a shared journey team: which control and candidate experience would you trade for speed, and which must remain?

Evidence reveal: The contested automated ranking is paused and a structured human selection route is kept. SYN-C201 has an induction completion but no practical assessment and an unconfirmed badge. Approved selection, accepted offer, established employment and verified work readiness become distinct states, and the candidate gets one clear status and a human contact.

Counterexample: A quicker funnel is not evidence of fairer selection, and a green dashboard does not prove a badge exists.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.
