Case story

The hiring decision

  • Arjun Rao, Global Talent Acquisition Product Owner, receives a service-growth hiring demand where speed matters to customer uptime. A screening pilot shows unequal progression and incomplete explanations.
  • Choose first: Centralised recruiting with local readiness ownership, site-owned hiring, or a shared journey team: which control and candidate experience would you trade for speed, and which must remain?
  • Evidence reveal: The contested automated ranking is paused and a structured human selection route is kept. SYN-C201 has an induction completion but no practical assessment and an unconfirmed badge.
  • Counterexample: A quicker funnel is not evidence of fairer selection, and a green dashboard does not prove a badge exists.

AI

AI guardrails

  • No agent decides on hiring, pay, employment records or access.
  • A named human is accountable for every outcome.
  • Adverse-impact testing before release and after each change.
  • Owner can pause any agent; every action is logged.
  • Data limited to the registered purpose; no sensitive attributes.
