Hiring speed cannot turn an unresolved check into clearance

Marcus Okoro (OCF-P08), Financial Crime Operations Director, needs a new investigator before an alert-review peak. Nadia Fernandes (OCF-P07), Group CHRO, sees an accepted offer for synthetic candidate FIN-C200 against funded position FIN-POS200. Elena Fischer (OCF-P05), Chief Compliance and Conduct Officer, finds an incomplete role check, and the manager wants the account enabled today.

Choose first: Treat offer acceptance as ready, delay all onboarding, or progress permitted tasks while holding restricted duties?

Evidence reveal: Identity linkage is still ambiguous and the required role check is not closed. Nadia starts only non-sensitive welcome tasks, Elena requests the appropriate reviewer and Marcus plans supervised non-restricted learning. Recruiting selection, employment creation, onboarding task progress and role authorisation stay separate states.

Counterexample: Customer KYC and employee readiness are different processes; customer identity checks are not copied into HR without purpose and policy review.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionFIN-POS200 in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionJob against funded position
  3. Assess and selectRole-relevant human assessment
  4. OfferAccepted offer
  5. Hire handoffIdentity linkage resolved by steward
  6. OnboardingWelcome tasks
  7. Role clearanceCompliance reviewer approval

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Requisition and selectionGroup CHRO and accountable panelFunded position; fair, role-relevant human assessment
Identity linkageHR stewardNo automatic merge of worker records
Role clearanceCompliance and conduct reviewerSensitive duties and access held until the designated reviewer approves
Supervised learningFinancial crime operations directorNon-restricted learning only

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
