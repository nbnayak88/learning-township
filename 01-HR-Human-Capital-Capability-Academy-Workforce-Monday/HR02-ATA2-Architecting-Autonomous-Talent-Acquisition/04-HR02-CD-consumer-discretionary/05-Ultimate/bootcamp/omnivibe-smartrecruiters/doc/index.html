The hiring decision

Sofia Mendes (OVC-P08), Head of Talent Acquisition and Onboarding, faces high-volume store hiring before a fictional seasonal peak. An accepted candidate looks green in the recruiting tracker, yet Oliver Brooks (OVC-P06), Store Operations Director, cannot confirm access, induction or a supervised first shift. Nikhil Das (OVC-P16), Store Associate and Workforce Representative, asks who will help the new colleague while serving customers.

Choose first: Recruiting speed alone, a fully centralised onboarding checklist, or a shared handoff with role- and market-specific evidence?

Evidence reveal: A digital-business candidate has been placed on a store template, and the generic checklist conceals missing service practice. The receiving operator's employer authority is unknown. Sofia revises the release gate and keeps a human selection decision; neither an automated ranking nor an accepted offer authorises work.

Counterexample: Faster hiring alone does not solve day-one readiness.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; the receiving owner carries readiness and access. A fact answers one question only.
