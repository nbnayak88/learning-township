An accepted offer before access

Group CHRO Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07) wants a new network engineer ready for a coverage expansion. Service Assurance and NOC Director Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08) prefers the candidate who can start immediately. A structured assessment is incomplete and onboarding already shows a green checklist.

Choose first: hire for speed, reject automatically, or complete a structured human assessment and keep access readiness separate?

Evidence reveal: the candidate meets the job criteria after an accessible practical assessment. The accepted offer is valid, but onboarding has no privileged-access approval, so access stays held while the manager arranges supervised induction.

Counterexample: an accepted offer cannot prove identity, equipment readiness or privileged-task authorization.

Hire-to-readiness architecture

SmartRecruiters carries recruiting decisions; Employee Central and Onboarding carry employment facts; IAM carries access. A fact answers one question only.

  1. PositionPOS-NET200 in Employee Central
  2. RequisitionREQ-COM200 job in SmartRecruiters
  3. Assess and selectScorecard, accessible assessment, panel decision
  4. OfferOFF200 accepted
  5. Hire handoffHIRNEW; person and employment matched
  6. OnboardingONB200 tasks and HR readiness
  7. AccessIAM and NOC approval, supervised work

Who owns each decision

Application responsibilityAccountable ownerData and human boundary
Position and requisitionTalent acquisition ownerPosition-led requisition; scorecard set before applications
Candidate evidence and selectionAccountable hiring panelPanel decides; automation may only assist low-risk administration
Employment factsOnboarding owner and HR steward (Employee Central)Core HR owns the employment record
Privileged network accessSecurity owner and NOC authorityIndependent approval and supervised work; never inferred from the offer

A source-system record, a receiving-system acknowledgment and a human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route for workers and candidates.
