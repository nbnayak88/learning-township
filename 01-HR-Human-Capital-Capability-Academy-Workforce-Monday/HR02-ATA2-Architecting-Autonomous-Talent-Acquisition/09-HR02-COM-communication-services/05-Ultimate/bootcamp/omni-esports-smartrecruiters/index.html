Hire-to-record architecture

SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors is the recruiting system; Employee Central remains the employment record.

  1. Approved positionEmployee Central Position Management
  2. JobPosition-to-Job creates the SmartRecruiters job
  3. CandidateApplications, interviews, S05 trigger point
  4. OfferOffer accepted, S06 trigger point
  5. HireHire event with HIRNEW reason
  6. Employee recordEmployee Central and Onboarding handoff
  7. ListeningS07 hiring manager; 30-day check-in from the HR10 design

Principles

  • One authoritative system per fact: position and employment in Employee Central, candidate and offer in SmartRecruiters.
  • Survey answers never decide a hire, and demographic items stay behind a privacy approval.
  • Triggers use stage events, not manual lists, so a candidate is not surveyed twice.
  • Where SmartRecruiters native feedback does not fit an item, the choice of an external survey tool is recorded per survey.
