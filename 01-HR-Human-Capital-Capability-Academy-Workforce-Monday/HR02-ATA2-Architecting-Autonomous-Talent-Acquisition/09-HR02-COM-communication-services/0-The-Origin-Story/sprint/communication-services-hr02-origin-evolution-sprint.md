## The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one hiring handoff that carries job-relevant evidence into a safe first day.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, wants a new network engineer ready for a coverage expansion. Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08), Service Assurance and NOC Director, prefers the candidate who can start immediately. A structured assessment is incomplete and onboarding already shows a green checklist.

**Choose first:** Hire for speed, reject automatically, or complete a structured human assessment and keep access readiness separate?

**Evidence reveal:** The candidate meets the job criteria after an accessible practical assessment. The accepted offer is valid, but onboarding has no privileged-access approval. Nadia confirms selection and preserves the access hold while the manager arranges supervised induction.

**Counterexample:** An accepted offer cannot prove identity, equipment readiness or privileged-task authorization.

OmniConnect is a fictional communication-services enterprise. The source stipulates 62,000 employees across 28 countries, spanning mobile and 5G, fiber, media and streaming, enterprise networks, and advertising. No new site or workforce counts are inferred. ## Origins and responsibility

These closed historical windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniConnect corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present.

| Teaching window | Process evolution |
|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Local applications and personnel induction |
| 1985–1994 | Applicant databases and standardized forms |
| 1995–2004 | Recruiting workflows connect approved positions to offers |
| 2005–2014 | Digital onboarding coordinates cross-functional tasks |
| 2015–2025 | Skills matching assists selection and flags readiness gaps |

| Application responsibility | Accountable owner | Data and human boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiting | Talent acquisition owner | Position-led requisition, candidate evidence, offer and selection decision by accountable panel |
| Onboarding and core HR | Onboarding owner and HR steward | Tasks and accepted-hire handoff; core HR owns employment facts |
| IAM and operations | Security owner and NOC authority | Independent approval of privileges and supervised work |

Recruiting (HR02a) and Onboarding (HR02b) remain separate source cases supporting one HR02 sprint.

A source-system record, receiving-system acknowledgment and human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route. Avoid personal-data joins with subscriber or audience data unless a legitimate purpose, access boundary and minimum data are established.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test ID | Focus | Evidence reveal | Expected result in the proposed design |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCN-XL-HR02-T01 | Job evidence | Candidate lacks a preferred credential but passes the relevant practical assessment. | Panel reviews demonstrated job criteria and documents a fair decision; avoid automatic credential exclusion. |
| OCN-XL-HR02-T02 | Green checklist | All HR tasks complete but privileged approval absent. | Keep network access held; show readiness by domain and assign the security exception. |
| OCN-XL-HR02-T03 | Duplicate hire event | Accepted-hire payload arrives twice with the same candidate/offer key. | Match one person/employment handoff, prevent duplicate creation and reconcile receiving-system outcome. |

Record initial decision → expected result → observed paper result → evidence → revision. All observed-result fields start empty. Acceptance requires a reasoned card and all three reviewed results after revisions. A material authority or privacy bypass requires redesign. Publishing the exercise does not execute it or prove operational outcomes.

## AI driven curiosity and the next chapter

| Scenario window | Exploration |
|---|---|
| 2026–2030 — Assist | Draft structured interview evidence summaries |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | Coordinate onboarding tasks across owners |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | Adapt learning pathways after observed proficiency |

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. The time bands support curiosity about possible designs.

**Competing future:** fragmented rules, weak interoperability or low trust keep local expert judgment and simpler manual workflows more effective than connected AI. Compare that path with the scenario before choosing an experiment.

**Question that could disprove your hypothesis:** Would faster hiring increase early readiness exceptions or exclusion of equally capable candidates?

**Reflect:** What evidence changed your first choice? Who can challenge the decision? What would make the alternative better?
