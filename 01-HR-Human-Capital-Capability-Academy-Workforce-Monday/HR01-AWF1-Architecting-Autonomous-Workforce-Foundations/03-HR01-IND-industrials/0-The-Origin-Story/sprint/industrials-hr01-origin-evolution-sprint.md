## 3. Origins: five closed historical teaching windows

These windows group teaching ideas, not universal invention dates or OmniWorks corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present. Each generation changes the medium without automatically resolving ownership, evidence or authority.

1970–1984 — The need is to know who works where. Personnel ledgers and payroll lists record people, but names alone cannot resolve duplicate identities.

1985–1994 — Plant personnel files and structured position registers support assignments; different sites still define worker and job differently.

1995–2004 — HRIS/ERP transactions connect employment and pay. Correct calculation cannot repair an incorrect person/employment mapping.

2005–2014 — Self-service and integrated lifecycle workflows improve coordination; a completed HR transaction does not prove a badge or payroll update.

2015–2025 — Cloud cores and governed local extensions improve shared meaning; historical accuracy, plant authority and local obligations still require evidence.

Recall prompt: reconstruct the five responses as problem → process/application response → unfinished problem. Which old failure could survive in the newest system?

## 4. One persona-led dilemma

### Scene 1

Asha opens a workshop with two regional records bearing the same name. The next maintenance assignment needs a reliable person, employment and position—not a faster copy of both records. Mei asks which employment payroll should use.

### Scene 2

Asha chooses between one rigid global template, independent country models and a shared semantic core with justified local extensions. Reveal: a source-approved assignment begins tomorrow, while today's employment must remain visible. Replacing today's record would erase the explanation for today's pay.

### Scene 3

New synthetic reveal: the receiving plant's practical qualification evidence is unavailable. The HR change can be assessed under its own policy; it cannot manufacture work readiness. Mei refuses to treat 'active employee' as permission to perform restricted maintenance.

### Scene 4

Asha revises the card: preserve history, separate downstream acknowledgements and name who resolves the missing evidence. A local extension request is held for justification rather than accepted or deleted merely because it is local.

Decision pause: Which option best preserves a common meaning without flattening justified local requirements? Name one fact that would make you favor a different option.

## 5. Responsibilities, ownership and human authority

Workforce core (HR data owner): person, employment, job, position and assignment facts under entity-specific history rules. SAP Employee Central is an illustrative core; do not assume every entity is technically effective-dated.

Payroll (payroll control owner): calculates results from approved inputs and reconciles changes; it does not own learning evidence.

Learning (learning owner): assessed capability and qualification records with validity. Site safety (authorized operations/safety owner): task-specific work authorization.

IAM and physical-access services (security owners): enforce approved digital/site access; employment status and course completion do not independently grant all access.

MES/EAM (operations/maintenance owners): production/work orders and asset work context. HR does not issue machine-control commands.

Design boundary: Asha recommends the model; authorized HR approvers accept the employment transaction. Site authorities independently approve restricted work and security owners confirm access actions. Proposed responsibilities are learning architecture, not proof that the fictional enterprise already operates this design. Select technology only after the business rule and accountable owner are clear. SAP examples illustrate capabilities, not mandatory product choices.

## 7. One build card: Workforce Foundation Decision Card

Synthetic experiment: Person SYN-P100, employment SYN-E100, approved proposed position SYN-POS200, transfer effective 1 November 2026. Receiving qualification evidence is missing. Identifiers and incident are new teaching data; actual country/plant rules are unknown.

Fill these nine fields on one page: purpose and affected person; current/proposed state and source evidence; identity/object definitions; business date/time and version; application/data owners; human approval and appeal route; chosen option versus alternative with trade-off; exception/fallback and automation boundary; three test observations and revision.

Minimum design reasoning: Keep the person anchor stable unless evidence requires otherwise; establish whether the move changes employment, assignment or only job information before selecting a transaction. Preserve entry time separately from business validity and per-consumer completion.

Do not invent country rules, safety thresholds, collective-agreement terms, live data or missing approvals. Record unknowns with an owner and discovery question. Support an accessible human route for the worker. Consider fatigue, learning opportunity, avoidable travel/rework or resource use where the dilemma provides evidence; do not infer environmental savings.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OW-TOS-HR01-T01 — As-of state

Reveal/input: View SYN-E100 on 31 October and 1 November against the approved change.

Expected design response: Show the correct respective job/assignment state without inventing a second person; flag unresolved employment treatment.

### OW-TOS-HR01-T02 — Missing qualification

Reveal/input: The assistant recommends restricted maintenance based only on active employment.

Expected design response: Hold restricted work and route to the qualified site authority; do not terminate employment or infer competence.

### OW-TOS-HR01-T03 — Receiving outage

Reveal/input: HR saved the approved change, but payroll or access outcome is unconfirmed.

Expected design response: Expose per-consumer exception and accountable owner; use only an approved fallback and reconcile before full completion.

For each test, record input → expected response → actual observed paper response → evidence note → pass/revise. Expected responses are proposed acceptance criteria, not executed results. Publication executes none of these tabletop tests. A bypass of the protected decision requires redesign, even if the card is complete.

## 9. Traceability and honest measures

OW-TOS-HR01-REQ01 — Preserve person/employment identity and business-valid history across the change. Source connection: supporting HR case mandate and OW-M18. Assessed by T01; card must identify the authoritative fact and owner.

OW-TOS-HR01-REQ02 — Separate employment approval, task authorization and receiving-system completion. Source connection: HR case governance/scenario injection and OW-M17/OW-M19. Assessed by T02–T03; card records exception, authority and recovery condition.

OW-TOS-HR01-ADR01 — Prefer a shared semantic core with governed local extensions over unbounded regional copies; compare a rigid global template and its local-fit cost. Compare the stated alternative and trade-off; this is a proposed educational decision that learners may reject with evidence.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: scenarios, not predictions

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. All require approved purpose, evidence, worker participation, accountable ownership and proportionate controls.

2026–2030 — Assist: An assistant could flag missing fields and draft exception explanations; Asha retains approval.

2031–2040 — Connect: Core, learning and plant services could exchange versioned readiness evidence; a connected status would still require valid owner decisions.

2041–2050 — Adapt: Adaptive skills-based assignment could suggest supervised routes as jobs change; local safety authority and worker contestability remain explicit.

Competing future: A federated, low-automation core with strong stewardship may preserve trust better where connectivity and local data quality remain weak.

Disconfirmation question: Would the shared core increase unresolved identity errors or block legitimate local processes compared with a controlled federated pilot? Record the evidence that would make you stop or change the hypothesis.
