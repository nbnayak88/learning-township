## One story: choose, reveal, revise

Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07), Group CHRO, receives a request to move synthetic engineer IT-P100 from a project team into cloud operations. Sofia Tan (OTS-P06), Platform Engineering Director, needs the engineer next week. Omar Haddad (OTS-P10), CISO and Supply-Chain Security Lead, asks who authorized access to the receiving client's environment. Nadia first proposes copying the old job and permissions to avoid delay.
Choose before reading on: Copy all access, delay the whole transfer, or separate the approved employment change from client-specific permissions?
Evidence reveal: The same-employer transfer is approved for 8 November 2026, but the old client assignment ends on 7 November and the new privileged role has no security approval. Nadia revises her decision: preserve person and employment identity, apply the approved assignment on its date, revoke old client access under policy, and hold new privileged access for the delegated security authority. A sandbox assignment can be considered by its owner.
Write what changed your decision and what remains unknown. An accessible explanation and a human challenge route must be available to the affected worker. An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorization.

## Application responsibility and human authority

Core HR — HR owns person, employment, position and assignment facts. Delegated HR approver authorizes effective-dated changes.
Learning — Learning owner holds assessed readiness evidence. Qualified assessor confirms role competence.
IAM / client access — Security owns entitlements and client trust boundaries. Delegated access owner approves grants and revocations.
Payroll / projects — Payroll owns pay results; project owner owns allocation. Each downstream owner confirms receipt and business outcome.
Business owners remain accountable; applications store and process the records. Discover the actual delegated approvers before implementation. Restrict client, candidate, pay and health information to its purpose and permitted viewers. Minimize replicated data and unnecessary compute rather than assuming automation is sustainable.
SAP examples in the preserved source are discussion options, not a technology selection or a claim that configuration has been validated. Start with the business need. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder; it cannot approve a consequential people decision, grant privilege or declare a payment complete.

## One build experiment: Decision Build Card

Synthetic inputs: IT-P100 / IT-E100; old assignment PROJECT-A; new assignment CLOUD-B; entry 1 November 2026; effective 8 November 2026; same employer assumed; new privilege approval absent.
Complete these fields once:
1. People and business need: who benefits, what could go wrong and why this matters in IT services or engineering.
2. Initial choice and reason: compare the choices before seeing the reveal.
3. Evidence reveal and revised choice: state what changed, and one counterexample.
4. Identity, date and source owner: identify authoritative records, effective time, provenance and uncertain facts.
5. Application responsibility: separate record keeping, orchestration, calculation, analytics and access enforcement.
6. Human approval and protected action: name the decision role, limits and worker challenge route.
7. Minimum data and permitted viewers: state purpose, retention question, client isolation and accessible communication.
8. Alternative and local variant: defend a common core with a justified regional/client variant, or explain a different design.
9. Exception owner and recovery: define hold, escalation, retry/reconciliation and confirmation conditions.
10. Measure and missing evidence: Approved dated views with intact identity / required dated views; boundary breaches must be zero in the proposed design.
11. Pre-Foundation question: Define person, employment, position, assignment and access as separate concepts; identify one field whose owner is still unknown.
For each test below record input → expected → observed paper result → evidence → revision. Blank observations remain blank. A complete card can still contain a poor decision.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

OTS-HR01-T01 — Before and on transfer date
Expected proposed behavior: 7 November shows the old assignment; 8 November shows the approved new assignment, with person/employment history retained.
Evidence to record: Save both dated views and approval reference.

OTS-HR01-T02 — Training complete, access unsigned

OTS-HR01-T03 — Downstream system unavailable
