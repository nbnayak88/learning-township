In brief. How requirements are found, analysed, agreed and traced, using BABOK practice.

7.1 Approach

Step What happens Output
Plan Agree stakeholders, techniques, cadence Analysis plan
Elicit Workshops per value stream stage, regional HR sessions, employee and manager research, legal and works-council input Needs, pain points
Analyse Fit-to-standard against the hub; gap, rule and data analysis Requirements, gaps
Specify Write with acceptance criteria; link to components Requirement register
Validate Walk through with owners; prototype in the sandbox Signed baseline
Manage Change control, impact analysis, traceability Updated register

7.2 Techniques used

Process mapping on the value stream, capability mapping, interviews, workshops, document analysis, data profiling of legacy employee, organisation and position records, prototyping in the sandbox, MoSCoW prioritisation and a stakeholder map.
