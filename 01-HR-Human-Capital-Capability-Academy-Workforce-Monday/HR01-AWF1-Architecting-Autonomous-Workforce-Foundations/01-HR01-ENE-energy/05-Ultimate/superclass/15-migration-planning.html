15.3 Waves

Wave 1 is the pilot: one country cluster, core records, organisation, positions, workflows, hand-off to payroll and finance, and SSO. Waves 2 to 4 add benefits, time, verification, mobility, analytics and further countries using the same phases and gates.

15.4 Cutover and data

Legacy employee, organisation and position data is migrated by country cluster after cleansing against the data-quality rules. A dress rehearsal precedes each cutover; the legacy record is frozen read-only at cutover and archived by policy.
