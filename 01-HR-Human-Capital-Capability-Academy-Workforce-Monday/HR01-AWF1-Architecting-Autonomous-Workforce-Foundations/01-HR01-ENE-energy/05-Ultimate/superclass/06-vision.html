Architecture Vision (Phase A)

In brief. One global people record and one position model for OmniVerse Energy, with Employee Central at the centre and every partner replaceable behind clean interfaces. Data is owned, quality is measured and changes are explained.

6.1 Problem

OmniVerse Energy runs an on-premise HR system plus about [30] local HR tools and spreadsheets across more than 50 countries. Organisation structures differ by country, positions are tracked in spreadsheets, benefits are administered by local carriers by file, and employee data is re-keyed between systems. Headcount reports disagree, joiner and mover changes are slow, and no one owns workforce data quality end to end.

6.2 Vision

One trusted record of every person, position and organisation unit, on one platform that connects the best partners today and lets OmniVerse replace any of them tomorrow.

6.4 Scope

In scope: Employment records, organisation and foundation data, position management, job architecture, workflows and approvals, employee documents, global benefits administration, the hand-off to payroll, time, finance and IT, self-service and HR service delivery, and the partner ecosystem around them.

Out of scope: Payroll calculation rules, recruiting, learning, performance and compensation processes, except for the data hand-offs they need from the core record.

6.7 Key decisions at vision stage

Employee Central is the global employee and position system of record; all partners attach through an integration hub; positions drive headcount; delivery follows SAP Activate with a pilot first. See the decision register in Chapter 14.
