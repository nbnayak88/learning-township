In brief. Twenty ecosystem categories mapped to a vendor-neutral capability, an integration pattern, components and a wave. Names are examples only.

21.1 Why vendor-agnostic

Core HR tools and service providers differ by country. OmniVerse defines what each category must do, how it connects and what data it exchanges, then chooses vendors against that definition. A different vendor can take over a category without touching the rest of the architecture.

21.5 Multi-vendor by design

Benefits carriers, pension funds, payroll engines and verification services are multi-vendor by country. The capability interface and canonical model make this possible without extra hub configuration.

21.6 Exit plan per category
