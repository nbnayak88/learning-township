Data Architecture (Phase C)

ADM phase C Information Systems: DataLead streams DataSupporting AI, Security, Integration9P4V V2 ValueStandards TOGAF Phase C, DAMA-DMBOK

In brief. A canonical workforce model, named owners, summary-only sensitive data and event streams to the data platform.

11.1 Principles applied

Employee data held in trust; a canonical workforce model that partners map to; summary, not raw sensitive detail; every entity has an owner and a steward (Chapter 6).
