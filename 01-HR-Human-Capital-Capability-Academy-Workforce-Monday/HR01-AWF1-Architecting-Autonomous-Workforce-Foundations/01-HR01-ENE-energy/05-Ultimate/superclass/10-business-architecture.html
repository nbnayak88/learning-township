10.5 Organisation and roles

Process owner: Head of HR Operations. Regional HR leads own local variants. The Workforce Data Council owns data definitions and quality. Total Rewards owns benefits rules. Legal and Privacy own consent, retention and works-council agreements. The Enterprise Architecture Office owns the capability interfaces.
