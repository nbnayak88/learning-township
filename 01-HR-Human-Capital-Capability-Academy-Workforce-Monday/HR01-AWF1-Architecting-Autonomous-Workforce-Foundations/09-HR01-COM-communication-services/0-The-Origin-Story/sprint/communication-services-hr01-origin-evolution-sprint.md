## The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one workforce transfer that preserves identity and separates employment from permission to operate.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, receives a request to move a fiber technician into a NOC support assignment before a live streaming event. Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08), Service Assurance and NOC Director, needs shift coverage. The personnel record is active, but the new assignment, learning evidence and network permissions disagree.

**Choose first:** Release all access, hold the whole transfer, or separate the approved employment change from operational authorization?

**Evidence reveal:** The same-employer transfer is approved for tomorrow. A course is complete, but no designated operational authority has approved privileged NOC work. Nadia allows the dated HR change and asks Marcus for an authorized alternative assignment while access remains held.

**Counterexample:** An active HR record and a completed course still cannot prove permission to change a network.

OmniConnect is a fictional communication-services enterprise. The source stipulates 62,000 employees across 28 countries, spanning mobile and 5G, fiber, media and streaming, enterprise networks, and advertising. No new site or workforce counts are inferred. Existing source KPIs and financial figures are fictional baselines, proposals or targets, with no achieved result asserted.

## Origins and responsibility

These closed historical windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniConnect corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present.

| Teaching window | Process evolution |
|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Personnel registers record the worker |
| 1985–1994 | Databases reconcile person and employment |
| 1995–2004 | HRIS connects positions and dated changes |
| 2005–2014 | Self-service links country and business records |
| 2015–2025 | Rules and analytics highlight inconsistent assignments |

| Application responsibility | Accountable owner | Data and human boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Core HR | HR steward | Person, employment, job, organization and position facts; delegated HR approver authorizes changes |
| Learning | Learning owner and assessor | Completion, assessment and expiry evidence |
| IAM and operations | Security owner and operational authority | Provision only approved entitlements; operational authority grants task permission |

Employee Central is a source example for core workforce records, with separate Learning and IAM responsibilities.

A source-system record, receiving-system acknowledgment and human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route. Avoid personal-data joins with subscriber or audience data unless a legitimate purpose, access boundary and minimum data are established.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test ID | Focus | Evidence reveal | Expected result in the proposed design |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCN-XL-HR01-T01 | Dated transfer | Compare 1 and 2 November views. | Keep prior history and stable identity; apply the approved new job only on its effective date. |
| OCN-XL-HR01-T02 | Course without authority | Reveal completed learning but absent NOC authorization. | Keep privileged work held and request the designated authority; offer approved alternative duties. |
| OCN-XL-HR01-T03 | Unconfirmed receiver | IAM endpoint is unavailable. | Show HR committed and IAM unconfirmed separately; reconcile before claiming completion. |

Record initial decision → expected result → observed paper result → evidence → revision. All observed-result fields start empty. Acceptance requires a reasoned card and all three reviewed results after revisions. A material authority or privacy bypass requires redesign. Publishing the exercise does not execute it or prove operational outcomes.

## AI driven curiosity and the next chapter

| Scenario window | Exploration |
|---|---|
| 2026–2030 — Assist | Flag conflicting transfer evidence |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | Coordinate readiness across learning and access |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | Adapt assignment suggestions to changing roles |

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. The time bands support curiosity about possible designs.

**Competing future:** fragmented rules, weak interoperability or low trust keep local expert judgment and simpler manual workflows more effective than connected AI. Compare that path with the scenario before choosing an experiment.

**Question that could disprove your hypothesis:** Would unresolved privilege exceptions remain as frequent as with a manual checklist?

**Reflect:** What evidence changed your first choice? Who can challenge the decision? What would make the alternative better?
