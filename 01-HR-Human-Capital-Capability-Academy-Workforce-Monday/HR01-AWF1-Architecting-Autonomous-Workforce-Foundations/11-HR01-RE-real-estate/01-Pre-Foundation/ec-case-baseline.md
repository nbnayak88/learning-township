## Bounded pilot adopted for this edition
New synthetic assumptions, chosen to make the case executable:
- Country: India; legal employer: OmniSpace Property Services India Pvt Ltd, synthetic code OSP-IN01.
- Operating center: Hyderabad; no global HQ claim.
- Population: 120 direct employees, represented by synthetic IDs; 20 supplier workers represented in the external-workforce register, outside employee migration.
- Two serviced properties: PROPERTY-A and PROPERTY-B, both Hyderabad; both under the same pilot employer. These are serviced assets, not automatically separately owned legal entities.
- 100 employees have PROPERTY-A as primary property and 20 PROPERTY-B at baseline. RE-P100 / RE-E100 is included within the 100.
- Initial RE-P100 assignment: OSP-IN01, department FAC-OPS, position FAC-TECH-01, location HYD-A, primary property PROPERTY-A, cost center IN-FAC-A.
- Approved permanent move: 2 November 2026, position FAC-TECH-02, location HYD-B, primary property PROPERTY-B, cost center IN-FAC-B. Source position becomes vacant; receiving position must be available. Employment identity continues.
- After the move: 99 primary assignments at A and 21 at B; employee population remains 120.
- Pilot excludes actual country statutory configuration, payroll calculation, tenant data, global assignments and production connections. Cross-employer and international moves are design/test variants requiring separate approval before implementation.
- No revenue, AUM, rollout dates, budget, measured error rates or ROI is adopted.

## People and outcomes
Nadia Fernandes (OSP-P06): accountable HR sponsor; Maya Patel (OSP-P03): property operations authority; Omar Haddad (OSP-P09): security authority. HR data steward owns record quality; payroll owner governs payroll acceptance; integration owner handles reconciliation; employee can challenge incorrect records through assisted HR service.

Value stream: Request workforce change → validate identity/assignment → approve → apply dated HR change → confirm receiving services → verify operational readiness → measure service continuity.
Outcome hypothesis: correct dated assignments and explicit handoff states reduce avoidable transfer exceptions. Workforce changes alone do not establish energy, occupancy, asset-value or tenant-satisfaction gains.

## Vocabulary
Person identifies the individual; employment identifies the employer relationship; job information describes dated assignment; job classification defines a type of work; position represents a planned role; location represents a work site; property identifies the serviced asset; cost center supports accounting responsibility. One person can serve multiple properties without automatically receiving multiple employments.
