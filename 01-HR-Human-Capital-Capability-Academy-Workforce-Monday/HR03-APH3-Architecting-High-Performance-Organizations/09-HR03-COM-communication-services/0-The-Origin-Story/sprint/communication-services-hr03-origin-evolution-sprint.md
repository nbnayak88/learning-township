## The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one team goal that rewards verified service recovery and fair contribution.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08), Service Assurance and NOC Director, sees a team celebrate fewer alarms during a streaming peak. Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, asks whether subscriber service recovered and whether quieter work by field staff appears in the appraisal.

**Choose first:** Reward the alarm reduction, discard the goal, or pair a service outcome with workload and evidence checks?

**Evidence reveal:** A suppression rule reduced alarms while a sample subscriber journey remained unavailable. Marcus revises the goal and records field restoration work before any rating discussion.

**Counterexample:** Fewer alerts can mean lower visibility rather than better service.

OmniConnect is a fictional communication-services enterprise. The source stipulates 62,000 employees across 28 countries, spanning mobile and 5G, fiber, media and streaming, enterprise networks, and advertising. No new site or workforce counts are inferred. These closed historical windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniConnect corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present.

| 2005–2014 | Continuous feedback joins project and team work |

| Application responsibility | Accountable owner | Data and human boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Performance system | People managers and HR process owner | Goals, feedback and documented ratings; managers decide after calibration |
| Service assurance | NOC service owner | Incident and restored-service evidence |
| Analytics | Metric steward | Definitions, cohort, lineage and balancing measures; correlation alone proves no cause |

Performance & Goals is an example system for goals and reviews; OSS owns service evidence.

A source-system record, receiving-system acknowledgment and human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route. Avoid personal-data joins with subscriber or audience data unless a legitimate purpose, access boundary and minimum data are established.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test ID | Focus | Evidence reveal | Expected result in the proposed design |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCN-XL-HR03-T01 | Metric gaming | Alarm count improves but subscriber check fails. | Withhold recovery claim; revise the goal using service evidence. |
| OCN-XL-HR03-T02 | Invisible contribution | Field work restores service but NOC dashboard credits only alert handling. | Review role-relevant contribution and shared dependencies before rating. |
| OCN-XL-HR03-T03 | AI summary challenge | An assistant omits the worker's documented correction. | Manager checks original evidence, invites response and repairs the summary before calibration. |

Record initial decision → expected result → observed paper result → evidence → revision. All observed-result fields start empty. Acceptance requires a reasoned card and all three reviewed results after revisions. A material authority or privacy bypass requires redesign. Publishing the exercise does not execute it or prove operational outcomes.

| 2026–2030 — Assist | Summarize review evidence with sources |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | Connect team goals and service dependencies |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | Adapt goal review cadence to changing work |

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. The time bands support curiosity about possible designs.

**Competing future:** fragmented rules, weak interoperability or low trust keep local expert judgment and simpler manual workflows more effective than connected AI. Compare that path with the scenario before choosing an experiment.

**Question that could disprove your hypothesis:** Would the new goal still encourage suppression or shift work unfairly to field teams?

**Reflect:** What evidence changed your first choice? Who can challenge the decision? What would make the alternative better?
