## 3. Origins: the problem survives the medium

These closed historical teaching windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniHealth's corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; paper and older systems can coexist through the present.

1970–1984 — Noticeboards, local HR desks and telephone help offer personal contact; access depends on shift and location.

1985–1994 — Intranets and shared directories make guidance searchable; stale documents may have no accountable owner.

1995–2004 — Employee portals bring forms and status together; an attractive page does not prove receipt by the service owner.

2005–2014 — Mobile entry points and personalized workspaces reduce navigation; shared devices and inaccessible journeys can exclude staff.

2015–2025 — Conversational assistance surfaces guidance; confidential handoffs, reliable status and authoritative policy still require deliberate design.

Counterexample: a single portal can reduce visible application count while increasing the steps needed by a night-shift worker.

## 4. One story, one consequential choice

Nina Fernandes (OHS-P06), Group CHRO, watches a synthetic night-shift worker submit a payroll question from a shared workstation. The screen freezes without a receipt. Elena Brooks (OHS-P08), Patient and Family Experience Director, lends an accessibility perspective, while Omar Haddad (OHS-P10), CISO and Privacy Lead, asks what the next user can see. Their roles support the design review; the HR service owner remains accountable for the case.

Pause before the reveal. Choose submit repeatedly, assume the portal resolved the issue, or expose verifiable status and a private accountable human route. State who benefits, who bears the risk and what evidence you need.

Evidence reveal: a ticket reference may exist, but the browser has no confirmation. The suggested policy article is superseded. The worker's next message is a confidential workplace concern, and a generic HR queue cannot substitute for urgent clinical escalation.

Revised decision: Nina separates uncertain submission from resolution and routes policy correction to its owner. Omar requires a private session boundary and restricted handling of the sensitive concern. The worker receives an accessible alternative route and a verified case-status check; the service must not make them repeat sensitive details unnecessarily. A learner may defend a different option if the card names its authority, evidence, trade-off and conditions for stopping. Missing evidence remains unknown; an urgent request does not manufacture approval.

## 5. Applications serve accountable people

Proposed responsibilities, subject to local discovery:

- The employee entry point presents navigation, permitted guidance and status; its product owner owns usability and accessibility, not every underlying transaction.
- HR case management owns receipt, routing and service-status records; assigned HR specialists own response and resolution.
- Policy owners approve/version guidance; source HR/payroll/learning applications own their actual business transactions and authoritative outcomes.
- IAM/security owns session and access controls. Designated confidential channels own sensitive concerns; clinical emergency/escalation routes remain governed by clinical operations.

Across these boundaries, HR owns employment facts; designated clinical governance owns clinical privilege decisions; IAM administers approved access. Employment, learning completion, account access and permission for clinical work are distinct states. Share only the data needed for the declared purpose; keep patient records and detailed health information out of general HR learning cards. Omar Haddad (OHS-P10), CISO and Privacy Lead, governs privacy/access assurance; jurisdiction-specific applicability remains unresolved.

SAP examples illustrate responsibilities rather than prescribe a purchase: Work Zone as an illustrative entry point over SuccessFactors and approved enterprise services; the portal is not the system of record for every task. No feature availability, license entitlement, configuration, legal compliance or clinical conformance is established here. AI may draft a reminder or summarize permitted evidence; it cannot grant clinical privilege, make a high-impact employment decision or attest to payment or delivery.

## 7. One synthetic build experiment

Synthetic worker HC-P1000; shared workstation HC-K1000; case reference HC-CASE1000 unconfirmed; article HC-POL1000 version 1 superseded by version 2. No real grievance or health data.

Complete one Employee Help Journey Build Card with these six fields:

1. Need and scope: Help a worker reach the correct accountable service with understandable status and an accessible alternative. Explain the capability and value-stream step before naming a tool.
2. Facts and time: preserve identifiers, source, effective/as-of time, unknowns and approved policy version. Distinguish a source fact from your assumption.
3. Decision and ownership: Map need → permitted channel → verified receipt → assigned owner → response → confirmed resolution, including a confidential branch and non-digital option. Record one alternative, its trade-off and the evidence that could favour it.
4. Handoff and recovery: Check the unique case reference before resubmitting; use the approved help fallback if status cannot be confirmed. End shared sessions, hide private case history and state which designated owner must resolve each uncertainty. Name the accountable exception role, accessible human route and condition for reconciliation.
5. Local variant and data: choose one facility/language/role variation; justify minimum fields, purpose, owner, retention decision and viewers. Retain a non-digital route.
6. Evidence and learning: record T01–T03 input → expected → observed paper result → revision → reviewer question on this same card. Leave results unfilled until observed.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OHS-HR10-T01 — Uncertain receipt

Input/reveal: Form freezes after submission and a second attempt is proposed.

Expected in the proposed design: Show unknown receipt; reconcile the stable case reference before duplication; offer an accessible human route.

Record: Receipt state, retry gate, service owner and worker message. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR10-T02 — Stale or wrong guidance

Input/reveal: Search returns superseded policy and an urgent clinical query.

Expected in the proposed design: Use current permitted HR guidance or escalate uncertainty; urgent clinical matters follow the designated operational route.

Record: Article/version, content owner and correct channel boundary. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR10-T03 — Confidential shared-device handoff

Input/reveal: Worker raises a sensitive concern before the next workstation user signs in.

Expected in the proposed design: Restrict details to the designated confidential channel; verify session separation and private continuation. Tabletop reasoning alone is not a security test.

Record: Minimum disclosure, permitted handler, session control and alternative access. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

Acceptance is a defensible completed card and an explanation of all three decisions after revision. A paper discussion proves neither a working system nor a safe clinical service. Publishing this lesson executes no tests.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: three scenarios

Scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps:
- 2026–2030 — Assist: An assistant explains current approved guidance and drafts a case summary with worker control.
- 2031–2040 — Connect: Governed services expose reliable status across HR applications without copying unnecessary details.
- 2041–2050 — Adapt: A bounded experience adapts channel and language to worker choice while preserving privacy and human escalation.

Competing future: facilities retain locally governed workflows and modest deterministic tools because evidence quality, cost or worker trust makes broader automation unsuitable.

Falsification question: Would conversational self-service increase repeated disclosure or exclude workers who use shared devices or need human help? State what observation would make you reduce, redesign or stop assistance. Human accountability does not disappear in any scenario.
