## Origins: the question that survives each era

These closed windows group process evolution for teaching; they are not universal invention or adoption dates. Adoption overlaps. Paper, spreadsheets and older systems can coexist through the present.
1970–1984 — Personnel clerks send forms between departments

2005–2014 — APIs and orchestration connect services

## One story: choose, reveal, revise

Daniel Weber (OTS-P12), Director Enterprise Architecture, reviews a synthetic client-assignment end event. Omar Haddad (OTS-P10), CISO and Supply-Chain Security Lead, still sees an active entitlement. Marcus Okoro (OTS-P08), SRE and Managed Services Director, asks whether the integration's green transport log proves revocation.
Choose before reading on: Replay everything, trust the transport log, or reconcile target state and recover under a versioned contract?
Evidence reveal: Event EVT-1200 was delivered twice and an older assignment-start event arrived later. The target rejected the new schema but the dashboard counted delivery as success. Daniel separates transport acknowledgement from business confirmation, quarantines the incompatible message, and requires ordered/version-aware, idempotent recovery with a security-owned urgent revocation route.
Write what changed your decision and what remains unknown. An accessible explanation and a human challenge route must be available to the affected worker. An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorization.

## Application responsibility and human authority

Core HR / assignment source — HR owns employment; assignment owner owns client assignment facts. Delegated owners authorize source lifecycle events.
Integration — Integration owner governs contracts, mapping and delivery evidence. Support authority controls quarantine and replay.
IAM target — Security owns entitlement state and revocation evidence. Access owner verifies target outcome and urgent containment.
Operations / monitoring — Service owner owns alerts and recovery coordination. Named incident owner resolves unknown outcomes and reports status.
Business owners remain accountable; applications store and process the records. Discover the actual delegated approvers before implementation. Restrict client, candidate, pay and health information to its purpose and permitted viewers. Minimize replicated data and unnecessary compute rather than assuming automation is sustainable.
SAP examples in the preserved source are discussion options, not a technology selection or a claim that configuration has been validated. Start with the business need. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder; it cannot approve a consequential people decision, grant privilege or declare a payment complete.

## One build experiment: Decision Build Card

Synthetic inputs: Worker IT-P1200; assignment ASG-1200 ends 8 November 2026; event EVT-1200 version 2 duplicated; older start version 1 delayed; target rejects schema; employment remains active.
Complete these fields once:
1. People and business need: who benefits, what could go wrong and why this matters in IT services or engineering.
2. Initial choice and reason: compare the choices before seeing the reveal.
3. Evidence reveal and revised choice: state what changed, and one counterexample.
4. Identity, date and source owner: identify authoritative records, effective time, provenance and uncertain facts.
5. Application responsibility: separate record keeping, orchestration, calculation, analytics and access enforcement.
6. Human approval and protected action: name the decision role, limits and worker challenge route.
7. Minimum data and permitted viewers: state purpose, retention question, client isolation and accessible communication.
8. Alternative and local variant: defend a common core with a justified regional/client variant, or explain a different design.
9. Exception owner and recovery: define hold, escalation, retry/reconciliation and confirmation conditions.
10. Measure and missing evidence: Critical handoffs with verified target business outcome / critical handoffs attempted; unknown outcomes stay unknown.
11. Pre-Foundation question: Define source event, contract version, event key, target acknowledgement and business confirmation; map one recovery owner.
For each test below record input → expected → observed paper result → evidence → revision. Blank observations remain blank. A complete card can still contain a poor decision.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

OTS-HR12-T01 — Target rejects schema
Expected proposed behavior: Quarantine incompatible event, show business outcome unconfirmed and route to contract/support owner.
Evidence to record: Record contract version, rejection and authorized recovery.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR12-T02 — Duplicate and reordered events
Expected proposed behavior: Use event identity, assignment key and version semantics; do not reinstate ended access from stale input.
Evidence to record: Record target state before/after and duplicate handling rationale.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR12-T03 — Unknown target after replay
Expected proposed behavior: Reconcile authoritative target state before another action; invoke security-owned urgent containment where needed.
Evidence to record: Record acknowledgement versus outcome, owner and closure evidence.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.
Acceptance: one complete card, three recorded tabletop observations after any revisions, and an explanation of source ownership and human authority. A breached authority or privacy boundary requires redesign. Publishing these tests does not execute them.
