# Day 1 V2: Model
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Every organisation I have worked with had the same problem: lots of HR tools, no map. [pause]

Before I teach you anything, let me earn the right to. I have spent my career in SAP HCM and SuccessFactors, designing and fixing human capital platforms for global enterprises. What you will learn here comes from real implementations, not theory.

**Story and mission.** Early in my career I watched a brilliant HR team fail, not because of the software, but because nobody could see how the pieces fit. That day I decided to build a map. My mission: Architecting for a Better World, one well-designed workforce at a time.

**The model.** Here is the map. [show slide] Twelve product academies: Employee Central, Recruiting and Onboarding, Performance and Goals, Succession and Development, Compensation and Variable Pay, Learning, Employee Central Payroll, Time Tracking, Workforce Analytics and Planning, Employee Experience, Business AI with Joule, and Integration Suite. Eleven GICS sectors: Energy, Materials, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary, Consumer Staples, Health Care, Financial Services, Information Technology, Communication Services, Utilities, and Real Estate. Each course ID follows one rule: Series dash Sector, like AWF1-EN. 12 x 11 = 132.

We compress the twelve into six concepts of two academies each, so every concept covers 22 courses. Learn six concepts, translate through one sector lens, and you have covered all 132.

**Take notes** now. Presence matters: if you are not writing, you are not learning. [pause] Reinforcement: say the formula once more out loud.

**CTA.** Take two minutes now and share your notes below. Three lines is enough: what you learned, what surprised you, what you will do. Next video: Self Discovery.
