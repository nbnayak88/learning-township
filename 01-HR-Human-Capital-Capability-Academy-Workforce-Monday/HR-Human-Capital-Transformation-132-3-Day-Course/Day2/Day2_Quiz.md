## Concept check (short answer)
- What is the main role of the Workforce Core?  
  *Answer: A single trusted employee record that all other HR processes read from*
- Which two product series anchor Concept 1?  
  *Answer: AWF1 Employee Central and ATA2 Recruiting and Onboarding*
- Why do many HR transformations fail?  
  *Answer: Inconsistent foundation data (org, job, hiring), not missing modules*
- What does Succession and Development convert performance data into?  
  *Answer: A visible pipeline of ready successors and development plans*
- Which series cover Concept 2?  
  *Answer: APH3 Performance and Goals, AGL4 Succession and Development*
- What is the risk of disconnected performance and succession data?  
  *Answer: Promotion by politics and critical roles with no successor*
- Why connect reward and learning?  
  *Answer: Reward signals what matters, learning builds the means to deliver it*
- Which series cover Concept 3?  
  *Answer: ARP5 Compensation and Variable Pay, ALM6 Learning and Opportunity Marketplace*
- What does the Opportunity Marketplace enable?  
  *Answer: Matching people to gigs, projects and internal roles*
