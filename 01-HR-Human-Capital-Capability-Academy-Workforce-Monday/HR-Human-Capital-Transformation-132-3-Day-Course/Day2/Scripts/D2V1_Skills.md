*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Ten skills. That is all that stands between you and architect-level human capital work.

Welcome to Day 2, and congratulations on completing Day 1! [pause]

Your goal from the Goal Card needs skills. Here are the top ten. [show slide]

1. **Systems thinking for people processes**: See hire-to-retire as one connected flow.
2. **Org and employee data modelling**: Model structure, jobs and positions once.
3. **Process mapping and fit-to-standard**: Map as-is, test against standard, isolate gaps.
4. **Business case and HR value storytelling**: Turn HR change into CFO-ready value.
5. **Stakeholder and change leadership**: Move CHRO, HRBP, IT and employees together.
6. **Payroll and compliance literacy**: Know where pay can break and why.
7. **Workforce analytics and KPI design**: Choose few metrics that drive decisions.
8. **Integration and data-flow design**: Design interfaces and master-data ownership.
9. **AI prompting and AI governance for HR**: Use AI well and safely on people data.
10. **Sector translation across 11 GICS sectors**: Adapt one architecture to any industry.

Rate yourself one to five on each. Circle your lowest two; we will build them through the six concepts.
