**1. What is the concept?**
Performance and Goals turns strategy into measurable individual and team outcomes. Succession and Development turns those results into a visible pipeline of ready successors and growth plans. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Organisations do not run out of strategy; they run out of ready leaders. Without connected performance and succession data, promotions become politics and critical roles stay single points of failure.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A health care group discovered that 40 percent of its critical roles had no named successor. Linking ratings, potential and talent pools in one place made the gap visible in a single view, and the board funded a development track that quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Goals cascade from strategy to team to individual; Calibrated, fairer performance conversations; Visible bench strength for critical roles; Development spend aimed at real succession gaps. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Health Care succession is licence-and-credential sensitive; Information Technology succession is skills-and-project driven.
