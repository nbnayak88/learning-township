## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: in Energy the core must model crews, sites and certifications; in Financial Services it must model regulated roles and licences. Same architecture, different data.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Health Care succession is licence-and-credential sensitive; Information Technology succession is skills-and-project driven.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Industrials ties variable pay to safety and uptime; Financial Services ties it to risk-adjusted outcomes and regulation.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Energy and Utilities need shift and standby pay; Real Estate and IT often need project-based and contractor time.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Consumer Discretionary needs seasonal planning; Communication Services needs fast skill-shift planning.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Financial Services demands strict AI explainability; Energy needs integration with operational and safety systems.
