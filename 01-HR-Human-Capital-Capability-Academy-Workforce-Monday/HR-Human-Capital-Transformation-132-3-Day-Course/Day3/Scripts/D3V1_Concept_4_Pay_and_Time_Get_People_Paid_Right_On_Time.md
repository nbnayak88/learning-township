**1. What is the concept?**
Employee Central Payroll turns the employee record into accurate, compliant pay. Time Tracking supplies the hours, absences and shift data that payroll depends on. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Payroll is the one HR process every employee notices when it breaks. Time data errors become pay errors, and pay errors become trust, legal and cost problems in any country.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A global manufacturer ran eight payroll vendors. Consolidating on one payroll architecture with time feeding it directly cut off-cycle runs sharply and gave Finance a single posting view. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Accurate, compliant pay across countries; Fewer manual corrections and retro runs; Clear audit trail from clock-in to payslip; Employee trust, the quiet engine of retention. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Energy and Utilities need shift and standby pay; Real Estate and IT often need project-based and contractor time.
