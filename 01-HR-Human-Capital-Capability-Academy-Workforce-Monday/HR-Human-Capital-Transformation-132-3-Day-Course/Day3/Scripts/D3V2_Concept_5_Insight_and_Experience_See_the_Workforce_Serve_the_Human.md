**1. What is the concept?**
Workforce Analytics and Planning gives leaders measurable insight and forward-looking workforce plans. The Employee Experience Suite captures how people actually feel at the moments that matter. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Planning on gut feel misses attrition, skill gaps and burnout until they are expensive. Experience data explains the numbers that analytics only reveals.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A telecom company paired attrition analytics with onboarding-experience surveys and found the real driver: week-two manager contact. One small process change moved retention more than a pay revision. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Early warning on attrition and capacity; Workforce plans tied to business demand; Experience insight at onboarding, growth and exit; Evidence-based HR value cases for the board. [on screen: four benefits]
