**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and insight into HR work. The Integration Suite connects HR with finance, IT, identity and partner systems so data flows once, reliably. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI is only as good as the data it can reach and the governance around it. Integration is what makes that data trustworthy; governance is what makes the AI safe to use on people decisions.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. An HR service team used a Joule-style assistant for policy questions, but only after integration gave it a single verified employee record. Answers improved overnight because the data underneath was finally one version. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster HR service with guided, explainable AI; Automated handoffs between HR, finance and IT; Governed, auditable AI on sensitive people data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
