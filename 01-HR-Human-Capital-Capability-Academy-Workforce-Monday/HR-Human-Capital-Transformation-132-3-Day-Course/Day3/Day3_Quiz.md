- What does Time Tracking supply to payroll?  
  *Answer: Hours, absences and shift data*
- Which series cover Concept 4?  
  *Answer: APE7 Employee Central Payroll, AWT8 Time Tracking*
- Why is payroll high stakes?  
  *Answer: Every employee notices errors; they hit trust, legal exposure and cost*
- What does experience data add to analytics?  
  *Answer: It explains why the numbers move*
- Which series cover Concept 5?  
  *Answer: AWI9 Workforce Analytics and Planning, ADX0 Employee Experience Suite*
- Name one early warning signal analytics provides.  
  *Answer: Attrition, capacity or skill-gap risk*
- What makes AI safe on people decisions?  
  *Answer: Governance, explainability and trustworthy integrated data*
- Which series cover Concept 6?  
  *Answer: AAI1 Business AI, Joule and AI Agents; AIG2 Integration Suite*
- What does the Integration Suite do for HR?
