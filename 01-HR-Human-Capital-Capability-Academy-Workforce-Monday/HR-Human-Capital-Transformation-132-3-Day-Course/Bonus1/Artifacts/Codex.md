# Bonus 1 Codex: Build the Hr Transformation Business Case

**Tool:** Hr Value Case Canvas (Excel) from the Codex

## What
A business case is a one-page, CHRO and CFO-ready argument that links a people change to money, risk and growth. It names the problem, the cost of doing nothing, the benefit, the investment and the proof.

## Why
Good designs die in budget meetings. Leaders fund outcomes, not modules. If you can express a HR transformation idea in their language (cost, risk, revenue, speed), your design gets built.

## Benefits
- Faster funding and sponsor commitment
- Clear success measures from day one
- Fewer scope arguments because value is agreed
- A career-defining skill that moves you from consultant to advisor

## Tactical steps
1. Pick one of the 132 courses and sector, for example Energy (AWF1-EN) or Financial Services (APE7-FS).
2. Write the problem in one sentence with one number.
3. Quantify the cost of doing nothing (cost, risk, lost revenue).
4. List 3 benefits and attach a KPI and an owner to each.
5. Estimate the investment (people, licences, change) and a range, not a point.
6. Add risks and the simplest mitigation for each.
7. Write the 60-second story for the CHRO and CFO: problem, impact, option, ask.
