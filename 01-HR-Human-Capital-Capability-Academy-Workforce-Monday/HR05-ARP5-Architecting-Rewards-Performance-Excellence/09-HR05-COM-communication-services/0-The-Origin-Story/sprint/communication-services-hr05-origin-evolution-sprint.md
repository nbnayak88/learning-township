## The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one reward decision that connects eligibility to trusted service and fair review.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, reviews an enterprise-sales bonus with David Chen (OCN-P02), Group CFO. A dashboard counts a connectivity contract as activated, while the service owner still records partial fulfillment.

**Choose first:** Pay from the dashboard, cancel the award, or hold the uncertain component for a documented evidence review?

**Evidence reveal:** The signed sale is valid, but part of the service is unavailable. The fictional policy requires verified activation for one component. David preserves the sales evidence and holds only that component pending review and employee appeal.

**Counterexample:** An order, invoice or commission calculation cannot independently prove that service works.

OmniConnect is a fictional communication-services enterprise. The source stipulates 62,000 employees across 28 countries, spanning mobile and 5G, fiber, media and streaming, enterprise networks, and advertising. No new site or workforce counts are inferred. ## Origins and responsibility

These closed historical windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniConnect corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present.

| 1970–1984 | Wage grades and local bonuses |
| 1985–1994 | Salary surveys and merit budgets |
| 1995–2004 | Integrated compensation cycles and eligibility rules |
| 2005–2014 | Variable pay joins performance and business metrics |
| 2015–2025 | Analytics assists equity and exception review |

| Application responsibility | Accountable owner | Data and human boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards application | Rewards policy owner | Eligibility, rules, proposals and approved awards |
| Service and finance | Service owner and finance controller | Verified activation and financial evidence |
| Payroll | Payroll owner | Approved earnings input, gross-to-net processing and payment reconciliation |

Compensation & Variable Pay can manage reward proposals; payroll independently processes approved earnings.

A source-system record, receiving-system acknowledgment and human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route. Avoid personal-data joins with subscriber or audience data unless a legitimate purpose, access boundary and minimum data are established.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test ID | Focus | Evidence reveal | Expected result in the proposed design |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCN-XL-HR05-T01 | Partial service | Sales closed but activation incomplete. | Apply the stated component rule; preserve uncertainty and review the affected portion. |
| OCN-XL-HR05-T02 | Cross-unit transfer | Worker moves during the eligibility period. | Use dated assignment and policy evidence; avoid double credit or silent loss of eligibility. |
| OCN-XL-HR05-T03 | Gross versus paid | Approved award exists but payroll receipt is absent. | Show award approved and payment unconfirmed separately; payroll reconciles before any payment claim. |

Record initial decision → expected result → observed paper result → evidence → revision. All observed-result fields start empty. Acceptance requires a reasoned card and all three reviewed results after revisions. A material authority or privacy bypass requires redesign. Publishing the exercise does not execute it or prove operational outcomes.

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. The time bands support curiosity about possible designs.

**Competing future:** fragmented rules, weak interoperability or low trust keep local expert judgment and simpler manual workflows more effective than connected AI. Compare that path with the scenario before choosing an experiment.

**Question that could disprove your hypothesis:** Would the proposed metric increase pressure to declare activation prematurely?

**Reflect:** What evidence changed your first choice? Who can challenge the decision? What would make the alternative better?
