## 3. Origins: the problem survives the medium

These closed historical teaching windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniHealth's corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; paper and older systems can coexist through the present.

1970–1984 — Local wage scales and discretionary awards support retention; inconsistent criteria create inequity.

1985–1994 — Pay surveys and compensation spreadsheets improve comparisons; a market number needs role and geography context.

1995–2004 — Integrated merit and variable-pay cycles standardize approval; budget availability does not prove fair allocation.

2005–2014 — Total-rewards and self-service statements improve visibility; workers still need understandable reasons and a review route.

2015–2025 — Analytics and assisted recommendations expose patterns; a model score cannot establish fair pay or authorize a payment.

Counterexample: rewarding the highest task volume can favour easier assignments and discourage careful coordination.

## 4. One story, one consequential choice

Michael Chen (OHS-P02), Group CFO, receives a proposed retention award for a synthetic diagnostic team. Nina Fernandes (OHS-P06), Group CHRO, wants to retain scarce skills without weakening internal equity. The proposal cites fast turnaround and fits an illustrative budget, but gives no validated comparison across roles or working patterns.

Pause before the reveal. Choose approve because funds remain, reject all differentiation, or examine evidence and equity before a bounded human approval. State who benefits, who bears the risk and what evidence you need.

Evidence reveal: one worker transferred mid-period and appears on both facility worksheets. The turnaround measure does not adjust for service mix, and a proposed award row is labelled 'paid' despite no payroll confirmation.

Revised decision: Nina removes the unsupported payment claim and reconciles eligibility periods before a decision. Michael asks for a documented affordability and equity rationale. A delegated compensation approver can authorize a proposal only after the gaps are resolved; payroll separately calculates and confirms the payment outcome. A learner may defend a different option if the card names its authority, evidence, trade-off and conditions for stopping. Missing evidence remains unknown; an urgent request does not manufacture approval.

## 5. Applications serve accountable people

Proposed responsibilities, subject to local discovery:

- Compensation services own proposals, eligibility and approval records under the rewards owner; the delegated compensation authority authorizes awards.
- Core HR owns effective-dated role, employment and facility facts used for eligibility; HR resolves inconsistent assignment data.
- Performance/quality owners define the approved measures and limitations; an incentive must not imply unvalidated clinical benefit.
- Payroll owns gross-to-net calculation and reconciliation; Finance owns funding/accounting assurance; banks confirm payment states through approved channels. A worksheet cannot attest to settlement.

Across these boundaries, HR owns employment facts; designated clinical governance owns clinical privilege decisions; IAM administers approved access. Employment, learning completion, account access and permission for clinical work are distinct states. Share only the data needed for the declared purpose; keep patient records and detailed health information out of general HR learning cards. Omar Haddad (OHS-P10), CISO and Privacy Lead, governs privacy/access assurance; jurisdiction-specific applicability remains unresolved.

SAP examples illustrate responsibilities rather than prescribe a purchase: Compensation and Variable Pay linked to Employee Central, payroll and Finance; these are separate responsibilities even when integrated. No feature availability, license entitlement, configuration, legal compliance or clinical conformance is established here. AI may draft a reminder or summarize permitted evidence; it cannot grant clinical privilege, make a high-impact employment decision or attest to payment or delivery.

## 7. One synthetic build experiment

Synthetic HC-P500 / HC-E500; same-employer transfer on 16 October 2026; facility award worksheets A and B each include the worker. Illustrative proposal 1,000 currency units; currency, local policy, tax and eligibility rules intentionally unconfirmed. No actual payment.

Complete one Reward Decision Build Card with these six fields:

1. Need and scope: Retain capability through a transparent reward decision without duplicate eligibility or unsupported outcome claims. Explain the capability and value-stream step before naming a tool.
2. Facts and time: preserve identifiers, source, effective/as-of time, unknowns and approved policy version. Distinguish a source fact from your assumption.
3. Decision and ownership: Record the approved policy version, eligibility period, measure limitations, equity comparator, affordability and delegated approver; distinguish proposal, approved input, calculated pay and settlement. Record one alternative, its trade-off and the evidence that could favour it.
4. Handoff and recovery: Hold only the disputed award input while the rewards/HR owners reconcile periods; payroll applies its approved exception process. Give the worker an explanation and review route without exposing colleagues' pay. Name the accountable exception role, accessible human route and condition for reconciliation.
5. Local variant and data: choose one facility/language/role variation; justify minimum fields, purpose, owner, retention decision and viewers. Retain a non-digital route.
6. Evidence and learning: record T01–T03 input → expected → observed paper result → revision → reviewer question on this same card. Leave results unfilled until observed.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OHS-HR05-T01 — Mid-period duplicate

Input/reveal: Worker appears in both facility worksheets.

Expected in the proposed design: Reconcile the approved eligibility rule and effective dates before any award; do not assume proration or two awards.

Record: Policy gap, dated eligibility views and resolved or held input. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR05-T02 — Volume versus value

Input/reveal: High turnaround lacks comparable service mix or quality evidence.

Expected in the proposed design: Do not infer clinical quality or automatically reward volume; request valid criteria and human review.

Record: Comparator, balancing criterion and decision rationale. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR05-T03 — Proposal versus payment

Input/reveal: Worksheet says paid; payroll acknowledgement is absent.

Expected in the proposed design: Correct status to unconfirmed; reconcile the unique award reference with payroll before any retry; never infer settlement.

Record: Per-stage status, award key and responsible reconciliation role. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

Acceptance is a defensible completed card and an explanation of all three decisions after revision. A paper discussion proves neither a working system nor a safe clinical service. Publishing this lesson executes no tests.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: three scenarios

Scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps:
- 2026–2030 — Assist: An assistant flags duplicate eligibility and drafts an explanation.
- 2031–2040 — Connect: Governed reward data connects roles, approved policy and payroll outcomes.
- 2041–2050 — Adapt: A bounded planner suggests reward options under changing affordability and equity constraints; approvers remain accountable.

Competing future: facilities retain locally governed workflows and modest deterministic tools because evidence quality, cost or worker trust makes broader automation unsuitable.

Falsification question: Would assisted awards increase unexplained differences between comparable workers or shift attention toward volume alone? State what observation would make you reduce, redesign or stop assistance. Human accountability does not disappear in any scenario.
