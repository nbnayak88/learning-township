## Origins: the question that survives each era

These closed windows group process evolution for teaching; they are not universal invention or adoption dates. Adoption overlaps. Paper, spreadsheets and older systems can coexist through the present.
1970–1984 — Wage registers and manager discretion administer rewards
1985–1994 — Payroll databases support structured salary administration
1995–2004 — Job grades and budget workflows formalize compensation
2005–2014 — Integrated performance and reward planning expands governance
2015–2025 — Analytics and automated recommendations highlight bias and metric gaming
A budget-approved gross award does not prove correct net pay or a settled bank payment.

## One story: choose, reveal, revise

David Chen (OTS-P02), Group CFO, receives a synthetic scarce-skill award proposal for cloud engineers. Maya Sterling (OTS-P03), Chief Product Officer, wants to reward platform reuse quickly. Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07), Group CHRO, asks why the same engineering contribution appears in two incentive measures.
Choose before reading on: Pay the proposal, cancel all rewards, or reconcile evidence, eligibility and currency before approval?
Evidence reveal: The proposal counts a reusable component twice and mixes a local-currency award with a USD planning total. David holds the award for evidence and currency correction, checks eligibility and equity with HR, and separates approved gross award input from the later payroll and payment outcomes.
Write what changed your decision and what remains unknown. An accessible explanation and a human challenge route must be available to the affected worker. An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorization.

## Application responsibility and human authority

Compensation — Rewards owner maintains eligibility and award rules. Authorized reward approver decides after equity review.
Performance / product evidence — Product owner validates contribution and reuse definitions. Evidence owner resolves duplicates and attribution.
Finance — Finance owns budget, currency basis and reconciliation. Budget authority approves within policy.
Payroll / treasury — Payroll owns calculation; treasury owns payment settlement. Separate authorized reviewers confirm payroll and payment.
Business owners remain accountable; applications store and process the records. Discover the actual delegated approvers before implementation. Restrict client, candidate, pay and health information to its purpose and permitted viewers. Minimize replicated data and unnecessary compute rather than assuming automation is sustainable.
SAP examples in the preserved source are discussion options, not a technology selection or a claim that configuration has been validated. Start with the business need. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder; it cannot approve a consequential people decision, grant privilege or declare a payment complete.

## One build experiment: Decision Build Card

Synthetic inputs: Award AWD-500; employee IT-P500; synthetic local-currency amount 10000; USD planning line also 10000 without exchange basis; duplicate reuse evidence; budget approval pending.
Complete these fields once:
1. People and business need: who benefits, what could go wrong and why this matters in IT services or engineering.
2. Initial choice and reason: compare the choices before seeing the reveal.
3. Evidence reveal and revised choice: state what changed, and one counterexample.
4. Identity, date and source owner: identify authoritative records, effective time, provenance and uncertain facts.
5. Application responsibility: separate record keeping, orchestration, calculation, analytics and access enforcement.
6. Human approval and protected action: name the decision role, limits and worker challenge route.
7. Minimum data and permitted viewers: state purpose, retention question, client isolation and accessible communication.
8. Alternative and local variant: defend a common core with a justified regional/client variant, or explain a different design.
9. Exception owner and recovery: define hold, escalation, retry/reconciliation and confirmation conditions.
10. Measure and missing evidence: Award proposals with unique contribution evidence, valid currency basis, eligibility and approval / proposals reviewed.
11. Pre-Foundation question: Define eligibility, salary, award, currency and payment; identify one measure that could be gamed.
For each test below record input → expected → observed paper result → evidence → revision. Blank observations remain blank. A complete card can still contain a poor decision.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

OTS-HR05-T01 — Duplicate contribution
Expected proposed behavior: Deduplicate evidence before calculating proposed entitlement; retain the reason and original trace.
Evidence to record: Record contribution key, revised amount basis and reviewer.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR05-T02 — Currency mismatch
Expected proposed behavior: Hold aggregation until currency, effective exchange basis and units are explicit.
Evidence to record: Record original currency, rate source/date and corrected comparison.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR05-T03 — Approved award, payment unknown
Expected proposed behavior: Show award approval separately from payroll acceptance and bank settlement; do not resend blindly.
Evidence to record: Record handoff reference, reconciliation owner and payment query.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.
Acceptance: one complete card, three recorded tabletop observations after any revisions, and an explanation of source ownership and human authority. A breached authority or privacy boundary requires redesign. Publishing these tests does not execute them.

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. Competing future: restrictive client contracts, unreliable data and fragmented regional systems favor smaller human-led workflows. Disproof question: would the proposed assistance still improve this decision if the trusted source is unavailable, stale or contested?
