# The award is approved—but is the right amount reaching payroll?

## 1. One learner promise

In 120 minutes, design and defend one reward decision that uses the right eligibility evidence and can be reconciled from approval to payroll.

One persona-led story · One Reward Decision Build Card · Three tabletop tests. Put history notes, ownership, choices and test results on the same card. Use synthetic data; no live pay action or tenant configuration is required.

## 2. One story: Thomas follows the award

Thomas van Dijk, Group CFO — OMF-P02, reviews a proposed incentive for a synthetic worker who moved from quarry operations to a cement plant team using recovered feedstock. A spreadsheet treats the worker as belonging to the receiving plant's plan for the entire year.

Arjun Mehta, Group CHRO — OMF-P05, asks which plan applies to each service period. Nikhil Sen, Shift Supervisor and Workforce Representative — OMF-P16, asks how the worker will understand the result and request a correction.

The plant has met a margin objective, but the proposed award also uses a recycled-content result that has not been validated. Sofia de Vries, Chief Digital and Sustainability Officer — OMF-P04, asks who owns that figure and its denominator.

Choose first: release the spreadsheet result, cancel the incentive, or validate the affected inputs and service segments before authorized approval. Record your reason.

New evidence: a receiving payroll preview differs from the approved gross award used in a separate test branch. Thomas asks for a like-for-like comparison: same worker, award, currency, version and pay period—not gross award versus take-home pay.

The team separates an eligibility correction, a disputed performance input and a payroll handoff error. Each has an accountable owner. A high margin does not validate a recycled-content claim; a validated sustainability result does not establish individual eligibility.

The worker, incident, transfer, amounts and proposed approval roles below are new teaching fiction. Existing personas/designations/IDs are preserved. Before this edition, the Materials HR05 Notion body was blank. CFO/CHRO sponsorship does not establish every delegated reward or payroll approval right; real delegations require discovery.

## 3. Origins and evolution: 1970–2025

Closed teaching windows make the sequence clear. Actual practices and adoption overlapped; these are not invention dates, universal transitions or OmniForge corporate history.

| Period | Process response | Unfinished question |
|---|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Wage/grade structures and manual incentive records | Can employees understand the rule and challenge an error? |
| 1985–1994 | Market comparisons, scarce-skill premiums and differentiated incentives | Are comparisons relevant to role, location and purpose? |
| 1995–2004 | Spreadsheets and electronic HR/payroll records | Which formula, currency and service period are authoritative? |
| 2005–2014 | Connected compensation planning, budgets and approvals | Does the approved award reach payroll unchanged where intended? |
| 2015–2025 | Broader reward choices, equity review and balanced performance measures | Do rules reward genuine contribution without hiding unsupported inputs? |

Counterexample: a reward statement can look correct while payroll receives an outdated award version. Conversely, a correct gross award can lead to a different net amount after payroll processing; that difference alone is not an error.

## 4. AI-driven Rewards: 2026–2050

All rows below are design scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed functionality, licensed tenant capability or an approved OmniForge roadmap. Earlier methods may continue alongside AI.

| Period | AI-driven scenario | Materials experiment and retained human decision |
|---|---|---|
| 2026–2030 — Assist | AI checks history anomalies and drafts explanations using approved sources | Compare its service-segment check with a manual review. Authorized owners validate evidence, exceptions and awards. |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | AI helps relate transition skills, team contribution and governed sustainability evidence | Explore one explainable reward option for a changed materials role. People agree eligibility and fair, relevant criteria. |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | AI models reward options as operating models and circular-materials capabilities change | Compare affordability, worker impact and evidence quality. Accountable people retain policy, approval and correction authority. |

Competing 2050 scenario: opaque reward scores or incompatible local plans make explanations harder and amplify unfairness. Choose one bounded experiment worth trying now and what evidence would make you stop it.

## 5. Application responsibilities and human authority

Proposed learning architecture, not a confirmed installed landscape. Separate policy, validated performance, recommended amount, approved gross award, payroll processing and confirmed payment.

| Responsibility | Accountable owner / authoritative information | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Core HR — HR01 | HR data owner: employment, assignment history and relevant pay facts | A current location does not establish full-year incentive eligibility. |
| Compensation / Variable Pay — HR05 | Rewards policy/process owners: eligibility, plan versions, approved formulas, recommendations and award decisions | Compensation planning and variable incentives are related but distinct; neither alone proves payment. |
| Performance — HR03; Operations / Quality / Sustainability | Performance reviewers own approved review results; domain owners validate operational, quality and sustainability inputs | Margin, recycled content, emissions and safe conduct are different measures with their own definitions and evidence. |
| Finance | Budget/accrual owners: funding controls, reconciliation and accounting requirements | Available budget does not by itself approve an individual's award. |
| Payroll — HR07 | Payroll owner: authorized gross input, processing, gross-to-net and reconciliation; payment owners confirm settlement | Input accepted or payroll run completed does not alone prove bank settlement. |
| Worker communication and correction | Rewards/HR service owner with delegated reviewers | Explain confirmed status through an accessible route; restrict access to personal compensation data. |

SAP Variable Pay can use employee history to determine eligibility and prorate awards (R1). SAP publishing into Employee Central is a handoff capability (R2), not proof of completed payroll/payment. Verify the actual product version, configuration, mapping and approval controls.

AI may flag discrepancies or draft an explanation from permitted records; it cannot approve awards, invent evidence or initiate payment. HR11 deepens AI governance, HR09 analytics and HR12 transport/retry design.

## 6. One build card: Reward Decision

Synthetic records: worker MAT-W501, plan MAT-BP501-v1, award MAT-AWD501-v1, currency INR. The teaching performance year is 2026: service segment A is 1 January–30 June at the quarry; segment B is 1 July–31 December at the cement plant. These are scenario records, not achieved service or a prescribed SAP field mapping.

For T03 only, assume authorized gross award INR 12,000 after all applicable reviews, while the matching payroll gross input is INR 9,000. The INR 3,000 difference is a synthetic discrepancy to investigate—not a net-pay shortfall or a real employee entitlement. T01/T02 explore earlier design states; they do not prove approval occurred.

| Reward Decision Build Card | Complete once |
|---|---|
| Purpose and policy | Worker outcome, plan purpose/version, currency and relevant approved policy; identify missing rules. |
| Eligibility and time | Preserve the two service segments, applicable plan and award basis for each. State proration and rounding rules rather than inventing them. |
| Evidence and gates | Owner, period, denominator, validation status and source reference for each applicable performance/sustainability input. |
| Amounts and approval | Distinguish recommendation from approved gross award. Record authorized reviewers, budget/equity checks and exception reasons. |
| Handoff and reconciliation | Worker/award IDs, version, currency, period, gross amount, receiving status and payment-confirmation boundary. |
| Communication and correction | Plain-language explanation, accessible challenge route, privacy controls and accountable correction owner. |
| Decision and alternative | Chosen approach, rejected alternative, trade-off and evidence needed to change the decision. |
| Tests and measures | Input → expected → observed paper result → revision for T01–T03; record limitations and unresolved exceptions. |

No real payout formula, weights, base-pay change, tax treatment or negotiated entitlement is prescribed. Investigate unexplained differences using comparable roles, locations, periods and eligibility; neither every difference nor every identical amount proves fairness.

If an input is disputed, apply an explicit approved exception/review rule to the affected incentive; do not invent a penalty, cancel unrelated awards or alter base pay automatically. Reporting a concern is not itself misconduct. Required statutory/contractual treatment must be established separately for a real implementation.

## 7. Three tabletop tests

| Test | Challenge | Expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| OMF-HR05-T01 — Margin without validated evidence | Margin objective met; a recycled-content input remains unvalidated | Preserve confirmed facts and hold/escalate the affected recommendation under explicit policy. No automatic full payout or invented penalty; name the evidence and decision owners. |
| OMF-HR05-T02 — Mid-year transfer | Current plant plan has been applied to the entire year | Preserve both service segments. Validate eligibility, basis and proration per applicable plan without gaps, unintended overlap or double counting. Keep justified exceptions visible. |
| OMF-HR05-T03 — Approved versus received | Synthetic approved gross award INR 12,000; matching payroll gross input INR 9,000 | Flag INR 3,000 difference on a like-for-like basis; investigate mapping/version/input, authorize any correction and reconcile. Prevent duplicate payment and do not claim paid while confirmation is absent. |

All tests are planned tabletop exercises. Record what your paper design actually did, including failures and revisions. No publication populates the evidence slot or demonstrates an executed SAP/payroll test.

Acceptance: one completed card, three observed tabletop results after revisions and a 60-second explanation covering eligibility, validation, approval and payroll boundaries. Unapproved payment, concealed mismatch or invented consequential evidence requires redesign.

| OMF-HR05-EXP01 / EV01 | One synthetic reward decision and planned T01–T03. Evidence slot remains unfilled until learners record observations and revisions. |
| OMF-HR05-M01 | Learning measure: tests meeting expected behavior / 3, with failures/revisions recorded. No achieved enterprise pay accuracy, fairness or financial benefit is asserted. |

For later enterprise reconciliation, define matched approved-award records / approved-award records due for the selected payroll cycle, with worker/award/version/currency/period/gross-amount checks and explicit exclusions. Track payment confirmation separately. A matched record or a zero net difference across a population can still hide individual errors.

Reflection: Which missing fact changed your decision? Who owns the difference between an approved award and payroll input? Could the worker understand and challenge the explanation?
