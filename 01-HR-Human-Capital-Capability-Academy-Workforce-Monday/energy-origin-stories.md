- **Notion:** 13 readable domain pages. HR01 includes the revised sprint above the preserved research. HR02a/HR02b and HR03–HR12 contain original cases and canonical expansion material without the Origin Story sprint.
- **Identity check:** the lead persona named in each of the 13 Notion source cases is also present in its corresponding Google Student Case. The existing GitHub HR02 sprint centres on Arjun/recruiting; it does not yet incorporate Priya's connected onboarding story from the proposed review draft.

## Learning focus

These concise descriptions help you choose a sprint; they do not replace its acceptance criteria or claim that a new single-promise revision has been published.

| Theme | Decision to explore |
|---|---|
| HR01 | One trusted workforce change: identity, dates, owners and evidence. |
| HR02 | Approved demand, relevant assessment, candidate trust and an acknowledged readiness handoff. |
| HR03 | Balanced performance evidence, role context and an accountable review/appeal. |
| HR04 | Evidence-backed successor readiness and interim coverage. |
| HR05 | Approved reward decisions reconciled through the payroll handoff. |
| HR06 | Learning evidence distinguished from competence and restricted-work authority. |
| HR07 | Payroll inputs, retroactivity and confirmed payment/reconciliation. |
| HR08 | Accurate time valuation with visible rest and fatigue exceptions. |
| HR09 | One defined workforce measure with population, lineage and uncertainty. |
| HR10 | One accessible employee task with reliable status and human escalation. |
| HR11 | One bounded assistant using current evidence, authorized actions and recovery. |
| HR12 | One observable workforce event with verified business outcome and safe replay. |
