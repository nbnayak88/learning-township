## Learner use and evidence

One learner promise, one persona-led story, one build card, exactly three tabletop tests and 120 minutes. The full story, origins, source traceability, official resources and Pre-Foundation handoff remain in the canonical lesson and Google Doc. The Google Doc's existing tabs were preserved; a dedicated Slides and Build Card tab provides navigation.

The workbook has two tabs. Pale yellow cells accept learner responses; Pass or Revise has a dropdown. Record status formulas describe whether evidence was entered, not whether a design is operationally safe or a test passed. All three test records start unexecuted with observed results, revisions, reviewer questions and decisions blank. Publication executes no tabletop test and establishes no clinical, payroll or workforce outcome.

The five historical windows are educational groupings with overlapping adoption. The 2026–2050 exploration contains scenarios, a competing future and a falsification question, not predictions or product roadmaps. Health Care context and original persona/source identities are retained. No sharing permissions were changed.
