## 3. Origins: the problem survives the medium

These closed historical teaching windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniHealth's corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; paper and older systems can coexist through the present.

1970–1984 — Apprenticeship and classroom attendance carry knowledge through local mentors; attendance says little about demonstrated capability.

1985–1994 — Training registers and mandatory-course schedules track participation; access varies by role and shift.

1995–2004 — Learning systems connect curricula and certification records; role relevance and evidence validity still need review.

2005–2014 — Mobile content and internal opportunities broaden learning; protected practice time and accessible alternatives determine real access.

2015–2025 — Skills recommendations connect learning and aspirations; a recommendation or completion badge is not permission for clinical work.

Counterexample: a 100% course-completion dashboard can coexist with no protected practice time and no assessed capability.

## 4. One story, one consequential choice

Nina Fernandes (OHS-P06), Group CHRO, proposes an internal mobility opportunity for a synthetic diagnostic worker. Dr. Mei Lin (OHS-P11), Diagnostics and Imaging Director, sees a completed digital-learning module and asks whether the worker can join a new service team. The worker wants growth, but completed the module during unpaid personal time and has no observed role assessment.

Pause before the reveal. Choose treat the badge as readiness, restrict mobility indefinitely, or fund practice and assess role-specific evidence before authorizing an assignment. State who benefits, who bears the risk and what evidence you need.

Evidence reveal: the learning completion is genuine, but the role requirement changed after the module was published. Night-shift workers could not attend the assessment window, and the employee's mobility preference has not been confirmed.

Revised decision: Nina arranges protected, accessible practice and asks about the worker's aspirations. Mei Lin requests assessment against current role requirements through designated assessors; clinical privilege and facility assignment remain separate decisions. The learning record stays accurate without overstating what it proves. A learner may defend a different option if the card names its authority, evidence, trade-off and conditions for stopping. Missing evidence remains unknown; an urgent request does not manufacture approval.

## 5. Applications serve accountable people

Proposed responsibilities, subject to local discovery:

- Learning services store enrolment, completion, assessment and validity evidence under the learning owner; designated assessors judge demonstrated capability.
- Employees provide aspirations and feedback; managers own fair access to protected practice and operational coverage.
- Core HR and mobility services hold current role/opportunity context and approved assignment changes; delegated managers authorize movement.
- Professional credentialing and receiving-facility authorities own clinical scope and local readiness; a learning badge or matching score cannot replace their decisions.

Across these boundaries, HR owns employment facts; designated clinical governance owns clinical privilege decisions; IAM administers approved access. Employment, learning completion, account access and permission for clinical work are distinct states. Share only the data needed for the declared purpose; keep patient records and detailed health information out of general HR learning cards. Omar Haddad (OHS-P10), CISO and Privacy Lead, governs privacy/access assurance; jurisdiction-specific applicability remains unresolved.

SAP examples illustrate responsibilities rather than prescribe a purchase: Learning linked to Employee Central, development and opportunity services; any marketplace example remains illustrative and subject to actual availability. No feature availability, license entitlement, configuration, legal compliance or clinical conformance is established here. AI may draft a reminder or summarize permitted evidence; it cannot grant clinical privilege, make a high-impact employment decision or attest to payment or delivery.

## 7. One synthetic build experiment

Synthetic HC-P600; module HC-L600 version 1 complete; target role HC-R600 version 2; assessment absent; night-shift access constrained. No real qualification or placement.

Complete one Learning-to-Mobility Build Card with these six fields:

1. Need and scope: Make development a supported pathway into opportunity without equating attendance with competence or excluding workers by shift. Explain the capability and value-stream step before naming a tool.
2. Facts and time: preserve identifiers, source, effective/as-of time, unknowns and approved policy version. Distinguish a source fact from your assumption.
3. Decision and ownership: Map role requirement → learning → protected practice → assessed evidence → employee preference → authorized opportunity, naming each owner. Record one alternative, its trade-off and the evidence that could favour it.
4. Handoff and recovery: Flag stale learning-to-role mappings; the learning owner and assessor define a bridging assessment. Escalate unavailable practice time to the manager rather than shifting the burden to unpaid work. Name the accountable exception role, accessible human route and condition for reconciliation.
5. Local variant and data: choose one facility/language/role variation; justify minimum fields, purpose, owner, retention decision and viewers. Retain a non-digital route.
6. Evidence and learning: record T01–T03 input → expected → observed paper result → revision → reviewer question on this same card. Leave results unfilled until observed.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OHS-HR06-T01 — Completion versus capability

Input/reveal: Module complete; observed assessment absent.

Expected in the proposed design: Completion remains true; readiness stays unresolved; assign authorized practice/assessment rather than clinical permission.

Record: Separate states, assessor and next permitted step. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR06-T02 — Stale requirement

Input/reveal: Role version 2 needs evidence absent from module version 1.

Expected in the proposed design: Preserve the old completion and identify the specific gap; no blanket recertification claim or automatic readiness.

Record: Version mapping, bridging question and evidence validity. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

### OHS-HR06-T03 — Unequal access

Input/reveal: Night-shift worker cannot attend the only assessment slot.

Expected in the proposed design: Provide a feasible accessible alternative and protected time; no lower capability judgment solely from scheduling barriers.

Record: Coverage owner, worker preference and revised opportunity plan. Observed result and pass/revise remain unfilled until learners perform the tabletop.

Acceptance is a defensible completed card and an explanation of all three decisions after revision. A paper discussion proves neither a working system nor a safe clinical service. Publishing this lesson executes no tests.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: three scenarios

Scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps:
- 2026–2030 — Assist: An assistant identifies missing or stale learning evidence.
- 2031–2040 — Connect: Governed skills records connect practice, assessment and employee-chosen opportunities.
- 2041–2050 — Adapt: A bounded planner proposes individualized development routes as roles change, subject to assessor and worker review.

Competing future: facilities retain locally governed workflows and modest deterministic tools because evidence quality, cost or worker trust makes broader automation unsuitable.

Falsification question: Would recommendation-led learning increase completion counts while reducing equitable access to assessed mobility opportunities? State what observation would make you reduce, redesign or stop assistance. Human accountability does not disappear in any scenario.
