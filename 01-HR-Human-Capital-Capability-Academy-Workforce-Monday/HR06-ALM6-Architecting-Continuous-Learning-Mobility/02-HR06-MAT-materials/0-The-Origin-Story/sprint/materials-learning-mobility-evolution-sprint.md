# The course is complete. The opportunity still needs evidence.

Level 0 → Pre-Foundation · Sprint · 120 minutes · Edition 2026-10-31 · Planning reference: HUB-ORIGIN-HR06-MAT-sprint (not an LMS ID).

## 1. One learner promise

In 120 minutes, design and defend one learning-to-mobility pathway that distinguishes completion, demonstrated competence and authorized assignment.

One persona-led story · One Learning-to-Mobility Build Card · Three tabletop tests. Keep timeline notes, owners, decisions and observed paper results on the same card. No tenant configuration or live industrial work is required.

## 2. One story: Nikhil opens a pathway

Nikhil Sen, Shift Supervisor and Workforce Representative — OMF-P16, supports a synthetic quarry-maintenance technician who wants a development assignment with a cement plant's recovered-materials team.

The technician has completed an online introduction. A matching assistant recommends the assignment and marks the worker as ready. Elena Petrova, Cement Plant Director — OMF-P07, asks for the evidence behind that claim.

The learning record proves the activity was completed; it contains no observed practical assessment. Arjun Mehta, Group CHRO — OMF-P05, asks how the worker can demonstrate capability without losing access to development.

Choose first: treat the course as sufficient, reject the worker, or retain the opportunity while defining the missing evidence and a supervised development route. Write your reason before reading further.

New evidence: the worker is on night shift, has no personal smartphone and cannot attend the only proposed daytime assessment. The receiving team also sees an old credential status from a delayed feed.

Nikhil proposes an accessible assessment window, agreed release/coverage and a qualified assessor. The team verifies current evidence and separates approval to join an assessed development activity from permission for restricted work. No unsafe task is used as an informal audition.

The worker can decline or revise the development choice. A recommendation is neither an appointment nor permission to change employment, pay or access. If a permanent move follows, the relevant owners must complete their own approved processes.

The worker, course, assignment, dialogue and incidents are new teaching fiction using existing persona identities. Source contractor numbers and device access remain unknown; the no-smartphone condition applies only to this synthetic worker. HR06's existing Notion body was blank before this edition.

## 3. Origins and evolution: 1970–2025

Closed teaching windows distinguish the periods. Actual practices and adoption overlapped; these are not invention dates, universal transitions or OmniForge corporate history.

| Period | Process response | Unfinished question |
|---|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Apprenticeship, classroom instruction and supervised practice | Is assessment consistent across supervisors and shifts? |
| 1985–1994 | Training matrices and structured certification records | Does attendance demonstrate ability? |
| 1995–2004 | Computer-based content and electronic training records | Can learners transfer knowledge into the actual work context? |
| 2005–2014 | LMS-led blended learning and assignment tracking | Are completion, competence and credential validity being confused? |
| 2015–2025 | Digital academies, mentoring and internal development opportunities | Who can access practice and mobility, beyond those already visible? |

Counterexample: a perfect course-completion dashboard can coexist with inaccessible assessments and unverified practical capability. Learning exists to support useful growth, not just generate completion records.

## 5. Application responsibilities and human authority

Proposed learning architecture, not a verified deployed landscape. A content author, assessor, opportunity owner and work authorizer have different responsibilities, even if one person holds several delegated roles.

| Responsibility | Accountable owner / authoritative evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| LMS; SAP Learning as an example | Learning/content owners: approved item version, assignment, completion and assessment records | A completion record alone does not establish current task competence or work permission. |
| Practical assessment | Qualified assessor and role/technical owner: criteria, observed evidence, scope and result | Assess against approved criteria; observer access in an application does not establish professional qualification. |
| Opportunity / mobility service | Opportunity owner: description, prerequisites, application and participation status; worker owns stated interests | Matching or accepting interest does not automatically appoint, transfer or authorize restricted work. |
| Core HR — HR01; Recruiting — HR02 | HR owns employment/role facts; Recruiting governs internal selection where applicable | Update authoritative records only through their approved processes. |
| Site safety and IAM | Designated site authorities: work authorization; Security: accounts/access | A valid credential or working badge does not replace task-specific approval. |
| Managers / development / time owners | Managers agree availability, cover and release; HR04 owns longer-term development links; HR08 governs time records | Do not assume daytime availability or infer time/pay changes from a learning recommendation. |

SAP Learning history records learning activities (R1); task checklists support observed evaluation under defined standards (R2). Neither product feature alone verifies the site-specific validity, assessor qualification or authorization required by this scenario.

SAP Opportunity Marketplace can surface assignments, learning, jobs and mentoring from distinct sources (R3). Recommendation and source ownership must remain separate. Confirm actual licensed/configured behavior before implementation.

AI may suggest content or opportunities and flag missing evidence. Expert owners review generated learning material before release; authorized assessors and site authorities retain competence/work decisions. HR11 deepens AI controls and HR12 detailed exchanges.

## 6. One build card: Learning-to-Mobility

Synthetic worker MAT-W601 completed item MAT-L601-v1, “Recovered-feedstock basics.” Opportunity MAT-OP601 is an assessed development assignment; assessment record MAT-AS601 is initially missing. These are teaching labels, not verified catalogue entries or prescribed SAP field mappings.

Define a safe simulation or separately approved supervised learning setting. This sprint designs evidence and decisions; it does not teach industrial procedures or qualify anyone for plant work.

| Card field | Record once |
|---|---|
| Purpose and choice | Worker's learning goal, desired opportunity and ability to revise/decline the pathway. |
| Required capability | Role/task context, relevant prerequisites, approved content version and assessment criteria; identify unknowns. |
| Learning evidence | What was completed, when, source/issuer and what the record does—and does not—prove. |
| Assessment and validity | Qualified assessor, observed evidence, scope, validity/expiry where applicable, correction owner and next review. |
| Opportunity and readiness | Owner, participation prerequisites, availability, release/coverage and outstanding conditions. |
| Authorization and access | Separate task/site approval, identity/access decision and authoritative evidence freshness; no inferred permission. |
| Inclusion and alternative | Accessible channel, shift-compatible option, shared-device privacy and a human correction route. |
| Decision and tests | Chosen option, alternative, trade-off; T01–T03 expected → observed paper result → revision. |

Suggested progression: explore → agree pathway → learn/practice → assess → verify current evidence → authorize applicable participation/work → confirm outcome. These are teaching stages, not one shared product status. Completion, assessment, opportunity and authorization retain their own states.

When freshness cannot be established, show unknown/pending status with a named owner. Use the site's approved fallback for the affected decision; missing connectivity creates no new work authority. Keep learning opportunities open where safely feasible.

## 7. Three tabletop tests

| Test | Challenge | Expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| OMF-HR06-T01 — Completion without practice | Course is complete; practical assessment evidence is missing | Preserve completion, avoid claiming verified competence and identify a qualified assessment/development step. No restricted-work permission follows. |
| OMF-HR06-T02 — Expired or stale evidence | A required credential is expired, or the receiving feed cannot establish current validity | Expose the exact validity/freshness issue, notify its owner and keep the affected restricted action held under approved policy. Renewal must not silently create site authorization. |
| OMF-HR06-T03 — Recommendation without a viable assignment | Worker is matched but release/coverage, accessible assessment and receiving acceptance are not agreed | Retain a proposed opportunity status, arrange an accessible alternative with the worker/managers and confirm prerequisites. Do not report participation started or employment changed. |

Record actual paper behavior, failures and revisions. These tests remain planned until performed; publication supplies no executed tenant, credential or plant evidence.

Acceptance: complete one card, record all three observed tabletop results after revision and explain the decision in 60 seconds. Show one feasible next learning step and the human authority boundaries. Unsupported competence, unauthorized work or invented participation requires redesign.
