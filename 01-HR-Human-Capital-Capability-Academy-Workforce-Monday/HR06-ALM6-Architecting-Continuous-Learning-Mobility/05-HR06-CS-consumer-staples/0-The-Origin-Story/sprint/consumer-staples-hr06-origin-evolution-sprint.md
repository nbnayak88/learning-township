## 3. Origins and process evolution

These closed windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniDaily corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present. Reconstruct each as need → response → unresolved problem.

1970–1984 — Apprenticeship and local demonstrations transmit practice; evidence can depend on memory.

1985–1994 — Training matrices organize courses by role; attendance may substitute for capability.

1995–2004 — Learning systems record histories; a completion date does not show practical proficiency.

2005–2014 — Blended learning and development plans connect experience; assessment validity and access remain uneven.

2015–2025 — Role/skills ecosystems broaden opportunity; current evidence and authorized assignment still need explicit owners.

## 5. Responsibilities and human decision rights

HR/role owners define capability needs; learning services own assignments, content versions and history; qualified assessors own practical assessment evidence. Operations/quality owns local duty authorization; core HR owns accepted role moves; security owns access. Workers can challenge inaccurate history and seek an alternative practice route. SAP Learning is illustrative; the proposed architecture does not verify assessment or a job placement.

These are proposed educational responsibilities. Establish the business rule, source of truth and accountable human before choosing technology. SAP examples illustrate a capability and are not verified deployments, licensing claims or product roadmaps. A worker must have an accessible human route to question incorrect data or a decision. HR status, learning completion, work authorization and quality/batch release are distinct decisions.

## 7. One build card: Learning-to-Mobility Decision Card

Synthetic experiment: Use SYN-ROLE606, SYN-LEARNER606 and three synthetic evidence labels: content complete, practical assessment absent, local duty authorization pending. They are states in one experiment, not three build cards.

Fill nine fields on one page: (1) purpose/person affected; (2) current/proposed state and evidence; (3) identifiers and definitions; (4) business time/version; (5) application/data owners; (6) human approval/appeal; (7) chosen option versus alternative and trade-off; (8) exception, fallback and automation boundary; (9) three observed test responses and revision.

Design constraint: Store evidence scope, assessor, version and validity. Preserve prior learning without treating every old certificate as current qualification; never infer food-safety approval from an LMS checkbox.

All SYN identifiers, episode details and injected evidence are new teaching data. Country rules, collective agreements, qualifications, worker/device populations and local approvals remain unknown unless explicitly sourced. Record each unknown with an owner and discovery question. Do not invent legal rules or calculate unsupported operational benefits.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### ODG-TOS-HR06-T01 — Completion versus competence

Reveal/input: The learner completes a module but lacks the practical assessment.

Expected design response: Record completion accurately; leave qualification unproven and route assessment to its owner.

### ODG-TOS-HR06-T02 — Different process

Reveal/input: Prior evidence covers another process/version.

Expected design response: Compare scope and gap; recognize transferable evidence without assuming full equivalence.

### ODG-TOS-HR06-T03 — Frontline access

Reveal/input: Learning access is interrupted before an assessment submission.

Expected design response: Provide an approved assisted/offline route with reconciliation; do not fabricate completion or deny opportunity solely because connectivity failed.

For each test record input → expected response → actual paper response → evidence note → pass/revise. These are proposed acceptance criteria. The evidence slot starts unfilled: publication runs none of the tests and proves no operational result. If a protected human decision can be bypassed, revise the design even if every card field is filled.

## 9. Traceability and measures

ODG-TOS-HR06-REQ01 — Trace one role capability gap to learning/practice and appropriate evidence. Derived from the supporting HR source mandate and ODG-M18; assessed by T01.

ODG-TOS-HR06-REQ02 — Keep evidence validity, accessible learning and duty authorization separate. Derived from the source governance mandate and ODG-M19; assessed by T02–T03.

ODG-TOS-HR06-ADR01 — Prefer a capability-led path with scoped recognition of prior evidence over a catalog-only assignment; compare assessment effort and readiness risk. This proposed educational decision can be rejected with evidence; record the alternative and consequence.

ODG-TOS-HR06-EXP01 — The one synthetic build card extends ODG-M19's delivery-readiness discipline; T01–T03 do not execute the larger implementation case.

ODG-TOS-HR06-M01 — Mandatory card fields completed / 9 × 100: learning completeness only. ODG-TOS-HR06-M02 — Record whether T02/T03 preserve scoped evidence and a usable alternative route. Record actual paper observations rather than assuming success.

ODG-TOS-HR06-EV01 — Unfilled evidence slot for test observations, revisions and reviewer questions. Existing case success measures are intended outcomes; no baseline or target attainment follows from publishing this edition.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: scenarios, not predictions

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. Each needs evidence, approved purpose, accountable ownership and proportionate controls.

2026–2030 — Assist: Assistance could recommend practice while humans judge evidence.

2031–2040 — Connect: Role, learning and mobility services could exchange scoped qualification evidence.

2041–2050 — Adapt: Learning paths could adapt to work changes with learner choice and reassessment authority.

Competing future: Local peer practice and verified assessor observation may outperform recommendation-heavy platforms where operational context dominates.

Disconfirmation question: Would the proposed path exclude experienced workers or increase completion without improving demonstrated capability? Name evidence that would stop or change the hypothesis.
