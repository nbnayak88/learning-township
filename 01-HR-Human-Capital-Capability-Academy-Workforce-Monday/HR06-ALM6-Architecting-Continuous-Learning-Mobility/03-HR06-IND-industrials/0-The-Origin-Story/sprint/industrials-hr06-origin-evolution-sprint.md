## 2. Read the enterprise and sources accurately

This edition adds a bounded synthetic episode; it does not assert a deployment.

## 3. Origins: five closed historical teaching windows

These windows group teaching ideas, not universal invention dates or OmniWorks corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present. Each generation changes the medium without automatically resolving ownership, evidence or authority.

1970–1984 — Apprenticeship and local instruction support craft learning; undocumented assessment makes capability hard to transfer.

1985–1994 — Training departments and paper certificates organize courses; attendance does not prove task competence.

1995–2004 — LMS records standardize assignment and completion; a completed item may still omit observed practice.

2005–2014 — Blended learning and competence frameworks connect content to roles; records can expire or apply to the wrong equipment.

2015–2025 — Mobile learning and opportunity matching widen access; a skills recommendation still requires current evidence and an authorized assignment.

Recall prompt: reconstruct the five responses as problem → process/application response → unfinished problem. Which old failure could survive in the newest system?

## 4. One persona-led dilemma

### Scene 1

Kabir asks Hannah what would help her maintain new automated equipment. She wants a real path from learning to a meaningful opportunity, not another list of courses.

### Scene 2

Yuki shows the digital-manufacturing pathway. Reveal: the online course is complete, but Hannah's practical assessment was never recorded. Diego cannot infer safe work from the course flag.

### Scene 3

Sarah asks whether the pathway's value is proven by completion rates. Kabir separates participation, demonstrated competence, mobility and operating impact. A future assessment plan is not evidence of improved reliability.

### Scene 4

Kabir revises one card: provide supervised practice, an authorized assessor, equipment/task scope and a fair opportunity review. The receiving manager can discuss development without releasing Hannah to restricted maintenance.

Decision pause: Prioritize more courses, verified role proficiency, or an open internal opportunity pathway. Which dependency must be resolved before mobility?

## 5. Responsibilities, ownership and human authority

Learning service (learning owner): assigned content, version, completion and assessed evidence; SAP Learning is an example, not the only solution.

Skills/assessment record (qualified assessor and skill owner): observed performance, task/equipment scope, validity and limitations.

HR core/opportunity service (HR and opportunity owners): current role and available assignments; a match is a suggestion, not an appointment.

EAM/operating service (maintenance owner): work orders and equipment context. Site safety/authorized operations owner decides work release; learning cannot grant it.

Kabir owns pathway design; workers participate in goals and correction of skill records; authorized managers/assessors make development and assignment decisions.

Design boundary: Completion, assessed competence, certificate currency, opportunity eligibility and task authorization remain separate claims. Proposed responsibilities are learning architecture, not proof that the fictional enterprise already operates this design. Select technology only after the business rule and accountable owner are clear. SAP examples illustrate capabilities, not mandatory product choices.

## 7. One build card: Learning-to-Mobility Build Card

Synthetic experiment: SYN-L601 is an online completion for Hannah's synthetic profile SYN-W601. SYN-A601 practical assessment is absent; SYN-OP601 is a maintenance opportunity on different equipment. Build one development pathway without real equipment operation.

Fill these nine fields on one page: purpose and affected person; current/proposed state and source evidence; identity/object definitions; business date/time and version; application/data owners; human approval and appeal route; chosen option versus alternative with trade-off; exception/fallback and automation boundary; three test observations and revision.

Minimum design reasoning: Reference the exact learning/assessment version and relevant equipment scope. Preserve a supervised development route and accessible learning time; a missing record may require verification rather than repeating all learning by default.

Do not invent country rules, safety thresholds, collective-agreement terms, live data or missing approvals. Record unknowns with an owner and discovery question. Support an accessible human route for the worker. Consider fatigue, learning opportunity, avoidable travel/rework or resource use where the dilemma provides evidence; do not infer environmental savings.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OW-TOS-HR06-T01 — Missing assessment

Reveal/input: Online completion is present but practical evidence is missing.

Expected design response: Keep competence unverified, identify an authorized assessor and hold restricted work while offering supervised development.

### OW-TOS-HR06-T02 — Scope counterexample

Reveal/input: A current certificate covers another equipment family.

Expected design response: Do not transfer competence automatically; seek a scoped assessment or validated equivalence from the responsible authority.

### OW-TOS-HR06-T03 — Mobility handoff

Reveal/input: The opportunity matcher marks Hannah eligible but the receiving authorization service is unavailable.

Expected design response: Keep the opportunity suggestion and readiness status separate, expose the missing decision and use an approved human route; do not claim placement.

For each test, record input → expected response → actual observed paper response → evidence note → pass/revise. Expected responses are proposed acceptance criteria, not executed results. Publication executes none of these tabletop tests. A bypass of the protected decision requires redesign, even if the card is complete.

## 9. Traceability and honest measures

OW-TOS-HR06-REQ01 — Connect one learning pathway to scoped, current assessment evidence. Source connection: supporting HR case mandate and OW-M18. Assessed by T01; card must identify the authoritative fact and owner.

OW-TOS-HR06-REQ02 — Keep mobility recommendations separate from appointment and restricted-work authority. Source connection: HR case governance/scenario injection and OW-M17/OW-M19. Assessed by T02–T03; card records exception, authority and recovery condition.

OW-TOS-HR06-ADR01 — Prefer a blended evidence-based pathway over completion-only assignment; compare supervised practice cost with the risk of false competence. Compare the stated alternative and trade-off; this is a proposed educational decision that learners may reject with evidence.

OW-TOS-HR06-EXP01 — this synthetic card and T01–T03 extend OW-M19's traceable-pilot discipline. They do not complete the wider case's tests or rollout.

OW-TOS-HR06-M01 — completed mandatory card fields / 9 × 100; learning completeness only. OW-TOS-HR06-M02 — Record whether the card distinguishes all five claims and T01–T03 preserve scope/authorization; no learning-impact result is measured. No baseline, target attainment or operating benefit is inferred from an empty result.

OW-TOS-HR06-EV01 — initially unfilled evidence slot for observed tabletop results, revisions and reviewer questions. Existing case success metrics remain separate planned outcomes.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: scenarios, not predictions

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. All require approved purpose, evidence, worker participation, accountable ownership and proportionate controls.

2026–2030 — Assist: Assistance could suggest practice and explain missing evidence while respecting worker aspirations.

2031–2040 — Connect: Connected pathways could link assessment evidence to internal opportunities with explicit consent/purpose and owners.

2041–2050 — Adapt: Adaptive learning pods could propose new practice as equipment evolves; authorized assessment and worker choice remain central.

Competing future: Human-led communities of practice with portable but carefully scoped evidence may outperform automated opportunity matching.

Disconfirmation question: Would the pathway increase course completions without improving observed capability or equitable opportunity? Record the evidence that would make you stop or change the hypothesis.
