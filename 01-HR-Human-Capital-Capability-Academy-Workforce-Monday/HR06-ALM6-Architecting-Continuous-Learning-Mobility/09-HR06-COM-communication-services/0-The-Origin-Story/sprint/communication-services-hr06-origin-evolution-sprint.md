## The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one learning-to-assignment decision using current evidence of capability.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, wants experienced legacy-network staff to move into fiber roles. Arjun Mehta (OCN-P04), Chief Network Officer, receives a course completion for a technician needed on tomorrow's field assignment.

**Choose first:** Assign immediately, require another identical course, or assess current capability and task permission?

**Evidence reveal:** The learning record is genuine, but it covers classroom concepts only. The task requires observed practice and local authorization. Nadia arranges a supervised practice assignment and a fair reassessment route.

**Counterexample:** Completion measures participation; independent evidence is needed for proficiency and restricted work.

OmniConnect is a fictional communication-services enterprise. The source stipulates 62,000 employees across 28 countries, spanning mobile and 5G, fiber, media and streaming, enterprise networks, and advertising. No new site or workforce counts are inferred. Existing source KPIs and financial figures are fictional baselines, proposals or targets, with no achieved result asserted.

## Origins and responsibility

These closed historical windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniConnect corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present.

| Teaching window | Process evolution |
|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Apprenticeship and supervisor demonstrations |
| 1985–1994 | Training calendars and qualification registers |
| 1995–2004 | Learning systems track curricula and completion |
| 2005–2014 | Mobile learning joins mentoring and project experience |
| 2015–2025 | Skills recommendations connect learning and mobility |

| Application responsibility | Accountable owner | Data and human boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Learning system | Learning owner | Completion, assessment, credential validity and evidence |
| Mobility and core HR | Talent owner and HR steward | Opportunity matching and approved assignment |
| Field operations and IAM | Operational authority and security | Task release and access under current prerequisites |

SuccessFactors Learning can hold learning records; task authorization belongs to operations.

A source-system record, receiving-system acknowledgment and human permission answer different questions. Provide a private, accessible correction or appeal route. Avoid personal-data joins with subscriber or audience data unless a legitimate purpose, access boundary and minimum data are established.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test ID | Focus | Evidence reveal | Expected result in the proposed design |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCN-XL-HR06-T01 | Course only | Completion exists without observed practice. | Hold restricted work, arrange supervised practice and record assessment needs. |
| OCN-XL-HR06-T02 | Expired evidence | Previously valid credential has expired. | Seek renewed evidence or an authorized alternative task; never silently extend validity. |
| OCN-XL-HR06-T03 | Unfair recommendation | Matching favors office-visible staff over equally capable field staff. | Review relevant evidence and accessible application routes before deciding. |

Record initial decision → expected result → observed paper result → evidence → revision. All observed-result fields start empty. Acceptance requires a reasoned card and all three reviewed results after revisions. A material authority or privacy bypass requires redesign. Publishing the exercise does not execute it or prove operational outcomes.

## AI driven curiosity and the next chapter

| Scenario window | Exploration |
|---|---|
| 2026–2030 — Assist | Suggest relevant practice from skill gaps |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | Connect learning, projects and mobility evidence |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | Adapt pathways using demonstrated capability |

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. The time bands support curiosity about possible designs.

**Competing future:** fragmented rules, weak interoperability or low trust keep local expert judgment and simpler manual workflows more effective than connected AI. Compare that path with the scenario before choosing an experiment.

**Question that could disprove your hypothesis:** Would time-to-proficiency remain unchanged despite higher course completion?

**Reflect:** What evidence changed your first choice? Who can challenge the decision? What would make the alternative better?
