## 4. One persona-led story

Priya Rao (OCF-P03), Chief Customer and Retail Officer, wants a synthetic branch specialist to move into advice. Nadia Fernandes (OCF-P07), Group CHRO, sees all assigned courses completed. Elena Fischer (OCF-P05), Chief Compliance and Conduct Officer, asks for current role evidence and supervised-practice assessment.

Choose before revealing: Treat the course certificate as clearance, deny the career move, or create a supported pathway while holding independent advice? Record your first reason on the card.

Evidence reveal: The course concerns a different product scope, and practical assessment is still pending. Nadia preserves the achievement but changes the pathway. Priya schedules appropriate supervised practice; Elena routes role-specific requirements to the designated authority.

Revise your decision: Learning opens opportunities when evidence stays specific and current. A recommendation, course completion or badge does not prove transferable competence or grant regulated duties. Preserve an accessible human route to challenge errors. Financial service continuity matters, but an urgent cut-off does not create authority. Customer KYC and employee readiness are different processes; do not copy customer identity checks into HR without purpose and policy review.

## 5. Application responsibilities and human authority

Frame the business need first; applications implement the chosen operating model. The following is a proposed teaching architecture. Confirm named delegates, country policy, legal basis and actual product scope before implementation.

| Application responsibility | Accountable data owner | Human decision right |
|---|---|---|
| Learning; SAP Learning example | Learning owner maintains content scope, version, completion and assessment records | Designated assessors validate practical competence; content owner reviews currency. |
| Mobility and opportunities | Talent owner maintains role requirements and opportunity record | Employee and hiring manager agree the move under delegated HR approval. |
| Role authorization / access | Compliance steward owns applicable credential evidence; Security executes access | Designated role authority releases independent duties after required evidence. |

Core HR owns workforce facts; domain owners own performance, learning, rewards, time or payroll facts as applicable. IAM executes access policy; integration transports and reconciles states; analytics reads authorized data and does not become its owner. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder, but cannot approve consequential employment, regulated duties, payments or access. An interface acknowledgment is not proof of the business outcome. SAP examples illustrate responsibilities, not guaranteed features or universal localization.

## 7. One Decision Build Card

Synthetic input: FIN-P600 completed course LEARN600 for product scope A; proposed role requires scope B; practical assessment pending; employee wants the move; fictional data only.

Keep all work in this one card. Capture: business problem; initial and revised choice with reason; entity/identity/date semantics; authoritative source and accountable owner; human approval boundary; one justified local variant with purpose/minimum data/viewers; known versus unknown downstream state; exception owner and approved fallback; all three test observations and evidence references. Missing evidence stays blank or unknown, never silently becomes approval.

Compare one alternative: a manual controlled handoff may be preferable to automation when exception ownership or recovery is absent. State the evidence needed to change your mind. Avoid collecting real screening, health, payroll, customer or conduct records in this learning card.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

| Test | Input / reveal | Expected proposed behavior | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCF-HR06-T01 — Wrong learning scope | Completed item covers a different product from the receiving role. | Retain achievement but do not treat it as equivalent; assess the gap. | Content version, scope, equivalence decision and assessor. |
| OCF-HR06-T02 — Expired credential | A credential was valid at course completion but is no longer current. | Route renewal/equivalence review; hold affected duties without erasing learning history. | Validity dates, role conditions and human review. |
| OCF-HR06-T03 — Restricted opportunity recommendation | Matching engine exposes a confidential role or invents proficiency. | Enforce permitted visibility; label inference; require employee and manager confirmation. | Permission boundary, evidence source and corrected recommendation. |

For each test, record input → expected → observed → revision/evidence → Pass, Revise or Blocked. All observed results are initially unfilled. Publication does not execute any tabletop, system test, approval or operational transaction. Acceptance requires a defensible card and all three observed tabletop results with revisions; bypassing a protected human decision requires redesign.

Scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps.
