## 3. Origins and process evolution

The five closed windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniVibe's corporate biography (the source gives its founding as 1992). Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present. Ask what problem each response solved and what it left unresolved.

1970–1984 — Manual headcount returns support planning; counting definitions can differ.

1985–1994 — Spreadsheets and standardized reports improve aggregation; different denominators can still appear comparable.

1995–2004 — HR reporting and data warehouses consolidate records; duplicate employments and stale joins can distort counts.

2005–2014 — Dashboards and self-service analytics speed questions; privacy and causal interpretation still need governance.

2015–2025 — Cloud analytics and predictive tools add patterns; lineage, small-cohort protection and uncertainty remain critical.

Recall: reconstruct problem → process/application response → unfinished problem. Which old failure could survive in a modern system?

## 4. One persona-led dilemma

Lead: Ravi Kapoor, Group CHRO (OVC-P04). Counterpart: Jonas Lim, Integration and HR Data Lead (OVC-P12). These established personas are fictional. The following incident, record IDs and reveals are new synthetic teaching additions.

Scene 1 — Ravi wants staffing insight for an illustrative store service pilot. Two dashboards disagree: one counts people, another assignments. The enterprise's source-stated 72,000 employees is not a ready-made denominator for every store or franchise measure.

Scene 2 — Choose the largest count, merge everything under one label or define a decision-specific metric with documented boundaries. Reveal: two assignments belong to one person, and some roster rows may belong to a franchise employer. Jonas will not silently classify them.

Scene 3 — Farah Ahmed, CISO and Privacy Lead (OVC-P13), reveals that a proposed drill-down isolates a tiny team. Ravi revises access and aggregation. A correlation between training and sales cannot establish that training caused the result; staffing assumptions must remain uncertain until tested.

Decision pause: record your first choice before the reveal, then revise it. Name one fact that would make the alternative better.

## 5. Responsibilities and decision rights

HR core owners govern person/employment facts; Time/Operations owns approved rosters and workload context; Learning owns assessment/completion evidence. Analytics stewards own metric definitions, denominators, period and lineage, not source truth. Security/privacy owners approve access and disclosure rules. Ravi decides staffing with accountable operational owners; Jonas traces data contracts. SAP People Analytics is the source example; a dashboard does not approve staffing or prove causality.

Frame the business rule and accountable owner before selecting technology. Source proposals do not authorize data sharing, sensitive transactions or autonomous decisions. Keep an accessible human exception and worker challenge route.

## 7. One build card — Workforce Insight Decision Card

Synthetic experiment (OVC-TOS-HR09-EXP01): Synthetic snapshot SYN-D900 has 12 assignment rows, 10 distinct person IDs, two unverified franchise rows and one three-person subgroup. Define people count separately from assignments and unknown employer inclusion. Construct a proposed staffing insight with uncertainty; no enterprise forecast or privacy threshold is invented.

Fill nine fields on one page: purpose/person affected; current and proposed state with source evidence; object/identity definitions; business date/time and version; application and data owners; human approval and challenge route; chosen option versus alternative and trade-off; exception/fallback and automation boundary; three test observations and your revision.

Unknown country, brand, franchise, device, policy and authority conditions need an owner and discovery question. Use no real personal data. Consider learning access, workload, service continuity and avoidable rework where evidence supports them; do not infer financial or environmental savings.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### OVC-TOS-HR09-T01 — Reconcile the denominator

Reveal/input: People, employment and assignment totals disagree.

Expected design response: Show distinct definitions, as-of date, source lineage and unknown employer treatment; do not force equality by relabeling.

### OVC-TOS-HR09-T02 — Small cohort and access

Reveal/input: A manager asks for identifiable drill-down on the three-person subgroup.

Expected design response: Apply a defined disclosure rule only after owner approval; with an unknown rule, withhold identifiable output and route review rather than invent a universal threshold.

### OVC-TOS-HR09-T03 — Correlation and scenario risk

Reveal/input: Higher course completion coincides with sales growth; forecast assumes the relationship will continue.

Expected design response: State confounders and uncertainty, compare alternative workload assumptions and request evidence before a causal staffing or investment decision.

For each, record input → expected response → actual observed paper response → evidence note → pass/revise. These expected responses are proposed acceptance criteria. Publication executes none of the tests; all observation slots start unfilled. A bypass of a protected decision calls for redesign, even if the card is complete.

OVC-TOS-HR09-REQ02 — Keep privacy, uncertainty and accountable staffing judgment explicit. Source connection: OVC-M17–OVC-M19. Acceptance: T02–T03; identify the protected decision, exception and recovery condition.

OVC-TOS-HR09-ADR01 — Proposed educational choice: Decision-specific governed metrics with separate unknown categories rather than a single merged headcount or unexplained dashboard score. Learners may reject it with evidence; it is not a client-approved architecture decision.

OVC-TOS-HR09-EXP01 — The single card experiment extends the existing case's bounded-pilot discipline; T01–T03 do not complete the wider case's experiments or implementation tests.

OVC-TOS-HR09-M01 — Mandatory card fields completed / 9 × 100: learning completeness only. OVC-TOS-HR09-M02 — Record whether each proposed acceptance criterion survives its evidence reveal; record observed paper results separately from expectations. Real baselines and targets remain TBD.

## 10. AI-driven exploration — scenarios, not predictions

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. Each requires approved purpose, evidence, worker participation, proportionate safeguards and accountable human decisions.

2026–2030 — Assist: Explain metric definitions and flag inconsistent denominators.

2031–2040 — Connect: Connect governed HR, learning and service evidence without unjustified personal disclosure.

2041–2050 — Adapt: Suggest staffing scenarios as demand changes; humans assess uncertainty, fairness and proposed action.

Competing future: A small set of agreed descriptive measures may outperform predictive models when definitions and representative data remain weak.

Disconfirmation question: Would the model improve out-of-sample staffing decisions, or simply fit historical sales while hiding uncertainty and privacy harm? State the observation that would make you stop, revise or prefer the competing future.
