# The workforce count rose. Did kiln-shift capacity?

SuccessLabs Academy · GICS02 Materials · OmniForge Materials Ltd. · HR09 — Workforce Intelligence · Level 0 → Pre-Foundation

IIL — Industry Immersion Lab · Sprint · 120 minutes · Edition 2026-10-31 · Planning reference: HUB-ORIGIN-HR09-MAT-sprint

Mission: Architecting Experiences for a Better World. Fictional educational case; a published lesson is not enrollment, certification or an implemented enterprise solution.

## 1. One learner promise

Turn conflicting workforce counts into one explainable capacity-planning decision with a defined population, traceable evidence and an accountable human owner.

One deliverable: a Workforce Decision Build Card. Master five distinctions: people versus employments; headcount versus FTE; current facts versus planning assumptions; association versus causation; permission to build a report versus permission to view its data.

Business question first: should the plant investigate a staffing shortfall, redeployment or training before requesting additional recruitment? A higher headcount cannot answer this without skills, working-time and demand evidence.

## 2. Origins and process evolution — 1970–2025

Workforce intelligence grew from personnel counts and payroll returns into reporting, analysis and conditional planning. A metric is a calculation with a purpose, unit, population, time boundary and owner. A dashboard makes a measure visible; it does not make the definition valid or establish causation.

The closed windows below organize learning, not invention dates or a universal chronology. Adoption overlaps: paper, spreadsheets, warehouses and cloud analytics coexist through the present. This history is a teaching synthesis, not OmniForge's corporate biography.

| Teaching window | Process change | Materials example and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1970–1984 | Manual staffing returns and personnel/payroll summaries. | A quarry counts people on the books; the count does not show who can cover the next shift. |
| 1985–1994 | Spreadsheets and periodic management reports. | Plants can compare totals, but local files use different dates and contractor definitions. |
| 1995–2004 | Warehouses and shared reporting definitions. | Historical comparisons improve; joins can double-count concurrent employments or transfers. |
| 2005–2014 | Dashboards connect workforce, skills and cost views. | A cement manager sees more measures, but decision owners and operational context may be absent. |
| 2015–2025 | Self-service analytics, predictive methods and scenario planning expand. | Faster analysis can hide stale qualifications, uncertain demand and small-group disclosure. |

Cover the table and reconstruct problem → response → remaining gap for each window. Which old control still helps during a data outage? Which modern feature could make a wrong count more persuasive?

## 3. AI-driven exploration — 2026–2050 scenarios

These are scenarios, not predictions, product roadmaps, guaranteed capabilities or OmniForge deployments. Historical teaching windows end in 2025; the separate AI-driven exploration begins in 2026. The current build must also work without AI.

| Scenario period | Possible assistance | Counterexample and question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026–2030 · Assist | Conversational explanations retrieve governed metric definitions and lineage. | An assistant calls employments “people.” Can a learner inspect and reject its calculation? |
| 2031–2040 · Connect | Planning compares quarry, cement and recycling skill/capacity assumptions. | A qualification feed is stale. Does the plan show uncertainty rather than imply safe readiness? |
| 2041–2050 · Adapt | Worker-informed planning explores interventions and evaluates their effects. | A model recommends penalizing an individual from an opaque risk score. Can humans block consequential use and challenge evidence? |

Competing future: fragmented local systems and manual reconciliations persist. Versioned definitions, bounded access and an inspectable decision log help in either world. Write one future hypothesis and an observation that would make you revise it.

## 4. Persona-led story — a bigger number, the same staffing concern

Source baseline: OmniForge has 46,000 employees plus contractors; contractor count is unknown. Production-site, quarry and cement-plant counts may overlap and must not be added. The incident, dataset and policy fixtures below are new educational fiction.

Elena Petrova (OMF-P07), Cement Plant Director, sees a workforce report rise after a quarry-to-cement transfer. Her kiln shifts still have capability gaps. Arjun Mehta (OMF-P05), Group CHRO, asks whether recruitment, learning or redeployment would address the need. Diego Alvarez (OMF-P14), Head of Data Governance and AI, asks which population and source versions produced the number.

Elena needs reliable coverage and retains local operational release/rollback authority. Arjun owns workforce adoption and skills concerns. Diego owns data products and model release evidence. Their stable source IDs and designations are preserved; job titles do not establish every metric, privacy or staffing approval delegation.

First choice: approve hiring from the total, average the conflicting reports, or reconcile the definitions and examine demand. Pause and record your choice before reading the reveal.

Evidence reveal: the total mixes current employees, a contractor, a future starter and a recently ended employment. One employee has two concurrent employments. A join to qualification history also repeats rows. The reporting error is actionable; it is not evidence that the plant has enough qualified, rested people.

Diego proposes a reconciled snapshot with visible lineage. Elena wants a conditional capacity scenario and the evidence still missing. Arjun challenges an attrition-risk drill-down for a tiny night-shift group. Revise the initial choice and identify the discovery action that can proceed without releasing work or deciding an individual's employment outcome.

A defensible next step is to reconcile the snapshot, validate productive-time and skill assumptions with plant/workforce owners, then compare bounded staffing options. Correlation between overtime and quality incidents does not prove that an individual caused a defect or that hiring will solve it.

## 5. Responsibilities, ownership and decision rights

| Area / application | Accountable responsibility | Boundary to protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HR master data / HR data owner | Worker identity, worker type, employment validity, assignments and contracted FTE. | Analytics consumes facts; it cannot silently correct employment history. |
| Time, learning and contractor sources / respective owners | Approved time, current qualification evidence and contractor assignment status. | Missing or unapproved records remain unknown; course completion is not task authorization. |
| Governed data product / metric steward | Versioned definitions, deduplication, lineage, reconciliation and freshness. | A new definition requires review; changing a dashboard label cannot change the population. |
| Reporting / analytics applications | Calculate and present authorized measures and conditional scenarios. | Do not convert FTE, predictions or reported attendance into safe-shift readiness. |
| Privacy and access authority / delegated reviewer | Approve purpose, audience, granularity, export and retention rules. | Aggregation alone does not prevent identification through drill-down or differencing. |
| Business decision owner / plant and workforce governance | Choose investigate, replan, stop or request a scoped intervention. | Models prepare evidence; people retain staffing, safety and employment decisions. |

For this prototype, Diego sponsors metric reconciliation; Arjun sponsors workforce interpretation; Elena owns the proposed plant investigation/replan decision. A delegated metric approver and privacy reviewer remain discovery roles, not invented canonical personas or established enterprise delegations.

Source objects: worker, employment, assignment history, qualification reference, approved-time record, metric definition, snapshot, transformation version, scenario assumption, access rule and decision log. For each used object record authoritative owner, purpose, effective date, extraction time, access, retention and quality checks.

Value stream: frame decision → agree measure → reconcile sources → analyze alternatives → authorize action → evaluate evidence. Workforce analysis is a capability; this value stream describes progress toward useful decisions. An application inventory is neither.

SAP examples can illustrate source reporting and planning. Verify relevant product release, connection, permission scope, licensing and export behavior before configuration; no tenant integration or deployment is established by this lesson.

## 7. One build card — Workforce Decision

Use paper or a simple table. No real worker data, production report or model is needed. The identifiers below are teaching records, not canonical enterprise IDs.

| Card field | Write or simulate |
| --- | --- |
| Question and scope | Should one illustrative cement site investigate additional skilled capacity for a proposed maintenance/recycling work package? Keep this planning question separate from immediate kiln-post release. |
| Snapshot · MAT-HR09-SYN01 | As of end 31 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata; extract 1 November. Use start date inclusive and end date exclusive. E901 has concurrent active employments A/B at this site, each 0.5 contracted FTE; E902 has one active employment at 1.0 FTE; E903 starts 1 November; E904 ended 30 October; C901 is one active contractor. |
| Definition and expected reconciliation | Employee headcount = distinct active employee persons at this site = 2. Active employee employments = 3; contracted employee FTE = 0.5 + 0.5 + 1.0 = 2.0. Active contractor persons = 1 separately. Combined distinct persons = 3, labelled explicitly, never relabelled employee headcount. FTE is not actual availability or ready capacity. |
| Deduplication and validity | Select effective records at the as-of instant; apply worker/employment keys before aggregation. Qualification history is a one-to-many relation: choose the relevant valid evidence or aggregate separately before joining. Preserve exceptions and lineage. |
| Planning variant · MAT-HR09-SYN02 | Assume 240 required qualified productive staff-hours/week. At assumed 30 productive hours/person/week, quotient = 8 person-equivalents; at 24 hours, quotient = 10. These are conditional workload equivalents, not required hires, headcount forecasts or confirmed available staffing. Demand, skills, rest, absences and current usable capacity remain unvalidated. |
| Decision ADR01 | Compare more independent dashboards, centralized totals alone, and governed decision products with domain-owned definitions. Propose the third for traceability and local context; compare a reconciled manual option before selecting technology. |
| Privacy fixture | General managers see approved aggregates only. For this exercise suppress cohorts under 5 and associated complementary cells; block row-level drill-down and export that could reconstruct a suppressed group. This is a classroom rule, not a universal lawful or sufficient privacy threshold. Do not publish the synthetic tiny-cohort result to the simulated general-manager view. |
| Lifecycle and recovery | Draft definition → source reconciliation → metric/access review → versioned publication → human decision → evaluation. Late corrections create a revised snapshot, impact notice and decision review; retain earlier published versions with status. If freshness or access checks fail, withhold the result and route the exception. |
| Evidence and measure | Log T01–T03 input → expected → actual paper observation → revision/rerun → reviewer. M01 = tests satisfying expected behavior / 3. Leave actual results and EV01 empty until learners execute and record observations. |

Headcount, employment counts and FTE are valid answers to different questions; do not force them to match or average them. The small dataset deliberately teaches units and filters, not an enterprise staffing baseline.

Proposed enterprise measure after discovery: reconciled published metric instances / published metric instances checked for one defined reporting cycle. Metric instance = definition version × site × as-of snapshot. Define the reconciliation tolerance, sampling, exclusions and owner before use; list withheld instances separately. No baseline, target, forecast accuracy or achieved benefit is asserted.

## 8. Three tabletop tests

| Test | Inject | Expected behavior — planned, not executed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OMF-HR09-T01 · Count and lineage | Run SYN01; duplicate E901's employment rows through qualification-history joins, then add a late source correction. | Reconcile the original fixture to 2 employee persons, 3 employments, 2.0 contracted FTE and 1 separate contractor. Remove join multiplication by documented keys/effective logic. Show exclusions, source versions and a separately versioned corrected snapshot; never silently rewrite prior output. |
| OMF-HR09-T02 · Tiny cohort and opaque score | A general manager requests the two-person kiln team's individual attrition scores, drill-down, export and totals that reveal a suppressed subgroup. | Apply the classroom suppression/complementary controls across views and export; deny unauthorized individual disclosure and consequential use of opaque scores. Route purpose, privacy and model concerns to accountable reviewers. Report-building permission alone does not grant data access. |
| OMF-HR09-T03 · Uncertain capacity | Run SYN02 first at 30, then 24 productive hours; remove the verified demand assumption and ask AI for a precise hiring number. | Explain 8 versus 10 conditional workload equivalents and their units. With missing demand evidence withhold the numerical recommendation, label unknowns and assign validation. Do not subtract unrelated headcount/FTE, imply readiness, assert causation or authorize recruitment/safety action from the quotient. |

Run all three manually. Write what actually happened, including failure, revise the same card and rerun affected steps. Acceptance requires explainable definitions, traceable calculations, access protection and a decision that respects unknowns. Publication verifies saved content; it does not execute these tests or populate OMF-HR09-EV01.

## 9. Reflection and Pre-Foundation handoff

Why can 2 people, 3 employments and 2.0 FTE all be correct? Which missing fact changes the staffing decision most? Could a total reveal a suppressed cohort? What evidence would justify changing the proposed architecture?

Pre-Foundation: choose one metric and validate its vocabulary, time boundaries, history handling, joins, freshness, permissions, export controls and acceptance tests with HR, data and plant owners. Establish a small decision experiment and evaluation period before configuring a report or model.

HR06 supplies learning/qualification context; HR07 owns payroll facts and corrections; HR08 owns approved-time and readiness handoffs. HR09 evaluates these inputs without taking ownership of their source facts. HR10 can later investigate how workers access and challenge information.
