## 3. Origins and process evolution

These closed windows are educational groupings, not universal invention dates or OmniDaily corporate biography. Adoption overlaps; older practices can coexist through the present. Reconstruct each as need → response → unresolved problem.

1970–1984 — Personnel summaries answer how many people; manual totals hide inconsistent boundaries.

1985–1994 — Spreadsheets compare sites; headcount and staffing capacity can be confused.

1995–2004 — HR data warehouses consolidate sources; shared storage does not create shared definitions.

2005–2014 — Dashboards expose trends; averages can hide local gaps and sensitive small groups.

2015–2025 — People analytics and forecasting connect decisions; lineage, privacy and uncertainty remain necessary.

## 4. One persona-led story: Three totals, one staffing decision

Scene 1: Nandita Sen, Group CHRO (ODG-P06), asks Sofia Alvarez, Chief Digital and Data Officer (ODG-P05), whether a synthetic distribution shift needs more capacity. Three reports show different workforce totals.

Scene 2: Choose the largest total, average the reports or reconcile their definitions. Reveal: one counts people, one counts employments and one mixes contractors with contracted FTE.

Scene 3: A requested breakdown identifies a tiny group. Another reveal shows a demand scenario has been presented as a certain staffing requirement.

Scene 4: Sofia narrows the question, separates populations and documents lineage. Nandita asks for an authorized privacy review and a human planning decision with assumptions, not an automatic individual action.

Decision pause: Would a reconciled small measure set or a broad dashboard help this decision? What evidence would justify adding another metric? Record a first answer before the reveal, then revise the same card. Name a counterexample that would favor the alternative.

## 5. Responsibilities and human decision rights

Core HR owns person/employment facts; time owns attendance; learning owns assessment evidence; business operations owns demand/workload context. Data stewards define populations, denominators and lineage; analytics owns transformations and uncertainty labels. Privacy/security owners approve access/disclosure rules; accountable HR/operations leaders decide staffing. SAP People Analytics is an example, not proof of a selected reporting stack.

These are proposed educational responsibilities. Establish the business rule, source of truth and accountable human before choosing technology. SAP examples illustrate a capability and are not verified deployments, licensing claims or product roadmaps. A worker must have an accessible human route to question incorrect data or a decision. HR status, learning completion, work authorization and quality/batch release are distinct decisions.

## 7. One build card: Workforce Evidence Decision Card

Synthetic experiment: Build SYN-METRIC909 for one synthetic staffing question. Use 10 distinct persons, 11 employment rows and 2 contractor records as teaching data; these are not OmniDaily staffing facts and must not be summed indiscriminately.

Fill nine fields on one page: (1) purpose/person affected; (2) current/proposed state and evidence; (3) identifiers and definitions; (4) business time/version; (5) application/data owners; (6) human approval/appeal; (7) chosen option versus alternative and trade-off; (8) exception, fallback and automation boundary; (9) three observed test responses and revision.

Design constraint: Define as-of date, inclusion rules, distinct key and denominator. FTE is a capacity measure with an agreed basis, not another person count. Aggregate insight does not justify identifying a worker or assigning blame.

All SYN identifiers, episode details and injected evidence are new teaching data. Country rules, collective agreements, qualifications, worker/device populations and local approvals remain unknown unless explicitly sourced. Record each unknown with an owner and discovery question. Do not invent legal rules or calculate unsupported operational benefits.

## 8. Exactly three tabletop tests

### ODG-TOS-HR09-T01 — Population reconciliation

Reveal/input: Three reports count the synthetic rows using different units.

Expected design response: Explain each total from definitions and lineage; do not average incompatible units or duplicate persons.

### ODG-TOS-HR09-T02 — Tiny cohort

Reveal/input: A filter makes a sensitive subgroup identifiable.

Expected design response: Hold/reduce disclosure under approved policy and route privacy review; do not invent a universal minimum-group threshold.

### ODG-TOS-HR09-T03 — Uncertain demand

Reveal/input: A forecast scenario is shown as a guaranteed staffing need.

Expected design response: Expose assumptions and ranges/scenario status; require a human planning decision and disconfirming evidence.

For each test record input → expected response → actual paper response → evidence note → pass/revise. These are proposed acceptance criteria. The evidence slot starts unfilled: publication runs none of the tests and proves no operational result. If a protected human decision can be bypassed, revise the design even if every card field is filled.

## 9. Traceability and measures

ODG-TOS-HR09-REQ01 — Trace one decision measure to population, unit, date, source and transformation. Derived from the supporting HR source mandate and ODG-M18; assessed by T01.

ODG-TOS-HR09-REQ02 — Protect sensitive disclosure and preserve uncertainty before staffing decisions. Derived from the source governance mandate and ODG-M19; assessed by T02–T03.

ODG-TOS-HR09-ADR01 — Prefer a small governed decision measure over an unreconciled dashboard catalog; compare insight coverage and definition effort. This proposed educational decision can be rejected with evidence; record the alternative and consequence.

ODG-TOS-HR09-EXP01 — The one synthetic build card extends ODG-M19's delivery-readiness discipline; T01–T03 do not execute the larger implementation case.

ODG-TOS-HR09-M01 — Mandatory card fields completed / 9 × 100: learning completeness only. ODG-TOS-HR09-M02 — Record whether T02/T03 preserve privacy and scenario uncertainty. Record actual paper observations rather than assuming success.

ODG-TOS-HR09-EV01 — Unfilled evidence slot for test observations, revisions and reviewer questions. Existing case success measures are intended outcomes; no baseline or target attainment follows from publishing this edition.

## 10. AI-driven exploration: scenarios, not predictions

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. Each needs evidence, approved purpose, accountable ownership and proportionate controls.

2026–2030 — Assist: Assistance could explain metric definitions and reconciliation gaps.

2031–2040 — Connect: Governed HR and business data products could share lineage and access context.

2041–2050 — Adapt: Planning models could adapt to changing demand while people challenge assumptions and retain staffing authority.

Competing future: A small trusted reporting set with local interpretation may outperform predictive dashboards where data quality remains weak.

Disconfirmation question: Would the governed metric still produce contradictory decisions or privacy harms compared with a simpler reviewed report? Name evidence that would stop or change the hypothesis.
