# The payroll run is complete. Has the worker been paid correctly?

HR07 — Autonomous Payroll Enterprises · HR07-APE7 · GICS02 Materials · OmniForge Materials Ltd.

IIL — Industry Immersion Lab · SuccessLabs Academy — Architecting Experiences for a Better World.

Level 0 → Pre-Foundation · Sprint · 120 minutes · Edition 2026-10-31 · Planning reference: HUB-ORIGIN-HR07-MAT-sprint (not an LMS ID).

Community/Citizen Edition. Educational content; no live course, enrollment, certification, real payroll calculation/payment, public companion access or achieved business result is implied.

## 1. One learner promise

In 120 minutes, design and defend one payroll correction with approved inputs, accountable release and separate evidence of payment and accounting reconciliation.

One persona-led story · One Payroll Decision Build Card · Three tabletop tests. Keep timeline notes, ownership, decisions and observed paper results on the same card. No live payroll, bank instruction or tenant configuration is required.

Autonomous in this lesson means bounded monitoring, preparation and exception routing under approved policy. It does not mean unreviewed pay changes, deductions or payment release.

## 2. One story: Nikhil follows the missing shift correction

Nikhil Sen, Shift Supervisor and Workforce Representative — OMF-P16, brings a synthetic quarry worker's question to Arjun Mehta, Group CHRO — OMF-P05: an approved correction to prior-period shift earnings has not been explained on the worker's pay statement.

The worker supported an additional maintenance shift during a quarry-to-plant supply disruption. The approved time correction arrived after the earlier processing cutoff. A dashboard now says the payroll run completed.

Thomas van Dijk, Group CFO — OMF-P02, asks whether three things agree: the approved source input, the calculated result and the financial/payment evidence. The dashboard establishes none of these by itself.

Choose first: suppress the retroactive change, rerun and resend everything, or validate the correction and reconcile its calculation, release and downstream status. Record your reason.

New evidence: the payment service timed out after receiving a business instruction. Its acceptance/settlement status is unknown. A retry could repeat the same payment. Meanwhile, one payroll posting is not confirmed as accepted by Finance.

The proposed design sends the case to the delegated payroll, payment and Finance owners. They investigate existing records before authorizing any correction or resend. The worker receives a clear status and correction route, without a false promise that payment has settled.

The worker, maintenance shift, dates, amounts, interface incident and proposed decision controls are new teaching fiction. Existing persona names/designations/IDs are preserved. CFO/CHRO sponsorship and Nikhil's representative role do not establish payroll release or bank authority. Those delegations must be confirmed. ## 3. Origins and evolution: 1970–2025

Closed teaching windows clarify the sequence. Actual adoption and practices overlapped; these are not invention dates, universal transitions or OmniForge corporate history.

| Period | Process response | Unfinished question |
|---|---|---|
| 1970–1984 | Wage books, time summaries and batch processing | Are the right workers, hours and approved earnings included? |
| 1985–1994 | Computerized payroll engines and structured deductions | Can correct rules still calculate the wrong source data? |
| 1995–2004 | ERP HR/payroll linked to banking and accounting | Which handoff or record explains a difference? |
| 2005–2014 | Shared-service operations, common controls and local variants | Who owns country/process exceptions and continuity? |
| 2015–2025 | Cloud/hybrid payroll, employee access and stronger exception monitoring | Does a completed run prove correct, timely pay and reconciled postings? |

Counterexample: a balanced total can hide two employees' offsetting errors. A payment file created successfully can still contain a rejected or unresolved instruction.

## 4. AI-driven Payroll: 2026–2050

| Period | AI-driven scenario | Materials experiment and retained human decision |
|---|---|---|
| 2026–2030 — Assist | AI summarizes authorized results and prioritizes payroll exceptions | Compare its explanation of a shift correction with a manual review. Qualified payroll owners validate rules, evidence and release. |
| 2031–2040 — Connect | AI supports earlier cross-system checks and correction simulations | Explore one traceable employment/time-to-pay handoff across quarry and plant operations. Source owners approve inputs; payment owners authorize disbursement. |
| 2041–2050 — Adapt | AI helps coordinate resilient payroll services as workforce and operating models change | Compare provider/country variants and recovery options. Accountable people retain policy, exception, funding and payment authority. |

Competing 2050 scenario: opaque automation or fragmented providers produce fast calculations but weak explanations and duplicate recovery actions. What observation would make you stop or redesign the proposed assistance?

## 5. Application responsibilities and human authority

Proposed learning architecture, not a verified deployed landscape. Approved inputs, calculated payroll, payment instructions, settlement and accounting postings require distinct evidence.

| Responsibility | Accountable owner / authoritative information | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Core HR — HR01 | HR data owner: approved employment/pay facts and relevant dates | A saved change is not proof payroll received or processed it. |
| Time and absence — HR08 | Time owners and delegated approvers: hours, absence and approved corrections | Time approval does not establish the net payment amount. |
| Rewards — HR05 | Rewards owners: approved award version, currency, period and eligibility | An approved gross award is not net pay or bank settlement. |
| Payroll / ECP as an example | Qualified payroll process owner: gross-to-net, retroactive calculation, validation and result version | A run completing does not prove every input, rule or downstream outcome is correct. |
| Treasury / payment service / bank | Delegated payment owners: authorization, instruction identity, acceptance, rejection and settlement evidence | Timeout or transmission success is not settlement; repeated instructions need controlled recovery. |
| Finance | Accounting owners: payroll liabilities, posting acceptance and reconciliation | A balanced summary alone does not prove worker-level pay accuracy. |
| Employee service and controls | Payroll/HR service owner: restricted explanations, correction routing and audit record | Do not expose personal pay data broadly or promise unconfirmed payment. |

SAP Payroll Control Center can assign and support investigation of alerts (R1). An alert indicates something to inspect, not proof that the result is wrong. Recalculation for approved past-period changes is a legitimate payroll behavior (R2); specific country rules, retro limits and recovery treatment require qualified validation.

AI may draft an explanation from authorized results, flag missing evidence or route an exception. It cannot invent entitlement, waive controls, alter bank details, release funds or silently close an unresolved discrepancy. HR11 deepens AI controls; HR12 transport/retry design; HR09 measure definitions.

## 6. One build card: Payroll Decision

Synthetic worker MAT-W701 has an approved September 2026 shift-earnings correction received for October processing. Input MAT-IN701-v1 adds INR 2,000 to gross earnings under an assumed approved teaching policy. It is not a net-payment promise, actual legal entitlement or prescribed wage-type mapping.

Use result MAT-PR701-v1, payment instruction MAT-PAY701-v1 and posting MAT-FI701-v1 as teaching references. The correct final net amount is deliberately unspecified: applicable deductions, country rules, prior results and other inputs must be validated. Never infer net pay by adding INR 2,000 to a bank amount.

| Payroll Decision Build Card | Record once |
|---|---|
| Purpose and scope | Worker outcome, currency, pay period, processing period and rules/owners still needed. |
| Source and dates | Approved time/pay evidence, business-effective date, approval/receipt dates and cutoff/exception policy. |
| Calculation and retro | Affected prior/current results, authorized input version, gross/net distinction and explainable delta; identify unknowns. |
| Validation and release | Payroll reviewer, release authority, segregation of duties and evidence required before the affected action. |
| Payment and accounting | Business instruction identity, authorization, acceptance/rejection/settlement status; posting status and reconciliation owner. |
| Continuity and correction | Named exception owner, worker impact, approved fallback and recovery/confirmation condition; no blind resend. |
| Worker explanation and privacy | Confirmed facts, outstanding questions, next update owner and accessible correction route; synthetic references only. |
| Decision and tests | Chosen approach, one alternative, trade-off; T01–T03 expected → observed paper result → revision. |

Suggested progression: approved inputs → validate → calculate → resolve exceptions → authorize applicable actions → confirm payment/postings → reconcile. Payment and accounting paths can progress separately; this is a conceptual control view, not a mandatory technical execution order or prescribed SAP status chain.

If data is unavailable near cutoff, invoke the pre-agreed continuity/exception process with qualified owners and worker-impact assessment. Do not invent hours, deductions, a universal hold-all rule or an unapproved payment shortcut. Actual obligations, agreements, deadlines and country settings are outside this fictional sprint.

Protect personal data. Do not include real names, salaries, account numbers, tax identifiers or internal endpoints. Same amount does not prove duplication; compare the actual business obligation, worker, period, version and existing payment status.

## 7. Three tabletop tests

| Test | Challenge | Expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| OMF-HR07-T01 — Approved retroactive correction | Past-period shift earnings are approved after the earlier cutoff | Validate source/date/version and the approved retro rules; explain the change across periods. Do not suppress all retroactivity or invent the final net amount. |
| OMF-HR07-T02 — Timeout and repeated payment instruction | MAT-PAY701-v1 is retried while its earlier status is unknown | Investigate the same business instruction and bank/payment status; prevent uncontrolled duplicate release. Retry, replacement or correction follows authorized recovery with an audit trail. |
| OMF-HR07-T03 — Missing input and unconfirmed posting | A time feed is unavailable near cutoff; MAT-FI701-v1 is not confirmed accepted | Expose input completeness and posting exceptions separately, invoke the approved continuity process and reconcile at worker/result/posting level. Run completion alone must not produce a fully-reconciled or paid status. |

Record actual paper behavior, failed assumptions and revisions. These are planned tabletop tests, not executed SAP, bank or statutory validation. OMF-HR07-EV01 stays unfilled until a learner supplies observations.

Acceptance: one completed card, all three observed tabletop results after revision and a 60-second explanation of calculation, payment and accounting boundaries. Blind resend, invented pay evidence or unreviewed release requires redesign.

| Learning anchor | Source connection and card evidence |

| OMF-HR07-REQ02 | OMF-M17/19: authorized recovery and distinct payment/accounting confirmations; tested by T02–T03. |
| OMF-HR07-ADR01 | Common payroll control/evidence principles with justified country/process variants. Compare a single run-success flag; state the trade-off and missing evidence. |
| OMF-HR07-EXP01 / EV01 | One synthetic payroll card and planned T01–T03; evidence slot unfilled until observed results/revisions exist. |
| OMF-HR07-M01 | Learning measure: tests meeting expected behavior / 3, with failed criteria and revisions. No enterprise accuracy, timeliness or financial benefit is asserted. |

For later enterprise measurement, define on-time correct pay against the population due, authorized correct amounts, deadline and payment-confirmation standard. Separate legitimate retroactivity from errors, worker-level exceptions from aggregate balances, and accounting closure from payment outcome. No baseline or achieved percentage is supplied.

Reflection: Which fact changed your release decision? What does the worker still need explained? What evidence would distinguish a failed transmission from an already accepted instruction?

Carry the same card into Pre-Foundation to define pay period, processing period, gross/net, approved input, wage type, retroactive difference, cutoff, payment instruction, posting and reconciliation.

Educational alignment: TOGAF for responsibilities/options; BIZBOK for capabilities/value; BABOK for needs/acceptance; DAMA-DMBOK for ownership/quality/lineage; COBIT for accountable controls; relevant ISO/IEC principles for restricted access, controlled change and evidence. No certification or compliance claim is made.
