## Governance and SAP validation
- System truth: `SE16N → T500L`, especially `MOLGA`, `INTCA`, and `PYSOL`, plus SAP release-specific documentation. Do not equate country grouping with a supported ECP payroll localization.
- Validate country taxes, social insurance, pay elements and payroll statutory output against official local authorities and document source/effective dates.
- Segregate HR, time, payroll, finance, banking and audit responsibilities; protect personnel data.
- **MOLGA 57** represents a historical territory designation and **MOLGA 99** is a generic international grouping, not a single sovereign payroll jurisdiction.
- This catalog uses an indicative numeric list only; compare against the actual T500L table for extra or changed codes.

## Completion evidence
Each country case must culminate in an executive one-pager, architecture pack, documented compliance sources, minimum 12 test cases, control matrix, country-localization decision, and a demo of the energy shift/overtime incident.
