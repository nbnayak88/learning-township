## Origins: the question that survives each era

These closed windows group process evolution for teaching; they are not universal invention or adoption dates. Adoption overlaps. Paper, spreadsheets and older systems can coexist through the present.
1970–1984 — Wage clerks calculate pay from paper registers
1985–1994 — Payroll databases automate recurring calculations
1995–2004 — ERP payroll connects employment, time and finance
2005–2014 — Shared services and cloud interfaces coordinate exceptions
2015–2025 — Control dashboards and AI triage require strict release authority
A successful payroll calculation is not proof of a settled payment; an unanswered bank call is not proof of failure.

## One story: choose, reveal, revise

David Chen (OTS-P02), Group CFO, receives an urgent request to resend a synthetic managed-service engineer's allowance. Marcus Okoro (OTS-P08), SRE and Managed Services Director, confirms the engineer worked an approved incident shift. Nadia Fernandes (OTS-P07), Group CHRO, wants a fast correction that preserves employee trust.
Choose before reading on: Resend immediately, wait without ownership, or reconcile the original payment while separately approving the late-input correction?
Evidence reveal: The allowance arrived after payroll cutoff, while the original bank instruction has unknown status. David separates the late-input correction from settlement reconciliation, assigns payroll and treasury owners, and holds a duplicate instruction until the original status is established. The employee receives an honest status and escalation route.
Write what changed your decision and what remains unknown. An accessible explanation and a human challenge route must be available to the affected worker. An uncertain record cannot silently become an authorization.

## Application responsibility and human authority

Core HR / time — HR owns employment; time owner owns approved attendance. Authorized owners approve corrections.
Payroll — Payroll owner controls country calculation and reconciliation. Payroll reviewer authorizes correction/off-cycle treatment.
Treasury / bank interface — Treasury owns payment instruction and settlement evidence. Payment authority authorizes release after reconciliation.
Finance — Finance owns posting and account reconciliation. Accounting reviewer confirms posting independently of payment.
Business owners remain accountable; applications store and process the records. Discover the actual delegated approvers before implementation. Restrict client, candidate, pay and health information to its purpose and permitted viewers. Minimize replicated data and unnecessary compute rather than assuming automation is sustainable.
SAP examples in the preserved source are discussion options, not a technology selection or a claim that configuration has been validated. Start with the business need. AI may summarize permitted evidence or draft a reminder; it cannot approve a consequential people decision, grant privilege or declare a payment complete.

## One build experiment: Decision Build Card

Synthetic inputs: Employee IT-P700; allowance ALW-700; approved incident time 2 hours as synthetic input; payroll cutoff passed; payment PAY-700 status unknown; no country-specific tax rules supplied.
Complete these fields once:
1. People and business need: who benefits, what could go wrong and why this matters in IT services or engineering.
2. Initial choice and reason: compare the choices before seeing the reveal.
3. Evidence reveal and revised choice: state what changed, and one counterexample.
4. Identity, date and source owner: identify authoritative records, effective time, provenance and uncertain facts.
5. Application responsibility: separate record keeping, orchestration, calculation, analytics and access enforcement.
6. Human approval and protected action: name the decision role, limits and worker challenge route.
7. Minimum data and permitted viewers: state purpose, retention question, client isolation and accessible communication.
8. Alternative and local variant: defend a common core with a justified regional/client variant, or explain a different design.
9. Exception owner and recovery: define hold, escalation, retry/reconciliation and confirmation conditions.
10. Measure and missing evidence: Exceptions with traceable input, approved correction and distinct payment/posting status / exceptions reviewed; no duplicate instruction in the proposed tests.
11. Pre-Foundation question: Define payroll input, result, posting, payment instruction and settlement; identify missing country and cutoff rules.
For each test below record input → expected → observed paper result → evidence → revision. Blank observations remain blank. A complete card can still contain a poor decision.

## Exactly three tabletop tests

OTS-HR07-T01 — Late approved input
Expected proposed behavior: Record the correction and owned cutoff decision; assess permitted next-cycle/off-cycle route without inventing statutory treatment.
Evidence to record: Record approval, period, amount basis and payroll reviewer.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR07-T02 — Payment status unknown
Expected proposed behavior: Query/reconcile the existing instruction using its unique reference; do not create a duplicate payment.
Evidence to record: Record settlement evidence gap and treasury owner.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.

OTS-HR07-T03 — Payroll accepted, posting rejected
Expected proposed behavior: Show calculation, payment and accounting as separate states; route the posting exception without recalculating or paying again blindly.
Evidence to record: Record target status and authorized recovery path.
Observed result / evidence / revision: initially unfilled; learner completes during the exercise.
Acceptance: one complete card, three recorded tabletop observations after any revisions, and an explanation of source ownership and human authority. A breached authority or privacy boundary requires redesign. Publishing these tests does not execute them.
