# OmniWorks Industrial Systems — Hungary Payroll Transformation Bootcamp

**HR07 APE7 | 03-HR07-IND-industrials | 03-Essentials | MOLGA 21 | Fictional case**

## Applied industry challenge
Investigate factory shifts and union agreements affecting the Hungary workforce: employee classification, time-to-pay, payroll cutoff, approvals, pay-element mapping, retroactivity, finance posting, privacy and exception monitoring.

## Learner mission
Discover country statutory requirements and stakeholders; map payroll capabilities and value streams; design EC/HCM/ECP or partner-engine integration and data migration; document configuration and controls; test 12+ scenarios; prepare cutover, AMS and executive defence. Map decisions to all 12 SuccessLabs Architecture Streams and 20 Academy learning tracks.

**Evidence:** country source register with effective dates, architecture, RACI, integration mapping, test pack, controls matrix, incident response and executive one-pager. **Rubric:** business 15; legal 20; architecture 20; controls 15; testing 20; defence 10 (pass 75; no critical legal/privacy gaps).

**Validation:** Check SAP T500L MOLGA/INTCA/PYSOL, actual release, licensing and country payroll support. MOLGA is not proof of SAP ECP localization. Do not invent statutory rates or wage types. This is an educational case, not proof of live LMS publication.
