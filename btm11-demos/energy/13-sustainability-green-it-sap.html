OmniVerse Energy Ltd. — Sustainability and Green IT for Intelligent Operations

SuccessLabs Academy · BTM11 · Energy (ENE) · Ultimate (Level 5) · Bootcamp Version: 2026-11-10 · Demonstration material; not an enrolled course, certification or published pathway. Evidence boundary: OmniVerse Energy Ltd. is a fictional educational case. The case sources hold little sustainability data for the IT estate; the data products, levers and tests below are new design. Nothing here is a record of a client implementation, an executed test or a realized benefit. Names marked synthetic carry the prefix ENE-SYN and are new design. SAP product statements come from public SAP documentation reviewed 2026-11-10 and must be verified before real delivery.

1. The question this file answers

How does the architecture support sustainability reporting, and how does it keep the footprint of IT and AI itself visible?

What this file shows: two separate topics for OmniVerse: (1) data products that support sustainability reporting, and (2) the footprint of the IT and AI estate, with levers, an owner and an evidence rule.

What it does not show: an emissions figure, a footprint measurement or a reduction claim.

2. Case anchor (source facts)

Attribute Case baseline

Business mix Upstream 35%; refining and petrochemicals 28%; retail and marketing 18%; power 12%; renewables 7%
Assets 8 refineries; 25,000 km pipelines; 12,500 retail sites; 15 GW generation

Decision under discussion (source): Which AI use cases stay assistive, which may trigger human-approved actions, and which low-risk processes may be automated (OVE-HR11 strategic dilemma).

Illustrative trigger (source): In the OVE-HR11 decision scenes, an employee (Rina) receives incorrect promotion guidance from an assistant, while HR operations wants automatic data corrections and case closure and a worker representative challenges sentiment inference and productivity scoring.

6. Two topics, kept apart

Topic What it covers Why separate
Reporting data Business data used for sustainability reporting (energy, emissions, supplier evidence) Owned by business functions; follows reporting rules
IT and AI footprint Energy and emissions from hosting, pipelines and model usage Owned by technology and operations; different data sources

Sector emphasis: Large operational estate: separate operational emissions reporting (business data) from the footprint of IT and AI itself.

7. Sustainability data products (new design)

Data product Main source Main risk
Energy and emissions report data Operations and finance systems Figures without a source or period
Asset energy-use view Asset management Asset ids that do not resolve
Refinery and site IT energy use Hosting and facilities records Estimates presented as measurements

Each product follows the Demo 07 rule: one owner, a quality rule and a visible status. Every figure shown carries its basis (measured, allocated or estimated) and its period.

8. Green IT levers (new design)

Lever Idea Link to other demos Owner (proposed)
Right-size non-production Switch off or shrink unused systems Demo 11 cost drivers OVE-P04 Farah Al-Hassan
Retention rules Delete data nobody needs Demo 07 data products OVE-P16 Dr. Leila Haddad
AI cost per use Choose the smallest model that passes evaluation; cache repeated answers Demos 03 and 09 OVE-P16 Dr. Leila Haddad

Three tabletop tests

A tabletop test is a paper walk-through, not a technical test of a running system.
