## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Health Care must treat consent and sensitive data with extra care; Consumer Staples often knows the shopper only through retailers, so identity is the hard part.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Industrials sells through long, multi-stakeholder cycles; Consumer Discretionary runs short, high-volume campaigns.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Utilities and Energy lean on field service and outage communication; Real Estate needs viewing, leasing and maintenance flows.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Utilities and Telecom need usage-based billing at scale; Industrials need contract-based and project billing.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Information Technology tracks product-usage signals; Health Care tracks access, wait time and satisfaction.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Financial Services requires explainable AI and strict consent; Energy integrates with operational and metering systems.
