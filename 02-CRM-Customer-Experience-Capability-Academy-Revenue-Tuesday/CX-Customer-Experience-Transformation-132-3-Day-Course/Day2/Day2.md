### Day 2 V2: Concept 1: Know the Customer: Data and Voice
*Duration: 12-15 min*

**Hook:** You cannot serve a customer you cannot see.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Know the Customer: Data and Voice**. It anchors two product academies: ACM1 Customer Data Cloud and ACX6 CX with Qualtrics. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example ACM1-EN, ACM1-MA, ACM1-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Customer Data Cloud builds one trusted, consented profile per customer. SAP CX with Qualtrics adds the voice: what customers say, feel and expect, captured at the moments that matter. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Every other experience decision, from offers to service to pricing, depends on knowing who the customer is and how they feel. Fragmented profiles and unheard feedback mean you personalise blindly and fix problems late.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities provider had a customer in six systems under six spellings. After unifying the profile and linking survey feedback to it, the contact centre stopped asking customers to repeat themselves, and complaints about 'being treated like a stranger' dropped within a quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: One consented profile across sales, service, commerce and marketing; Privacy and consent managed once, not in every system; Feedback tied to real customer records, so insight becomes action; A shared base for every journey you design later. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Health Care must treat consent and sensitive data with extra care; Consumer Staples often knows the shopper only through retailers, so identity is the hard part.

**1. What is the concept?**
Commerce Cloud runs digital selling across web, app and marketplaces. Service Cloud handles cases, knowledge, field service and omnichannel support after the sale. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Customers judge you across the whole journey: how easy it was to buy and how well you fix things when they go wrong. Strong commerce with weak service loses the repeat customer.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A telecom operator let customers buy online but sent every issue to a phone queue with no order history. Putting orders and cases in one agent view cut handling time and turned a complaint channel into a retention channel. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Consistent buying experience across channels; Faster case resolution with shared knowledge; One view of orders and cases for every agent; Lower cost to serve, higher loyalty. [on screen: four benefits]
