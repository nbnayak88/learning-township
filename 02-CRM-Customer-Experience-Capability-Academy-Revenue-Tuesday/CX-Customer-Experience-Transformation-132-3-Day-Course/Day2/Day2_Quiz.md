## Concept check (short answer)
- What does Customer Data Cloud give you?  
  *Answer: One trusted, consented profile per customer*
- Which series anchor Concept 1?  
  *Answer: ACM1 Customer Data Cloud and ACX6 CX with Qualtrics*
- Why link feedback to customer records?  
  *Answer: So insight turns into action for the right customer*
- What does Emarsys orchestrate?  
  *Answer: Personalised multi-channel campaigns and lifecycle journeys*
- Which series cover Concept 2?  
  *Answer: AMK2 Emarsys and ASL3 Sales Cloud*
- Why connect engagement and sales?  
  *Answer: Warm handoffs shorten the path from interest to contract*
- What does Service Cloud manage?  
  *Answer: Cases, knowledge, field service and omnichannel support*
- Which series cover Concept 3?  
  *Answer: AC04 Commerce Cloud and ACS5 Service Cloud*
- Why connect buying and serving?  
  *Answer: Customers judge the whole journey, not one touchpoint*
