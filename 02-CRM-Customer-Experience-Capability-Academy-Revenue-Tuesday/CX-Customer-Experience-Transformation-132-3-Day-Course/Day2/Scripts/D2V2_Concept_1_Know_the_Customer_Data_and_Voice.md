**1. What is the concept?**
Customer Data Cloud builds one trusted, consented profile per customer. SAP CX with Qualtrics adds the voice: what customers say, feel and expect, captured at the moments that matter. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Every other experience decision, from offers to service to pricing, depends on knowing who the customer is and how they feel. Fragmented profiles and unheard feedback mean you personalise blindly and fix problems late.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities provider had a customer in six systems under six spellings. After unifying the profile and linking survey feedback to it, the contact centre stopped asking customers to repeat themselves, and complaints about 'being treated like a stranger' dropped within a quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: One consented profile across sales, service, commerce and marketing; Privacy and consent managed once, not in every system; Feedback tied to real customer records, so insight becomes action; A shared base for every journey you design later.
