**1. What is the concept?**
Emarsys Customer Engagement orchestrates personalised, multi-channel campaigns and lifecycle journeys. Sales Cloud manages leads, opportunities, quotes and pipeline so engagement turns into revenue. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Marketing without a sales handoff creates noise, and sales without engagement context starts cold. Connecting the two shortens the path from first interest to signed deal.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A financial services firm tracked campaign opens and deal outcomes in different tools. Once Emarsys signals fed Sales Cloud opportunities, advisers called warm prospects first and conversion rose while discounting fell. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Right message, right channel, right moment; Clean lead handoff with full engagement history; Visible pipeline and forecast accuracy; Higher conversion at lower acquisition cost. [on screen: four benefits]
