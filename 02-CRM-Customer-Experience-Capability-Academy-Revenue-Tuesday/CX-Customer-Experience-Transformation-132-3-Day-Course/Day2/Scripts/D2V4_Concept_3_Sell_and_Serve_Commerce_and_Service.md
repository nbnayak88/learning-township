**1. What is the concept?**
Commerce Cloud runs digital selling across web, app and marketplaces. Service Cloud handles cases, knowledge, field service and omnichannel support after the sale. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Customers judge you across the whole journey: how easy it was to buy and how well you fix things when they go wrong. Strong commerce with weak service loses the repeat customer.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A telecom operator let customers buy online but sent every issue to a phone queue with no order history. Putting orders and cases in one agent view cut handling time and turned a complaint channel into a retention channel. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Consistent buying experience across channels; Faster case resolution with shared knowledge; One view of orders and cases for every agent; Lower cost to serve, higher loyalty. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Utilities and Energy lean on field service and outage communication; Real Estate needs viewing, leasing and maintenance flows.
