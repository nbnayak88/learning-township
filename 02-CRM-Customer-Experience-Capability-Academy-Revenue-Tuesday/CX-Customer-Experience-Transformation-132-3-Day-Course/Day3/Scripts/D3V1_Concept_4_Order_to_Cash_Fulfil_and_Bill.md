**1. What is the concept?**
SD, OMS and CX Integration connects what customers order with what you can fulfil. BRIM handles complex billing, subscriptions, usage-based charging and revenue management. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Front-end promises break at fulfilment and billing. Wrong stock, late delivery or a confusing invoice undoes every earlier good experience and creates disputes and revenue leakage.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A materials supplier promised delivery dates its order system could not honour. Linking the OMS availability to the commerce front end made promises realistic, and invoice disputes fell with them. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Orders the business can actually fulfil; Accurate, explainable invoices and charges; Support for subscription and usage models; Fewer disputes and faster cash. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Utilities and Telecom need usage-based billing at scale; Industrials need contract-based and project billing.
