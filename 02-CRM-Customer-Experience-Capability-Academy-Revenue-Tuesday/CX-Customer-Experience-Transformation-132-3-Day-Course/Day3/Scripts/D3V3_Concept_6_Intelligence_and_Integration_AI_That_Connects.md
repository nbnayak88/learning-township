**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and next-best-action to customer work. The Integration Suite connects CX with ERP, finance, data and partners so information flows once. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI can only act on what it can reach, and customers only trust it if it is accurate and governed. Integration provides the reach; governance provides the trust.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A service team deployed an assistant that drafted replies, but only after integration gave it live order and billing data. Before that, it answered confidently and wrongly; after, it became the agents' favourite tool. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster service and sales with assistive AI; Automated handoffs across front and back office; Governed, explainable AI on customer data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
