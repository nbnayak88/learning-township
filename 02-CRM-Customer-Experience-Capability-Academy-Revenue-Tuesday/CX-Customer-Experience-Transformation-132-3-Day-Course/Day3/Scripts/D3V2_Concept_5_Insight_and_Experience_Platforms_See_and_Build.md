**1. What is the concept?**
SAP Analytics Cloud and Databricks turn customer data into insight and prediction. SAP Build, Work Zone and Experience Platforms let you compose apps, portals and workspaces around the journey. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Without insight you improve what is loudest, not what matters. Without a way to build quickly, insight sits in a slide deck while customers wait.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer company saw churn predicted weeks ahead, then used low-code apps to launch a save-journey in days. The insight and the build were one motion instead of two projects. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Customer KPIs leaders trust; Predictive signals on churn and demand; Faster delivery of portals and apps; Experiences tailored by sector and role. [on screen: four benefits]
