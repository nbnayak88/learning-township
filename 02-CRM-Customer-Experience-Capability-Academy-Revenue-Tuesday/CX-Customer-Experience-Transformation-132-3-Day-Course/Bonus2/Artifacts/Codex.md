# Bonus 2 Codex: Deliver with Fit-to-Standard and Clean Core (SAP Activate)

**Tool:** Fit-Gap Matrix and the SAP Activate six-phase checklist (Codex)

## What
Fit-to-standard means testing business requirements against the standard solution first and only building where there is a real gap. SAP Activate organises delivery into six phases: Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy, Run. Clean core keeps extensions upgrade-safe.

## Why
Customisation is the quiet reason projects run late and over budget. A disciplined fit-gap keeps scope honest, protects upgrades and lets you reuse one design across many sectors.

## Benefits
- Shorter, more predictable delivery
- Lower cost of ownership and safer upgrades
- Clear decisions on what to configure, extend or defer
- Reusable designs across the 11 sectors

## Tactical steps
1. List requirements as short, testable statements.
2. Run each against standard: Fit, Configure, Gap.
3. For every Gap choose: adopt standard, extend in a clean-core way, or defer.
4. Record the decision, owner and effort in the Fit-Gap Matrix.
5. Map decisions to the six Activate phases and their quality gates.
6. Review the matrix with the business before Explore ends.
7. Keep the matrix alive in Run as the change log.
