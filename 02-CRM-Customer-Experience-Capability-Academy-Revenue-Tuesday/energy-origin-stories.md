## Pick your experiment

| Theme | One learner promise — in two hours… |
|---|---|
| CRM01 | design one trustworthy Customer 360 decision without confusing identity, relationships and permission. |
| CRM02 | design one relevant energy campaign that respects channel preferences and keeps essential service messages separate. |
| CRM03 | design one evidence-backed opportunity-to-quote decision without confusing a sales promise with confirmed supply. |
| CRM04 | design one transparent energy-service checkout that separates customer intent, payment and accepted order. |
| CRM05 | design one customer-service resolution that uses authoritative evidence and confirms the customer's next step. |
| CRM06 | design one customer-feedback experiment that turns a real friction point into a verified improvement decision. |
| CRM07 | design one auditable subscription change that keeps contract, entitlement and billing outcomes consistent. |
| CRM08 | design one customer-order handoff that distinguishes a promised date from a confirmed fulfillment outcome. |
| CRM09 | design one decision-ready customer measure with a clear population, source lineage and uncertainty. |
| CRM10 | design one accessible customer self-service task with truthful status and a recoverable failure path. |
| CRM11 | design one bounded customer assistant that answers from approved evidence and escalates consequential actions. |
| CRM12 | design one observable customer event exchange with safe retry and confirmed business outcome. |

## Source and scope boundaries

The canonical [OmniVerse customer-experience blueprint](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd/derived-streams/02-customer-experience.md) was an initial scope blueprint. These twelve new **Origin Story learning expansions** do not imply that twelve detailed implementation cases already existed or that the broader enterprise blueprint has been rewritten.

[Enterprise executive case](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd/executive-one-pager.md) · [Fictional enterprise personas](https://github.com/nbnayak88/successlabs-case-studies/blob/main/01-energy/omniverse-energy-ltd/personas/persona-cards.md) · [Existing CRM academy taxonomy](https://app.notion.com/p/b65e0b41255b4fcaa6c4fea15197f44a)

Existing enterprise identities and OVE-P persona IDs are retained. New scenarios and controls use the **OVE-CRMnn-OS** namespace for capability, value stages, requirement, decision, experiment, tests and measures. These are proposed learning designs; evidence slots remain empty until a learner records actual results.

All organizations, personas, dialogue and incidents in the case are fictional. No production configuration, actual transaction, achieved KPI, certification or standards compliance is asserted. Company targets remain targets. Historical periodization is an overlapping teaching lens; future scenes are hypotheses rather than forecasts.

Application boundaries matter: CRM01 governs customer meaning/ownership; CRM12 governs exchange/recovery. CRM06 improves experience through feedback; CRM10 designs the self-service interaction. Quotes, accepted orders, dispatch, delivery, invoicing and payment remain distinct states owned by the relevant business applications.
