## Choose a decision

- **FI01:** In two hours, design one evidence-backed close decision that keeps unresolved differences visible.
- **FI02:** In two hours, design one payable-to-payment proposal that validates obligations and prevents duplicate release.
- **FI03:** In two hours, design one receivables decision that separates billing, recognized revenue, cash receipt and clearing.
- **FI04:** In two hours, design one traceable tax-document flow that distinguishes determination, submission, acknowledgment and correction.
- **FI05:** In two hours, design one liquidity and payment-release decision using current bank evidence and independent authorization.
- **FI06:** In two hours, design one financial scenario that makes energy-transition assumptions, uncertainty and cash timing visible.
- **FI07:** In two hours, design one explainable cost-to-margin view that reconciles allocation choices without hiding business impact.
- **FI08:** In two hours, design one asset-accounting decision that links technical readiness, approved policy and valuation evidence.
- **FI09:** In two hours, design one financial-control exception with independent approval, time limits and evidence of closure.
- **FI10:** In two hours, design one traceable financial measure that separates opportunity, accounting impact and realized value.
- **FI11:** In two hours, design one bounded finance assistant that proposes explainable work without bypassing posting authority.
- **FI12:** In two hours, design one reconciled financial event exchange with safe replay and verified accounting outcome.
