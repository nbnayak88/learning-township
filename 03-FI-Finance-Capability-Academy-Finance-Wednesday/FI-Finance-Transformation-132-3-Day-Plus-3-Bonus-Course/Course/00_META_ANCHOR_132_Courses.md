### Concept 1: Record and Measure: Ledger and Controlling (Day 2 V2)
*If the books are wrong, everything built on them is wrong.*  
Summary: S/4HANA Finance is the single ledger that records every financial transaction once. Controlling (management accounting) adds cost centres, profitability and internal reporting so you can see not only what happened but where value is made or lost.

## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Energy and Materials need asset-heavy cost tracking and joint-venture accounting; Financial Services needs regulatory ledgers and product profitability.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Retail-heavy Consumer Discretionary handles huge volumes of small payments; Industrials handles milestone and project-based billing.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Financial Services and Health Care face heavy regulation and audit scrutiny; Utilities and Energy face tariff, tax and environmental reporting.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Energy and Materials carry commodity and FX exposure; Real Estate and Utilities carry large long-term financing.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Information Technology handles many small legal entities; Financial Services needs regulatory consolidation views.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Financial Services requires strict explainability and model governance; Industrials integrates with plant and supply chain systems.
