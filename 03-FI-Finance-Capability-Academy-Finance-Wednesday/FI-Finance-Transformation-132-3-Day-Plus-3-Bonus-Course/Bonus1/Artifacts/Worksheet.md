- [ ] Quantify the cost of doing nothing (cost, risk, lost revenue).
- [ ] List 3 benefits and attach a KPI and an owner to each.
- [ ] Estimate the investment (people, licences, change) and a range, not a point.
- [ ] Add risks and the simplest mitigation for each.
- [ ] Write the 60-second story for the CFO and CEO: problem, impact, option, ask.
