# Bonus 3 Codex: Land Adoption and Your Capstone Portfolio

**Tool:** Capstone Portfolio Codex and Adoption Scorecard

## What
Adoption is the share of people who actually use the new way of working. A capstone portfolio is your proof: three or four artifacts (a map, a case, a fit-gap, a plan) that show you can do the work in a real sector.

## Why
A finance transformation that nobody uses delivers nothing, and a learner without proof stays invisible. Adoption turns design into value; the portfolio turns learning into opportunity.

## Benefits
- Higher usage and faster time to value
- Visible, shareable proof of skill
- Stronger interviews, proposals and promotions
- A natural path to Pro and Star stages

## Tactical steps
1. Define who must change and what exactly changes for them.
2. Pick 3 adoption measures (usage, quality, sentiment) and a target.
3. Plan communication and training per group, in that order.
4. Create a Champion network of one person per team.
5. Assemble your portfolio: value case, fit-gap, process or journey map, adoption plan.
6. Publish it (one page per artifact) and share the link in the community.
7. Book a review with your accountability partner.
