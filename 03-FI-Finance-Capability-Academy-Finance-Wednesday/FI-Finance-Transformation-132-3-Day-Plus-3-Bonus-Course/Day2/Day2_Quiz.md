## Concept check (short answer)
- What does S/4HANA Finance give you?  
  *Answer: A single ledger that records every financial transaction once*
- Which series anchor Concept 1?  
  *Answer: AFR1 S/4HANA Finance and ACC7 S/4HANA Controlling*
- Why does controlling matter?  
  *Answer: It shows where value is made or lost, not only what happened*
- What does FI-AR manage?  
  *Answer: Invoicing, collections, credit and disputes owed by customers*
- Which series cover Concept 2?  
  *Answer: APT2 FI-AP and AOT3 FI-AR*
- Why do AP and AR matter together?  
  *Answer: They set working capital and the rhythm of cash*
- What does DRC produce?  
  *Answer: Statutory e-documents and country reports in the right format on time*
- Which series cover Concept 3?  
  *Answer: ATX4 Document and Reporting Compliance and AGR9 GRC for Finance*
- Why design compliance in?  
  *Answer: It is cheaper and more reliable than bolting it on*
