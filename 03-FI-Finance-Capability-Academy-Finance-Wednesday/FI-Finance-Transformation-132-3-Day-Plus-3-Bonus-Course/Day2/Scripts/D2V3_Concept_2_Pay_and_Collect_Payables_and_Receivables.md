**1. What is the concept?**
FI-AP manages what the company owes: invoices, approvals, payments and supplier relationships. FI-AR manages what customers owe: invoicing, collections, credit and dispute handling. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Payables and receivables set the rhythm of working capital. Slow invoice processing, late collections and unmanaged disputes lock cash inside the business and strain supplier and customer relationships.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer staples company cut its collection cycle once disputes were tracked in one queue rather than inboxes. Cash arrived weeks earlier, and treasury finally trusted the forecast. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster invoice-to-pay and fewer duplicate or late payments; Shorter days sales outstanding; Cleaner supplier and customer master data; Predictable cash flow for treasury and planning. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Retail-heavy Consumer Discretionary handles huge volumes of small payments; Industrials handles milestone and project-based billing.
