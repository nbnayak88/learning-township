**1. What is the concept?**
Document and Reporting Compliance (DRC) produces statutory e-documents and country reports in the right format at the right time. GRC for Finance defines who can do what, monitors risk and proves control to auditors. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Tax authorities and auditors do not accept 'the system was down'. Manual compliance work is expensive and fragile, and weak segregation of duties is how fraud and audit findings begin.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A group operating in 20 countries filed statutory reports from spreadsheets. Automating them through a single compliance layer meant a new country went live in weeks, not quarters, and audit requests were answered from the system. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: On-time, correct statutory filings across countries; Segregation of duties designed into processes; Audit evidence produced as a by-product of work; Lower compliance cost and less firefighting. [on screen: four benefits]
