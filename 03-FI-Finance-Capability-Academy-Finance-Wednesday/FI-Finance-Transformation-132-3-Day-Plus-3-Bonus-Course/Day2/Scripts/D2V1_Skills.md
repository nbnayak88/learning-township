# Day 2 V1: Skills
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Ten skills. That is all that stands between you and architect-level finance work.

Welcome to Day 2, and congratulations on completing Day 1! [pause]

Your goal from the Goal Card needs skills. Here are the top ten. [show slide]

1. **Financial process mapping (record to report, procure to pay, order to cash)**: See finance as connected flows.
2. **Chart of accounts and master data design**: Model the ledger and structures once.
3. **Fit-to-standard and process mapping**: Test requirements against standard and isolate gaps.
4. **Finance business case and value storytelling**: Turn finance change into CFO-ready value.
5. **Stakeholder and change leadership**: Align finance, business, audit and IT.
6. **Compliance and controls literacy**: Know where audit and tax can bite.
7. **Finance analytics and KPI design**: Choose few metrics that drive decisions.
8. **Integration and data-flow design**: Design interfaces and master-data ownership.
9. **AI prompting and AI governance for Finance**: Use AI well and safely on financial data.
10. **Sector translation across 11 GICS sectors**: Adapt one architecture to any industry.
