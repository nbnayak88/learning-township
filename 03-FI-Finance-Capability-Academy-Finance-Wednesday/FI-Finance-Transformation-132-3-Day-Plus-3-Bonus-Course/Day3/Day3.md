**1. What is the concept?**
Group Reporting and Financial Consolidation brings entities together: intercompany eliminations, currency translation, and group financial statements. Analytics Cloud for Finance turns those numbers into dashboards, variance analysis and insight. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Boards and investors see the group, not the entities. Late or inconsistent consolidation hides problems, and numbers without insight leave leaders asking 'so what?'.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A technology company with 40 entities consolidated by spreadsheet over 10 days. A single consolidation process halved that, and finance used the saved time for variance commentary that executives actually read. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster, more reliable group close; Consistent group and management views; Variance analysis that explains the numbers; Finance as a partner to the business, not a scorekeeper. [on screen: four benefits]

It anchors two product academies: AAI1-FI Business AI, Joule and AI Agents for Finance and AIG2-FI Integration Suite for Finance. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example AAI1-FI-EN, AAI1-FI-MA, AAI1-FI-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and anomaly detection into finance work. The Integration Suite connects finance with banks, tax authorities, ERP, procurement and partners so data flows once. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI can only be trusted on financial data that is complete, correct and governed. Integration provides the reach; governance, audit trails and explainability provide the trust finance requires.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A finance shared-services team introduced an assistant for invoice queries, but only after integration gave it one verified view of invoices and payments. Before that, it answered fluently and wrongly; afterwards it cleared a large share of routine queries. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster close, reconciliation and queries with assistive AI; Automated bank, tax and partner connections; Governed, auditable AI on financial data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
