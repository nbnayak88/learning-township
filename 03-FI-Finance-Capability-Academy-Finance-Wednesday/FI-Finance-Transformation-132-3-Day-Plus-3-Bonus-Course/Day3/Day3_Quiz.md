## Concept check (short answer)
- What does Treasury and Risk Management cover?  
  *Answer: Cash, liquidity, funding, FX and interest-rate exposure*
- Which series cover Concept 4?  
  *Answer: ATR5 Treasury and Risk Management and AFP6 Analytics Cloud Planning*
- Why connect planning and treasury?  
  *Answer: Forecasts need funding reality and treasury needs demand signals*
- What does Group Reporting do?  
  *Answer: Consolidates entities with eliminations, translation and group statements*
- Which series cover Concept 5?  
  *Answer: AFA8 Group Reporting and Consolidation; AFI0 Analytics Cloud for Finance*
- What do analytics add to consolidation?  
  *Answer: Explanations and insight behind the numbers*
- What makes AI trustworthy on financial data?  
  *Answer: Complete, governed data plus audit trail and explainability*
- Which series cover Concept 6?  
  *Answer: AAI1-FI Business AI, Joule, AI Agents; AIG2-FI Integration Suite*
- What does the Integration Suite do for finance?  
  *Answer: Connects banks, tax authorities, ERP and partners so data flows once*
