**1. What is the concept?**
Treasury and Risk Management handles cash, liquidity, funding, FX and interest-rate exposure. SAP Analytics Cloud planning supports budgeting, forecasting and scenario modelling across the business. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Surprises in cash or in the forecast cost real money: emergency borrowing, missed investments, weak negotiations. Planning without treasury ignores funding; treasury without planning guesses demand.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities company moved from monthly spreadsheet forecasts to rolling planning linked to treasury positions. When fuel prices spiked it modelled three scenarios in a day, and funding decisions were made with confidence. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Real-time cash and liquidity visibility; Managed FX and interest-rate risk; Faster, collaborative forecasts and scenarios; Better-funded decisions and calmer board conversations. [on screen: four benefits]
