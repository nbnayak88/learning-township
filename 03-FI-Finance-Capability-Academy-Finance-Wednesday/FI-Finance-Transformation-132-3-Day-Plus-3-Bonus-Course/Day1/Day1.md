### Day 1 V2: Model
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Every organisation I have worked with had the same problem: lots of finance tools, no map. [pause]

Before I teach you anything, let me earn the right to. I have spent my career designing and fixing enterprise platforms for global organisations, and I have seen finance transformation projects succeed and fail at close range. What you will learn here comes from real implementations, not theory.

**Story and mission.** Early in my career I watched a brilliant finance team fail, not because of the software, but because nobody could see how the pieces fit. That day I decided to build a map. My mission: Architecting for a Better World, one well-designed finance function at a time.

**The model.** Here is the map. [show slide] Twelve product academies: S/4HANA Finance, Controlling, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Document and Reporting Compliance, GRC, Treasury and Risk, Analytics Cloud Planning, Group Reporting and Consolidation, Analytics Cloud for Finance, Business AI with Joule, and Integration Suite. Eleven GICS sectors: Energy, Materials, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary, Consumer Staples, Health Care, Financial Services, Information Technology, Communication Services, Utilities, and Real Estate. Each course ID follows one rule: Series dash Sector, like AFR1-EN. 12 x 11 = 132.

We compress the twelve into six concepts of two academies each, so every concept covers 22 courses. Learn six concepts, translate through one sector lens, and you have covered all 132.

**Take notes** now. Presence matters: if you are not writing, you are not learning. [pause] Reinforcement: say the formula once more out loud.

**CTA.** Take two minutes now and share your notes below. Three lines is enough: what you learned, what surprised you, what you will do. Next video: Self Discovery.

**On-screen / slide cues:** Slide: 12 academies grid; Slide: 11 sectors ring; Slide: course ID rule [Series]-[Sector]
**Closing CTA:** Take two minutes now and share your notes below. Three lines is enough: what you learned, what surprised you, what you will do.

### Day 1 V5: Community Context
*Duration: 8-10 min*

**Hook:** A course is a video. A community is a transformation.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

**Code of Honour.** [show slide] We honour time, truth and each other. We share what we learn. We credit sources. We never share confidential client data.

**Guidelines.** Do: share notes, ask questions, help one person. Don't: spam, pitch, share employer secrets, or ghost your partner.
