## 1. The decision behind the numbers

A renewable-project turbine has arrived, but installation, testing and readiness evidence are incomplete. A project team wants to transfer every accumulated cost to a finished asset to close its budget.

**Workshop lead:** Aditi Sen — Group Financial Controller (OVE-FI-OS-P01, new fictional learning persona).  
**Counterpart:** Carlos Mendes — VP Asset Reliability and Maintenance (OVE-P09).

Carlos distinguishes delivery from technical readiness. Aditi asks which costs and dates meet the applicable approved accounting policy and which evidence supports that judgment.

They record the asset/component, project costs, readiness evidence and reviewer decision. They distinguish technical equipment records, financial asset records and policy-dependent capitalization/depreciation decisions.

**Pause:** Which fact would change your decision? Ask the counterpart one question before proposing a product.

OmniVerse is a fictional energy enterprise with 68,000 employees in 42 countries. Existing OVE-P identities come from the enterprise register. Aditi, Rohan and Amira are explicitly new fictional operational personas with OVE-FI-OS-P identifiers. All new dialogue and incidents are learning designs. Country, entity, accounting basis and approved policy must be established rather than assumed.

## 2. Origins and evolution, 1970–2025

These closed, non-overlapping periods are teaching lenses, not invention dates or universal adoption deadlines. Practices coexist. Before OmniVerse's fictional 1978 founding, examples describe the wider domain.

- **1970–1989:** Physical asset registers and paper depreciation schedules support periodic controls.
- **1990–1999:** Asset spreadsheets track cost and useful-life assumptions; technical and financial identities diverge.
- **2000–2009:** ERP asset subledgers connect acquisition and ledger accounting; components and readiness still need evidence.
- **2010–2019:** Project/maintenance integration improves lifecycle visibility; operational and accounting milestones differ.
- **2020–2025:** Assisted classification suggests treatments; policy interpretation and significant estimates retain accountable review.

**Mindset:** faster processing is useful when the evidence and decision remain trustworthy. Who bears the cost of an incorrect result: a supplier, worker, customer, investor or community?

## 3. AI-driven horizon, 2026–2050

**2026 starting experiment:** let assistance prepare a low-risk draft, match suggestion or evidence summary. Test its limits before permitting consequential actions.

**2030 checkpoint:** compare the assisted process with a human-led baseline. Require source evidence, approved authority and a usable challenge route.

**2035–2050 scenario:** Imagine a 2050 digital twin supplying readiness and condition evidence. It may support a decision, but a model's prediction cannot itself authorize a financial valuation or useful-life change.

**Alternative:** If technical evidence is delayed, keep provisional status and an owned decision rather than equate invoice or delivery date with readiness.

These are possibilities and test questions, not forecasts, accounting interpretations or product commitments. Autonomy means bounded assistance under approved authority.

## 4. Smallest useful architecture

**Capability — OVE-FI08-OS-CAP01:** evidence-based asset lifecycle accounting.

**Value stages — OVE-FI08-OS-VS01:** Accumulate cost → inspect evidence → assess policy → approve classification/date → post → reconcile → review.

**Objects:** project cost, asset/component ID, readiness evidence, capitalization proposal, useful-life assumption and ledger.

**Application responsibility:** Project systems own cost accumulation; engineering/operations supply readiness evidence; accounting owns recognition and valuation under applicable policy; asset subledger owns financial records; maintenance owns technical equipment.

**Boundary:** Delivery, commissioning and accounting availability/readiness criteria require deliberate mapping. No universal capitalization date, rate or useful life is prescribed here.

The twelve FI themes are a curriculum axis, separate from the twelve architecture streams. SAP Finance is an optional demonstration context; edition, licensing and local requirements remain to validate. Live configuration, posting and payment execution are outside this paper sprint.

## 5. One Decision Card

Use synthetic case **OVE-FI08-OS-SYN001**. Keep the response to one card or screen.

1. **Need:** one business decision, affected population and missing fact.
2. **Evidence:** source IDs, entity, period/effective date, currency or other relevant unit, and approved policy owner.
3. **Ownership:** source application, accountable decision maker and receiving owner.
4. **Gate:** prerequisite, authorized next state and failure/hold condition.
5. **Alternative:** Compare automatic capitalization at delivery with a reviewed evidence gate for the appropriate asset/component.
6. **Recovery:** exception owner, escalation and safe correction/retry.
7. **Proof:** the three actual paper results, measure and stop/revise/proceed decision.

**Requirement — OVE-FI08-OS-REQ01:** link the approved asset treatment and relevant date to technical evidence, cost scope and accounting-policy authority.

**Decision — OVE-FI08-OS-ADR01:** record your choice, trade-off and the evidence that would make you change it.

## 6. Three tabletop tests

### T01 — Delivery exists but readiness evidence is missing

**Expected:** Hold unsupported final treatment; seek engineering and accounting-policy review.

### T02 — Routine maintenance is mixed with project costs

**Expected:** Separate the cost facts and apply approved policy; do not capitalize merely to meet budget.

### T03 — One component is replaced or retired

**Expected:** Trace component identity and policy-based retirement/replacement entries; reconcile technical and financial records.

Record **input → expected → actual paper result → evidence note → revision → retest**. Tests OVE-FI08-OS-T01/T02/T03 support REQ01; expected results are not observed tenant evidence.

**Measure — OVE-FI08-OS-KPI01:** asset decisions with complete cost/readiness/policy evidence / decisions reviewed. Name the accountable operational owner, baseline, exclusions and as-of date before setting a target.

**Acceptance:** your card preserves the boundary, names accountable owners, handles all three tests after revisions and supports a 60-second explanation. Unresolved facts remain visible; no achieved financial benefit is claimed.

## 7. Your 120 minutes

- **0–20:** explore the history and explain the enduring problem.
- **20–45:** role-play the lead and counterpart; ask for evidence.
- **45–65:** map responsibilities and consult one resource selectively.
- **65–100:** complete the card and compare the alternative.
- **100–115:** run the three tests and revise.
- **115–120:** explain the decision and choose the next experiment.
