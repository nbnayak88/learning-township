## 1. The decision behind the numbers

A board dashboard treats OmniVerse's USD 635 million annual benefit categories as realized net cash and adds overlapping maintenance improvements from two programs. No owner can explain the baseline period.

**Workshop lead:** Michael Reed — Group Chief Financial Officer (OVE-P03).  
**Counterpart:** Dr. Leila Haddad — Head of Data Governance and AI (OVE-P16).

Michael asks where the cash-flow evidence is. Leila asks which definitions, sources, scope and exclusions produced the number.

They preserve the source's figures as unvalidated hypotheses, select one measure and trace it to underlying observations. They separate gross opportunity, costs, accounting effects, cash timing and attribution.

**Pause:** Which fact would change your decision? Ask the counterpart one question before proposing a product.

OmniVerse is a fictional energy enterprise with 68,000 employees in 42 countries. Existing OVE-P identities come from the enterprise register. Aditi, Rohan and Amira are explicitly new fictional operational personas with OVE-FI-OS-P identifiers. All new dialogue and incidents are learning designs. Country, entity, accounting basis and approved policy must be established rather than assumed.

## 2. Origins and evolution, 1970–2025

These closed, non-overlapping periods are teaching lenses, not invention dates or universal adoption deadlines. Practices coexist. Before OmniVerse's fictional 1978 founding, examples describe the wider domain.

- **1970–1989:** Periodic statements and local reports summarize results; context travels through explanation.
- **1990–1999:** Spreadsheet dashboards speed analysis but can hide formulas and definitions.
- **2000–2009:** Warehouses and ERP reporting reconcile dimensions; measure semantics still differ.
- **2010–2019:** Self-service analytics broadens use; attractive dashboards can conceal lineage gaps.
- **2020–2025:** Generated narratives summarize results quickly; unsupported causal and value claims remain possible.

**Mindset:** faster processing is useful when the evidence and decision remain trustworthy. Who bears the cost of an incorrect result: a supplier, worker, customer, investor or community?

## 3. AI-driven horizon, 2026–2050

**2026 starting experiment:** let assistance prepare a low-risk draft, match suggestion or evidence summary. Test its limits before permitting consequential actions.

**2030 checkpoint:** compare the assisted process with a human-led baseline. Require source evidence, approved authority and a usable challenge route.

**2035–2050 scenario:** Imagine a 2050 executive assistant explaining financial performance from evidence graphs. It should expose uncertainty, missing baselines and overlapping attribution rather than make a confident invented valuation.

**Alternative:** If sources cannot be reconciled, publish a qualified smaller measure with limitations instead of a false enterprise total.

These are possibilities and test questions, not forecasts, accounting interpretations or product commitments. Autonomy means bounded assistance under approved authority.

## 4. Smallest useful architecture

**Capability — OVE-FI10-OS-CAP01:** governed financial decision intelligence.

**Value stages — OVE-FI10-OS-VS01:** Define question → select measure → trace sources → reconcile → assess attribution → explain → review.

**Objects:** measure definition, baseline, period, currency, source, benefit owner, enabling cost and attribution.

**Application responsibility:** Ledgers own recorded actuals; business owners substantiate outcomes; analytics owns definitions/lineage; Finance validates value claims. FI10 reports evidence; FI06 models forecasts and FI07 owns management allocation logic.

**Boundary:** The enterprise USD 900M investment and USD 635M annual categories do not establish realized ROI, payback or NPV without supporting cash flows and assumptions.

The twelve FI themes are a curriculum axis, separate from the twelve architecture streams. SAP Finance is an optional demonstration context; edition, licensing and local requirements remain to validate. Live configuration, posting and payment execution are outside this paper sprint.

## 5. One Decision Card

Use synthetic case **OVE-FI10-OS-SYN001**. Keep the response to one card or screen.

1. **Need:** one business decision, affected population and missing fact.
2. **Evidence:** source IDs, entity, period/effective date, currency or other relevant unit, and approved policy owner.
3. **Ownership:** source application, accountable decision maker and receiving owner.
4. **Gate:** prerequisite, authorized next state and failure/hold condition.
5. **Alternative:** Compare an aggregated headline benefit with a reconciled measure and visible overlap/uncertainty.
6. **Recovery:** exception owner, escalation and safe correction/retry.
7. **Proof:** the three actual paper results, measure and stop/revise/proceed decision.

**Requirement — OVE-FI10-OS-REQ01:** publish one measure with baseline, population, currency/period, source, owner, costs and attribution limits.

**Decision — OVE-FI10-OS-ADR01:** record your choice, trade-off and the evidence that would make you change it.

## 6. Three tabletop tests

### T01 — Two initiatives claim the same cost reduction

**Expected:** Identify overlap and agree attribution before aggregating.

### T02 — Revenue/profit change is labelled realized cash

**Expected:** Require cash timing and cost evidence; show the distinctions.

### T03 — Source period or currency is inconsistent

**Expected:** Reconcile definitions and conversions; retain the as-of date and method.

Record **input → expected → actual paper result → evidence note → revision → retest**. Tests OVE-FI10-OS-T01/T02/T03 support REQ01; expected results are not observed tenant evidence.

**Measure — OVE-FI10-OS-KPI01:** material value claims with complete baseline/source/owner/cost/attribution evidence / claims reviewed. Name the accountable operational owner, baseline, exclusions and as-of date before setting a target.

**Acceptance:** your card preserves the boundary, names accountable owners, handles all three tests after revisions and supports a 60-second explanation. Unresolved facts remain visible; no achieved financial benefit is claimed.
