One learner promise

Design a critical-role succession decision that separates nomination, demonstrated readiness and delegated authority. In 120 minutes, complete one Critical-Role Continuity Build Card and exactly three tabletop tests. All work uses synthetic data; no tenant configuration or live utility action is required.

Read the case accurately

OmniGrid is a aspirational utility. The existing case stipulates 38,000 staff across 17 countries, covering generation, networks, retail energy and water services. These are aspirational source baselines, not verified real-world facts. Existing persona names and IDs below are preserved. This incident, records and choices are newly introduced educational material. The source case owns enterprise facts; its success measures are targets, not achieved results. The 20% versus 30% restoration targets, metric populations, and USD970M investment / USD360M annual-value assumptions remain unresolved in the source register. No site counts, country laws, payroll rates or operational authority are invented.

One persona-led story

Arjun Mehta (OGU-P04), Chief Operations and Grid Officer works with Nadia Fernandes (OGU-P07), Group CHRO.

A control-room leadership vacancy appears in a synthetic continuity exercise. Nadia’s succession slate identifies a ready-now candidate. Arjun needs a continuity plan but cannot treat a talent label as proof that the candidate may take every operational decision. The candidate wants a development opportunity and a transparent explanation of the remaining gap.

Choose before the reveal: Appoint directly from the talent label, delay all development, or distinguish emergency delegation from a development plan? Write your first answer on the card.

Evidence reveal: The evidence packet supports people leadership but lacks a current observed emergency exercise for this role. Arjun requests the role’s authorized interim arrangements and a supervised development opportunity. Nadia keeps the successor nomination, revises the readiness statement and restricts access to sensitive assessments.

Counterexample: High performance in one role does not establish readiness for another role or confer operational authority.

Revise the answer on the same card. What changed, what remains unknown and whose decision is protected? The proposed response is open to challenge when learners provide better evidence.

Application responsibility, data ownership and decision rights

Application responsibility Accountable owner Human decision and boundary
Succession / development Talent owner Calibrated panel recommends; nomination is not appointment.
Operational delegation Accountable operations owner Authorizes interim duty within verified qualifications and limits.
Learning evidence / access Assessor and security owners Assess demonstration and restrict talent data to legitimate viewers.

Business owners define policy and authoritative facts; software stores or processes them. Detailed health, pay and candidate data stay outside the shared card unless necessary and authorized. Local delegations, jurisdiction and operational procedures need discovery. AI may draft a reminder or flag an inconsistency within permission; it cannot approve hiring, ratings, pay, work or access. HR, learning, IAM and utility operational systems retain distinct responsibilities.

One synthetic build experiment

Critical-Role Continuity Build Card.

Synthetic inputs: UT-ROLE400, synthetic nominee UT-P400, leadership evidence, missing emergency-exercise evidence and an unfilled delegation record.

Single build card Complete once
Purpose and evolution State the people problem, one lasting historical lesson and the decision being supported.
Design 1 Critical capability, vacancy impact and role-specific criteria
Design 2 Nomination, evidence currency and readiness gaps
Design 3 Interim delegation owner and explicit authority limits
Design 4 Practical development, reassessment and confidential feedback
Choice and counterexample Record the initial choice, an alternative, the evidence reveal, the revised decision and a reason to reject your preferred design.
Exception and recovery Name the accountable role, worker notification, escalation, approved fallback and the condition for reconciliation. Missing connectivity or evidence grants no additional authority.
Privacy and local variation Record minimum data, purpose, permitted viewers, retention question and one justified local variant. Offer a route without a personal device.
Tests and learning evidence For T01–T03 record input → expected → observed → evidence → revision. Leave observations unfilled until the exercise is actually performed.

Learning acceptance: one defensible card, three observed tabletop records after any revision, and a 60-second explanation of owners, dates/states and human authority. A protected-decision bypass requires redesign. Completeness alone does not prove correctness.

Exactly three tabletop tests

Test Proposed expected result Record evidence; observations initially blank
OGU-EDU-HR04-T01 — Talent label without demonstration Keep readiness conditional and define the missing evidence. Criterion-to-evidence mapping; observed result and revision to be recorded by learner.
OGU-EDU-HR04-T02 — Emergency vacancy Escalate through the authorized continuity process; no automatic expanded authority. Interim owner, scope and review condition; observed result and revision to be recorded by learner.
OGU-EDU-HR04-T03 — Overbroad talent-data access Limit access and provide a confidential correction route. Minimum fields and permitted viewers; observed result and revision to be recorded by learner.

These are planned learning tests, not executed system, security, operational-safety or legal validations. Publication creates no pass results.

AI-driven exploration: scenarios, not predictions

Scenario window Possibility to investigate Question or boundary
2026–2030 — Assist An assistant flags missing or inconsistent HR04 evidence. Can it explain the gap using current, authorized sources?
2031–2040 — Connect Governed services exchange relevant evidence across owners. Does each target confirm the right business state without excess personal data?
2041–2050 — Adapt A system proposes revised workflows as utility roles and conditions change. Can accountable people inspect, challenge, stop and reverse the proposal?

These are scenarios, not predictions, guaranteed capabilities, deployments or product roadmaps. Competing future: fragmented regional systems and local human coordination remain more workable than a broadly connected assistant because evidence, trust or connectivity are insufficient. Falsification question: in a comparison using the same synthetic cases, would the proposed assistance produce more unsupported decisions, privacy exposure or unresolved exceptions than a simpler governed process? If yes, revise or reject the hypothesis.
