The story and learner promise

In 120 minutes: Design one employee journey with a clear completion state and an accessible human fallback.

One persona-led story · One build card · Exactly three tabletop tests. Use synthetic records without a tenant.

Nadia Fernandes (OCN-P07), Group CHRO, follows a fiber technician submitting a time correction from a shared device. Marcus Okoro (OCN-P08), Service Assurance and NOC Director, needs reliable coverage information. The portal spins and the worker presses submit again.

Choose first: Show success immediately, ask for another submission, or expose pending status with a safe reconciliation route?

Evidence reveal: The backend accepted the first request but the device lost the response. The proposed journey retrieves status by request key, avoids duplication and clears personal data after sign-out.

Counterexample: A polished portal or successful click cannot prove the receiving service completed the request.

Sources and architecture learning

NIST AI Risk Management Framework — Which risk, evidence and human oversight boundary should your proposed assistant preserve?

W3C WCAG overview — Can the worker understand and complete the journey with an accessible alternative? These are selective learning references, not a compliance assessment.

Educational alignment: TOGAF supports architecture options and decisions; BIZBOK connects capabilities to value; BABOK connects needs and acceptance; DAMA-DMBOK supports ownership, quality and lineage; COBIT supports accountability and controls; relevant ISO/IEC security and quality principles support least privilege, controlled change and evidence. No certification or compliance is claimed. HR01–HR12 themes, the twelve architecture streams and the seven enterprise transformation streams are separate classification axes. Cross-reference them without treating them as interchangeable.
