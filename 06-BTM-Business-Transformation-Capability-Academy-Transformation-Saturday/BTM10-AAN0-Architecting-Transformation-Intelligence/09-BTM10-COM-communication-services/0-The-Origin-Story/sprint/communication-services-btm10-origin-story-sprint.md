# The transformation dashboard was green; the benefits were not visible
## BTM10 — Architecting Transformation Intelligence

**SuccessLabs Academy · OmniConnect Media Networks Ltd. · Level 0 → Pre-Foundation**  
**IIL — Industry Immersion Lab · Version 1.0 · 10 November 2026**

**Format:** Origin Story Sprint · 120 minutes · Free — Community/Citizen Edition

> **Your promise:** In two hours, design one transformation report that shows outcomes and unknowns, not only schedule.

**One story · One build card · Three tabletop tests**

## 1. Start with the story

A dashboard for a platform change at a media and networks group shows every workstream green, and the sponsor asks where the benefits are.

**Transformation lead:** Mei Lin — Subscriber Data and Consent Lead (OCN-P09).  
**Counterpart:** David Chen — Group CFO (OCN-P02).  
**Business or data owner:** Daniel Weber — Director Enterprise Architecture (OCN-P12).

Mei Lin asks what must be true before the team decides. David Chen asks which evidence and owner support the decision. Daniel Weber asks for the outcome that matters to the people affected.

**Evidence reveal:** In the synthetic data, green reflects schedule only, and benefit evidence is missing for most workstreams.

**Pause:** What must be true for this decision to stand? Which evidence is missing, and who can confirm it? Ask the counterpart one discovery question before choosing a solution.

**Case boundary:** OmniConnect Media Networks Ltd. is an aspirational case study (USD 37B communications group; 62,000 employees; 28 countries; 210M subscriptions). These incidents, services and dialogue are proposed learning scenarios, not corporate history, confirmed deployments or achieved results. Preserve the enterprise persona IDs above. Country-specific permissions, contracts and operating rules require validation; this sprint does not invent them.

## 2. Trace the origin: 1970–2026

These overlapping periods are teaching lenses, not invention dates or a universal adoption sequence. Earlier practices can coexist with newer ones. Pre-1978 scenes describe the wider domain, not OmniConnect Media Networks Ltd. history.

- **1970s–1980s:** Progress is reported verbally.
- **1980s–1990s:** Programme reports track milestones.
- **2000s–2010s:** Dashboards spread; status is schedule-based.
- **2020s–2026:** Assisted analysis speeds reporting; outcomes, unknowns and owners still need people.

**Mindset shift:** start with the decision, its evidence and its owner, then choose the tool or programme. Digitizing an unclear decision can accelerate the same mistake.

**Pause:** Which old problem survives in a newer interface? Who bears its cost?

## 3. Explore possible futures: 2030–2050

**2030 experiment:** allow assistance to draft options and flag gaps; retain the evidence gate, a named decision owner and a challenge route.

**2040 question:** if the enterprise and its partners share more signals, can each decision carry its evidence and owner without losing trust or choice?

**2050 scene:** Imagine an enterprise that adjusts within agreed boundaries. Providers may act faster, but people must own the purpose, the trade-offs and the right to stop.

**Competing future:** A simpler, well-owned way of working with trained people may beat a larger transformation on reliability, cost and trust.

All future passages are design hypotheses, not forecasts or product roadmaps. Test a useful part today rather than assume the future will arrive as described.

## 4. Draw the smallest useful architecture

**Capability — OCN-BTM10-OS-CAP01:** outcome-based transformation reporting. A capability is an ability; it is not an application or a sequence of steps.

**Value stages — OCN-BTM10-OS-VS01:** Define outcomes → map sources → measure → reconcile → report → decide.

**Objects to distinguish:** outcome, benefit measure, source, status and owner.

**Proposed responsibility:** The sponsor owns outcomes; programme analytics owns definitions; workstream owners own data. BTM10 owns the reporting decision, not the dashboard tool.

**Protected boundary:** A green status reflects schedule, not benefit or outcome.

Transformation, architecture and platform applications are optional demonstration candidates, chosen after requirements. No product is assumed installed, licensed or able to cover the whole programme. This sprint focuses on application responsibility while referencing data, integration and security owners.

## 5. Build one Decision Card

Use synthetic case **OCN-BTM10-OS-SYN001**. Synthetic dashboard COM-DB300, workstreams COM-WS300 to COM-WS303, benefit evidence missing. Keep everything on one card or screen.

1. **Need:** the outcome, affected people and one missing fact.
2. **Evidence:** relevant object IDs, source, validity or effective time, data purpose and permitted access.
3. **Ownership:** business decision owner, authoritative application and receiving owner.
4. **Gate:** prerequisite, allowed next state, authorized actor and the message anyone affected will see.
5. **Alternative:** Compare publishing the green dashboard with adding outcome measures and showing unknowns.
6. **Exception:** one failure state, escalation owner and a safe recovery or assisted route.
7. **Proof:** the three results below, one measure and a stop, revise or proceed decision.

**Requirement — OCN-BTM10-OS-REQ01:** link one outcome-reporting experiment hypothesis to an owned intervention and observed follow-up, including negative evidence.

**Decision — OCN-BTM10-OS-ADR01:** record your chosen option and the alternative from item 5, one trade-off, and the evidence that would change your choice.

## 6. Run three tabletop tests

### T01 — Green reflects schedule only

**Expected:** Add outcome and benefit measures with owners.

### T02 — A workstream reports no data

**Expected:** Show it as unknown, not green.

### T03 — Sources use different dates

**Expected:** Normalize with a stated rule.

For each, record **input → expected → actual paper result → evidence note → revision → retest**. These are sprint-local tests OCN-BTM10-OS-T01/T02/T03, supporting OCN-BTM10-OS-REQ01. A completed lesson is not an executed tenant test.

**Measure — OCN-BTM10-OS-KPI01:** Share of workstreams with outcome measures and an owner; define window and exclusions. Assign Mei Lin as proposed measure owner, establish the baseline and exclusions, and review after the experiment. No numeric improvement is asserted.

**Acceptance:** the card identifies source and owner, preserves the protected boundary, handles all three tests after documented revisions and explains the decision in 60 seconds. Missing evidence remains visible. Record what is still unverified.

## 7. Use your 120 minutes

- **0–20:** read the origin timeline; explain what changed and what did not.
- **20–45:** role-play the story; ask the lead, counterpart and voice one useful question each.
- **45–65:** draw responsibilities and consult one resource selectively.
- **65–100:** complete the single Decision Card and compare the alternative.
- **100–115:** run the three tests; revise from observed paper behavior.
- **115–120:** explain the decision in 60 seconds and choose the next experiment.

All reading fits within these blocks. Do not complete an entire product course during the sprint.
