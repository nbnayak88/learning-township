**1. What is the concept?**
LeanIX Enterprise Architecture gives a living map of applications, capabilities, data and technology, and how they relate. Master Data Governance defines ownership, quality rules and workflows for the core data the enterprise shares: customers, suppliers, materials, employees. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Transformation on an unknown landscape duplicates systems, breaks integrations and leaves master data in conflict. Architecture and data governance are the foundation that every later change either respects or pays for.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities group discovered it ran four systems for the same capability, found only when the landscape was mapped. Retiring two of them funded the next phase, and one governed master data process removed a year of reconciliation work. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: A trusted map of the application and capability landscape; Rationalised systems and reduced duplication; Consistent, owned and high-quality master data; Faster, lower-risk change decisions. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Financials needs strict data lineage and regulatory architecture; Consumer Staples needs consistent product and supplier master data.
