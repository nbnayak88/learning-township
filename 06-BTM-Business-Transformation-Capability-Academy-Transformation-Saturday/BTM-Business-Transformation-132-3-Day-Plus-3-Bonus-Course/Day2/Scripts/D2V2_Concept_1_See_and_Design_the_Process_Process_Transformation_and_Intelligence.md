**1. What is the concept?**
The Signavio Process Transformation Suite helps you model, design and govern business processes end to end. Process Intelligence and Process Manager show how processes actually run, using event data to expose delays, rework and deviations from the model. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Most transformation programmes design the process people think they run, not the one they actually run. Without evidence, you automate waste and argue about opinions.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A materials company believed its order process took five days. Process mining showed the median was nine, with a third of orders touched by rework. The redesign targeted three real causes and cut cycle time by almost half. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: A shared, documented view of how work flows; Evidence of bottlenecks, rework and compliance gaps; Redesign priorities ranked by real impact; A living process model instead of a shelf document. [on screen: four benefits]
