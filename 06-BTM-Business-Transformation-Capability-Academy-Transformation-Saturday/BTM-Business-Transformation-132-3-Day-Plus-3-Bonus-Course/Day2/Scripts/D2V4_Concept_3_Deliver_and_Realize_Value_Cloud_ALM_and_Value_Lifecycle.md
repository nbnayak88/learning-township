**1. What is the concept?**
Cloud ALM (CALM) manages the implementation and operation lifecycle: scope, requirements, tests, deployment and monitoring. Value Lifecycle Manager tracks the business case from promised value to realised value, with owners and measures. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Projects celebrate go-live, while the business case quietly decays. Without delivery discipline you overrun, and without value tracking nobody can prove the transformation paid off.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A programme went live on time and under budget, yet two years later nobody could say what it had delivered. A later initiative tracked each promised benefit with an owner, and the board saw value realised every quarter. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Transparent delivery from scope to deployment; Fewer test and release surprises; Business case benefits owned and tracked; Credible proof of value for sponsors and the board. [on screen: four benefits]
