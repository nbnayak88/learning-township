**Q1. Which skill covers using AI safely on people data?**
- A. AI prompting and AI governance for transformation
- B. Spreadsheet formatting
- C. Journey mapping
- D. Process mining
*Answer: A*

**Q2. Which two series make Concept 1?**
- A. ABT1 and APM3
- B. ARP2 and ATD6
- C. AIO8 and ARG9

**Q3. Why use process mining before redesign?**
- A. It shows how work really flows, not how people think it does
- B. To remove process owners
- C. To avoid documentation

**Q4. Why track value after go-live?**
- A. Business cases decay unless benefits have owners and measures
- B. To reduce headcount

  *Answer: ABT1 Signavio Process Transformation Suite and APM3 Process Intelligence and Process Manager*

  *Answer: You end up automating waste instead of fixing real causes*

  *Answer: A living map of applications, capabilities, data and technology*

  *Answer: ARP2 LeanIX Enterprise Architecture and ATD6 Master Data Governance*
- Why govern master data?  
  *Answer: Shared data needs ownership, quality rules and workflows*
- What does Cloud ALM manage?  
  *Answer: Scope, requirements, tests, deployment and operation of the solution*
- Which series cover Concept 3?  
  *Answer: ACH4 Cloud ALM and AVP5 Value Lifecycle Manager*

  *Answer: Business cases decay unless benefits have owners and measures*
