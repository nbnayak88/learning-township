## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Health Care and Financials design processes around regulation and audit; Industrials designs around plants, projects and service.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Financials needs strict data lineage and regulatory architecture; Consumer Staples needs consistent product and supplier master data.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Energy and Industrials run long, capital-heavy programmes; Information Technology runs fast, iterative releases.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Financials and Health Care carry the heaviest control requirements; Consumer Discretionary integrates many channels and partners.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Health Care and Energy adoption depends on frontline and field workers; Information Technology adoption depends on developer and product teams.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Financials needs explainability and model governance; Industrials applies AI to operations and maintenance processes.
