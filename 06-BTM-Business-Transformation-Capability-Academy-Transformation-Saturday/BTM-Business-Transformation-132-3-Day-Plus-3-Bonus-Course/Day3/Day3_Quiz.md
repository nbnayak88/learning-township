## Concept check (short answer)
- What does the Integration Suite provide?  
  *Answer: Managed, monitored interfaces across applications, data and partners*
- Which series cover Concept 4?  
  *Answer: AIO8 Integration Suite and ARG9 GRC*
- Why design integration and control together?  
  *Answer: Transformation raises exposure unless both are designed in*
- Why do transformations underperform?  
  *Answer: Adoption lags, not because the design is wrong*
- Which series cover Concept 5?  
  *Answer: AAN0 Analytics Cloud and Databricks; AEC2 Change Enablement and Adoption*
- What does measurement allow?  
  *Answer: Seeing adoption gaps early so they can be closed*
- What does the Business AI Platform provide?  
  *Answer: A governed foundation to build, run and monitor AI use cases*
- Which series cover Concept 6?  
  *Answer: AAI1 Business AI, Joule, AI Agents; ATE7 Business AI Platform*
- Why not run isolated AI pilots?  
  *Answer: They become scattered, risky experiments without governance*
