**1. What is the concept?**
Analytics Cloud with Databricks measures transformation progress and outcomes with trusted dashboards and predictive insight. Change Enablement and Adoption helps people understand, accept and use the new ways of working through guidance, training and reinforcement. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Most transformations underperform because adoption lags, not because the design is wrong. Without measurement you cannot see the lag, and without enablement you cannot close it.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer staples company watched usage data in the first month and found one region barely using the new process. Targeted coaching there lifted adoption to match the rest, and the planned benefits followed. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Visible adoption and outcome measures; Early detection of resistance and gaps; Targeted training and support where it matters; Faster time to value and lasting change. [on screen: four benefits]
