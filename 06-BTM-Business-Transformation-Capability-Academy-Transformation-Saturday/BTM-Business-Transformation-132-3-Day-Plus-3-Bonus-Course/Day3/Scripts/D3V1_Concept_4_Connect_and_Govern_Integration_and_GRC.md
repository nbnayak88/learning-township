**1. What is the concept?**
The Integration Suite connects applications, data and partners with managed, monitored interfaces. GRC defines policies, risks, controls and access rules so the transformed enterprise stays compliant and auditable. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Point-to-point integrations become a hidden tangle, and unmanaged risk grows with every new system. Transformation raises exposure unless integration and control are designed together.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A group replaced scores of custom interfaces with a governed integration layer and built access controls into the redesigned processes. Audit findings dropped and new acquisitions were connected in weeks. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Reliable, reusable interfaces across the landscape; Policies and controls built into the new processes; Audit-ready evidence and fewer findings; Clean-core extensions that stay upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
