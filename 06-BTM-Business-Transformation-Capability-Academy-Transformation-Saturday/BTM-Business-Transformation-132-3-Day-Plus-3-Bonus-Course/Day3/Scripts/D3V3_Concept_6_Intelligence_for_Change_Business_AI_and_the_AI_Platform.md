**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and recommendations into transformation work and everyday processes. The Business AI Platform (BAIP) provides the governed foundation to build, run and monitor AI use cases safely. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI pilots without a platform become a scatter of risky experiments. AI needs trustworthy data, clear ownership, and governance for explainability, security and cost.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A transformation office used an assistant to summarise workshop outputs and draft process documentation, but only after the platform gave it governed access to the approved models and data. Productivity rose and security signed off. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster analysis, documentation and decisions; A governed route from pilot to production; Trusted, explainable AI with audit trails; AI aligned with the transformation roadmap, not beside it. [on screen: four benefits]
