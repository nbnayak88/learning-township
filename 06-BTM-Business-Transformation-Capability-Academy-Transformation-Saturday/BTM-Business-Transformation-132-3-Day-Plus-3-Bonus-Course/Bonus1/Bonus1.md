### B1 V2: In-Depth Concept: Build the Business Transformation Business Case
*Duration: 20-30 min*

**Hook:** Business Case is where theory becomes income and impact.

**Take notes.** Keep a page open. Write three things as we go and share them towards the end. [pause]

**1. What is the concept?** (about 3 min)
A business case is a one-page, CEO and CFO-ready argument that links a transformation change to money, risk and growth. It names the problem, the cost of doing nothing, the benefit, the investment and the proof. [show slide]

**2. Why is it important?** (about 4 min)
Good designs die in budget meetings. Leaders fund outcomes, not modules. If you can express a business transformation idea in their language (cost, risk, revenue, speed), your design gets built. Here is what I have seen. A mid-size firm had a sound business transformation design that stalled for a year. One page with three numbers and one owner per benefit got it approved in a single meeting.

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way** (about 3 min)
When this is done well: Faster funding and sponsor commitment; Clear success measures from day one; Fewer scope arguments because value is agreed; A career-defining skill that moves you from consultant to advisor. [on screen: benefits]

**4. How to do it? Tactical** (about 10-12 min)
Follow these steps in order. Pause the video at each step and do it. [screen share]
1. Pick one of the 132 courses and sector, for example Energy (ABT1-SIGN-ENE-FND) or Financials (ARP2-LNX-FIN-FND).
2. Write the problem in one sentence with one number.
3. Quantify the cost of doing nothing (cost, risk, lost revenue).
4. List 3 benefits and attach a KPI and an owner to each.
5. Estimate the investment (people, licences, change) and a range, not a point.
6. Add risks and the simplest mitigation for each.
7. Write the 60-second story for the CEO and CFO: problem, impact, option, ask.

**5. Resources and tool** (about 3 min)
Your tool for this bonus: **Transformation Value Case Canvas (Excel) from the Codex**. Open it from the Codex linked below. [on screen: tool walkthrough] Assignment: write a one-page transformation value case for one course ID of your choice. Submit the canvas and your 60-second story.

**CTA.** Share your notes below: three lines on what you learned, what surprised you and what you will do first. Next video: the outro.
