## 1. The question this file answers

> *How does the architecture support sustainability reporting, and how does it keep the footprint of IT and AI itself visible?*

**What this file shows:** two separate topics for OmniHealth: (1) data products that support sustainability reporting, and (2) the footprint of the IT and AI estate, with levers, an owner and an evidence rule.

**What it does not show:** an emissions figure, a footprint measurement or a reduction claim.

## 2. Case anchor (source facts)

| Attribute | Case baseline |
|---|---|
| Scale | USD 34B healthcare group; 126,000 staff; 19 countries |
| Network | 86 hospitals; 440 clinics; 670 diagnostic centres |
| Business mix | Acute 38%; primary 22%; diagnostics 18%; digital and pharmacy 12%; research and value-based 10% |

**Decision under discussion (source):** Select a bounded, non-clinical care-coordination pilot and evidence gates before enterprise expansion.

**Illustrative trigger (source):** A referral crosses facilities without clear ownership; the patient repeats information and a dashboard cannot reconcile episode status (invented; no actual patient incident is asserted).

## 6. Two topics, kept apart

| Topic | What it covers | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting data | Business data used for sustainability reporting (energy, emissions, supplier evidence) | Owned by business functions; follows reporting rules |
| IT and AI footprint | Energy and emissions from hosting, pipelines and model usage | Owned by technology and operations; different data sources |

**Sector emphasis:** Keep sustainability analysis administrative; clinical systems are out of scope.

## 7. Sustainability data products (new design)

| Data product | Main source | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Facility energy view | Facilities records | Clinical areas excluded or unknown |
| Supply and waste records | Supply chain data | Unclear scope |
| IT and AI footprint | Hosting records | Unmeasured assumptions |

Each product follows the Demo 07 rule: one owner, a quality rule and a visible status. Every figure shown carries its basis (measured, allocated or estimated) and its period.

## 8. Green IT levers (new design)

| Lever | Idea | Link to other demos | Owner (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right-size non-production | Switch off or shrink unused systems | Demo 11 cost drivers | OHS-P03 Dr. Leila Morgan |
| Retention rules | Delete data nobody needs | Demo 07 data products | OHS-P09 Priya Nair |
| AI cost per use | Choose the smallest model that passes evaluation; cache repeated answers | Demos 03 and 09 | OHS-P09 Priya Nair |
| Event and batch volume | Avoid chatty integrations; reduce redundant replays | Demo 05 | OHS-P13 Daniel Weber |
| Peak scheduling | Move heavy jobs away from peaks where the business allows | Demo 06 rhythm | OHS-P03 Dr. Leila Morgan |
| Hosting choices | Ask providers for footprint data; record the method | Demo 10 paths | OHS-P05 Sofia Tan |

## 9. Measurement approach (no values stated)

| Item | Design | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Use a recognised accounting method (for example the Greenhouse Gas Protocol corporate standard) and record which scopes are in or out | Method to be agreed |
| Data centre efficiency | Ask providers for facility efficiency data (ISO/IEC 30134 series is reading material; verify) | Not collected |
| Per-assistant footprint | Estimate from usage and provider data; label as estimate | Not measured |
| Reporting basis | measured, allocated or estimated, shown with each figure | Rule |

## Three tabletop tests

A tabletop test is a paper walk-through, not a technical test of a running system.

| Test | Setup | Expected finding |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Clinical scope creep | A footprint study reaches clinical systems | Out of scope; owner decides |
| T2 Unclear waste scope | Waste data excludes sites | Gap shown |
| T3 AI footprint ignored | A new assistant skips footprint | Gate asks for an estimate |
