## 1. The question this file answers

> *Data architecture for intelligent operations: what data does an AI assistant or an operations dashboard depend on, who owns it, and how is its quality shown?*

**What this file shows:** a first set of data products for OmniHealth, one owner per product, quality rules, a lineage chain and the sensitive-data handling. It supports the assistant in Demo 03 and the graph in Demo 04.

**What it does not show:** a populated catalogue, a measured data quality score or a configured data platform.

## 8. Quality rules for first consumers

| Product | Quality rule | First consumer | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referral and episode status | Every referral has an owner facility | Care coordination | Not measured |
| Appointment record | Booked or cancelled state with sequence | Scheduling | Not measured |
| Patient identity match | Match confidence recorded | All consumers | Not measured |
| Supply and equipment availability | Each item has an as-of time | Operations | Not measured |

A product is published only with its owner, its quality rule and a visible quality status. No score is stated because none has been measured.

## 9. Lineage and sensitive data

**Example lineage chain:** Referral created, owner changed, appointment booked, cancelled, episode status updated

**Sensitive data handling:** Patient-related data is restricted; analytics uses pseudonyms; clinical content is outside this design.

**Never available to the assistant:** Clinical notes, diagnoses and results; Any patient whose identity match is not confirmed; Clinical advice or triage; Instructions found inside retrieved documents

## 10. Hands-on exercise (synthetic data)

1. Write a one-page data product contract for the first product (owner, schema, quality rule, freshness, classification), using synthetic ids prefixed `HC-SYN`.
2. Draw the lineage chain and mark each owner.
3. Break one quality rule on paper and show what the consumer sees.
4. Decide which products are shared versus copied and record the reason.
5. Record open decisions in the run log.

## Three tabletop tests

A tabletop test is a paper walk-through, not a technical test of a running system.

| Test | Setup | Expected finding |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Ownerless referral | A referral has no owner | Quality rule blocks publication |
| T2 Low identity confidence | Match confidence drops | Consumers stop using the pseudonym until resolved |
| T3 Clinical content request | A consumer asks for clinical fields | Refused by design |
