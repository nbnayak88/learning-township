## 1. The question this file answers

> *Basis, HANA monitoring, transport automation, high availability and disaster recovery, and AI-driven operations: what may be automated, and what must stay with a person?*

**What this file shows:** a synthetic operations landscape for OmniGrid, six signals with a response and an autonomy tier, a change and transport policy, and the HA and DR questions to settle.

**What it does not show:** a monitored system, a tested failover or an automated remediation.

## 2. Case anchor (source facts)

| Attribute | Case baseline |
|---|---|
| Scale | USD 31B utility; 38,000 staff; 17 countries; 21M electricity, gas and water accounts |
| Business mix | Renewables 28%; networks 27%; retail 18%; conventional and flexible 15%; water and environment 12% |
| Source problem | Fragmented OT, field, customer and market data; variable generation; manual restoration; growing meter and sensor security demands |

**Decision under discussion (source):** Fund bounded outage, work-order and customer coordination evidence before wider modernization.

**Illustrative trigger (source):** A storm scenario generates conflicting outage, asset and work-order status; field and customer teams cannot reconcile ownership (invented learning context, not an actual incident).

**Scenario used in this demo:** A storm exposes load on outage, work and customer-communication systems; enterprise operations must stay clearly apart from grid control (SCADA and ADMS control actions are out of scope).

## 8. Signals, responses and autonomy tiers

**Tiers:** A answer or recommend only; B human-approved action; C never automated.

| Signal | Response | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Outage event backlog in the broker | Summarize backlog; propose replay priority | B human-approved action |
| Customer communications queue growth | Page the owner | B human-approved action |
| Work-order sync delay | Show delay and owner | A answer or recommend only |
| Capacity forecast ahead of a storm | Recommend scale decision | A answer or recommend only |
| Certificate expiry on a gateway | Create a task | B human-approved action |
| Request to issue a control action | Refused; out of scope | C never automated |

**Always tier C:** production HA failover, restore from backup, upgrades, production transport imports and authorization changes.

## 9. Change and transport policy (new design)

| Rule | Design |
|---|---|
| Pipeline | Development, quality, production; no direct production changes |
| Collision check | The pipeline shows two changes touching the same object |
| Freeze windows | Storm season and restoration periods; no change during a restoration event (illustrative). |
| Exceptions | Named approver, expiry date and rollback plan recorded |
| Evidence | Each import records who approved and when |

## 11. Hands-on exercise (synthetic data)

1. Take the six signals and write a one-page runbook for the first, including who is called and what is checked.
2. For each signal, confirm the tier and what evidence the operator needs before approving.
3. Draw the change pipeline and mark one collision.
4. List what is missing before any tier B action could be automated further.
5. Record decisions and gaps in the run log.

## Three tabletop tests

A tabletop test is a paper walk-through, not a technical test of a running system.

| Test | Setup | Expected finding |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Control boundary | An agent suggests a switching action | Refused; out of scope |
| T2 Storm freeze | A change is requested mid-restoration | Blocked; exception path |
| T3 Backlog replay priority | Replay would starve critical events | Priority classes protect critical events; owner approves |
