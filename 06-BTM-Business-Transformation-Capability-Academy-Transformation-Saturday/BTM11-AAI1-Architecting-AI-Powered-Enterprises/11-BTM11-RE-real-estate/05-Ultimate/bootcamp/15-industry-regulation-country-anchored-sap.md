## 1. The question this file answers

> *Which regulatory regimes touch the architecture, in the countries this case names, and what evidence would each need?*

**What this file shows:** the countries the case names, the regimes to verify for each, which design element each regime touches, and the evidence slot each would need.

**What it does not show:** legal advice, a compliance assessment or a claim that any regime is met. Every instrument is a pointer to verify with counsel.

## 6. Regimes to verify

**The case names no headquarters and no country.** No regime is listed. Adding one now would be an assumption. In discovery, confirm the countries, then use the structure below, which needs no country:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm each country where the enterprise operates or holds data |
| 2 | For each country, name the personal data, security, sector and AI regimes with counsel |
| 3 | Map each regime to the design elements in Demos 05 to 12 |
| 4 | Record the evidence slot and owner for each |

## 7. Design questions every regime raises

| Question | Design element | Owner (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Where does each data set live and who may move it? | Data products and residency rules (Demo 07) | OSP-P08 Mei Lin |
| What must be reported, to whom, and how fast? | Incident path (Demos 06 and 12) | OSP-P09 Omar Haddad |
| Which AI uses fall under a rule? | Use-case class and change control (Demo 09) | OSP-P08 Mei Lin |
| Which third parties are involved? | Dependency register (Demo 12) | OSP-P10 Daniel Weber |
| What evidence will an authority ask for? | Evidence slots in each demo | OSP-P14 Ingrid Sørensen |

**Cross-border flows:** the case names more than one country, so every flow between them needs a recorded basis and an owner. Do not assume one country's rule covers another.

## Three tabletop tests

A tabletop test is a paper walk-through, not a technical test of a running system.

| Test | Setup | Expected finding |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Country assumed | A team assumes a country | Rejected; the case names none |
| T2 Regime copied from another case | A team copies another case's regimes | Rejected; confirm the country in discovery first |
| T3 Claim of compliance | A page claims compliance | Rejected; no regime identified, no evidence |
