## Concept check (short answer)
- What does EWM manage?  
  *Answer: Receiving, putaway, picking, packing and inventory accuracy*
- Which series cover Concept 4?  
  *Answer: AIN6 Extended Warehouse Management and ADL5 Transportation Management*
- Why coordinate warehouse and transport?  
  *Answer: Fixing one without the other just moves the bottleneck*
- What does the Sustainability Control Tower do?  
  *Answer: Measures and manages environmental and social impact across the supply chain*
- Which series cover Concept 5?  
  *Answer: ASD9 Sustainability Control Tower and AAN0-SCM Analytics Cloud and Databricks*
- Why measure before reducing?  
  *Answer: You cannot reduce what you cannot measure*
- What makes AI trustworthy on supply chain data?  
  *Answer: Current integrated data plus governance and explainability*
- Which series cover Concept 6?  
  *Answer: AAI1-SCM Business AI, Joule, AI Agents; AIG2-SCM Integration Suite*
- What does the Integration Suite do for supply chain?  
  *Answer: Connects ERP, partners, carriers and data so information flows once*
