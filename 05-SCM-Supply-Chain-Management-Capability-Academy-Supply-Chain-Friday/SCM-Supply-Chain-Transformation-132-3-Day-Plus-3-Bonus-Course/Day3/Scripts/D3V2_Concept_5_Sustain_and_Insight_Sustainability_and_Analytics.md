**1. What is the concept?**
Sustainability Control Tower measures and manages environmental and social impact across the supply chain, such as emissions, waste and supplier practices. Analytics Cloud with Databricks turns supply chain data into dashboards, root-cause analysis and predictions. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Customers, regulators and investors now ask for evidence of sustainable operations, and cost and resilience gains hide in the same data. Without measurement, sustainability is a slogan and analytics is a report nobody acts on.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities company mapped emissions across its logistics network and found a handful of lanes responsible for most of the footprint. Rerouting them reduced emissions and freight cost together. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Credible, auditable sustainability reporting; Visibility of emissions and waste hotspots; Predictive insight on delays, demand and risk; Supply chain as a source of value, not only cost. [on screen: four benefits]
