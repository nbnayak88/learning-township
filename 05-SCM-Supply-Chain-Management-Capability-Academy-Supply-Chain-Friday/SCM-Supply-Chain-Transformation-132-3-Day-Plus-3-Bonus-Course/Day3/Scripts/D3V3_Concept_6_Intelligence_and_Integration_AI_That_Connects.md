**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and recommendations into planning, execution and logistics. The Integration Suite connects supply chain systems with ERP, partners, carriers and data platforms so information flows once. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI can only recommend well on complete, current supply chain data, and only be trusted if it is governed and explainable. Integration provides the reach across planning, execution and partners; governance provides the trust.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A planning team used an assistant to explain shortages, but only after integration gave it live inventory, orders and shipment status. Before that, it guessed; afterwards planners used it to resolve exceptions in minutes. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster exception handling and planning decisions; Automated handoffs across planning, plants, warehouses and carriers; Governed, auditable AI on operational data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]
