*Duration: 12-15 min*

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Store and Move: Warehousing and Transportation**. It anchors two product academies: AIN6 Extended Warehouse Management and ADL5 Transportation Management. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example AIN6-EN, AIN6-MA, AIN6-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Extended Warehouse Management runs receiving, putaway, picking, packing and inventory accuracy inside the warehouse. Transportation Management plans loads, carriers, routes, freight cost and delivery visibility outside it. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Warehouse errors and poor transport planning show up as late deliveries, damaged goods and high freight cost, and they are visible to customers. Optimising one without the other just moves the bottleneck.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A consumer discretionary retailer found its warehouse could pick faster than trucks could load. Coordinating wave planning with transport planning cut dock congestion and improved on-time delivery without adding staff. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Accurate inventory and faster order fulfilment; Optimised loads, carriers and freight spend; End-to-end shipment visibility; Higher on-time, in-full delivery. [on screen: four benefits]

### Day 3 V3: Concept 6: Intelligence and Integration: AI That Connects
*Duration: 12-15 min*

**Hook:** Intelligence without integration is a demo. Integration without intelligence is plumbing.

Did you share your notes in the previous video? [pause] If not, pause here, drop them in the comments, then come back. Sharing is how learning becomes yours.

Welcome back. Today's concept is **Intelligence and Integration: AI That Connects**. It anchors two product academies: AAI1-SCM Business AI, Joule and AI Agents for Supply Chain and AIG2-SCM Integration Suite for Supply Chain. Together with the 11 GICS sectors that is 22 of the 132 courses in our meta anchor file, for example AAI1-SCM-EN, AAI1-SCM-MA, AAI1-SCM-IN ... (22 courses in this concept). [show slide: concept map]

**1. What is the concept?**
Business AI, Joule and AI Agents bring assistance, automation and recommendations into planning, execution and logistics. The Integration Suite connects supply chain systems with ERP, partners, carriers and data platforms so information flows once. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
AI can only recommend well on complete, current supply chain data, and only be trusted if it is governed and explainable. Integration provides the reach across planning, execution and partners; governance provides the trust.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A planning team used an assistant to explain shortages, but only after integration gave it live inventory, orders and shipment status. Before that, it guessed; afterwards planners used it to resolve exceptions in minutes. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Faster exception handling and planning decisions; Automated handoffs across planning, plants, warehouses and carriers; Governed, auditable AI on operational data; A clean-core approach that stays upgrade-safe. [on screen: four benefits]

**Sector lens.** Sector lens: Health Care requires explainability and traceability for critical items; Industrials integrates with plant, supplier and carrier systems.
