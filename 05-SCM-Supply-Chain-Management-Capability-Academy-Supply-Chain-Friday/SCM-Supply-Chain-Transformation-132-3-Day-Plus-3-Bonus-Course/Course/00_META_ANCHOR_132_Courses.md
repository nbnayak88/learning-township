## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Consumer Staples plans around promotions and seasonality; Health Care plans around shelf life and critical availability; Energy plans around outages and maintenance windows.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Industrials depends on long lead-time components; Materials and Energy must balance capacity against volatile commodity demand.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Health Care manufacturing carries strict traceability and regulatory quality; Industrials focuses on uptime and yield.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Consumer Discretionary peaks around seasons; Energy and Materials move bulk and hazardous goods with strict safety rules.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Energy and Utilities face transition and reporting pressure; Consumer Staples faces supplier and packaging scrutiny.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Health Care requires explainability and traceability for critical items; Industrials integrates with plant, supplier and carrier systems.
