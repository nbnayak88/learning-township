**1. What is the concept?**
Digital Manufacturing connects the plan to the shop floor: work orders, execution, tracking and performance on the line. Quality Management defines inspections, controls and corrective actions so defects are prevented, caught and traced. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Plans mean nothing if production cannot execute them, and output means little if quality fails. Disconnected shop floor data hides losses, while late quality findings create scrap, recalls and damaged trust.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A materials producer saw a recurring defect only at final inspection. Linking shop floor data with quality records showed the cause in one machine setting, and the defect rate fell within weeks. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Plan-to-execution visibility on the shop floor; Less scrap, rework and unplanned downtime; Traceability from supplier batch to customer shipment; Quality built into the process, not inspected at the end. [on screen: four benefits]
