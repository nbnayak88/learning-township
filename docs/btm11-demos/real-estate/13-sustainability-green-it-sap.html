Evidence boundary: OmniSpace is a fictional educational case. The case sources hold little sustainability data for the IT estate; the data products, levers and tests below are new design. Nothing here is a record of a client implementation, an executed test or a realized benefit. Names marked synthetic carry the prefix RE-SYN and are new design. SAP product statements come from public SAP documentation reviewed 2026-11-10 and must be verified before real delivery.

1. The question this file answers

How does the architecture support sustainability reporting, and how does it keep the footprint of IT and AI itself visible?

What this file shows: two separate topics for OmniSpace: (1) data products that support sustainability reporting, and (2) the footprint of the IT and AI estate, with levers, an owner and an evidence rule.

What it does not show: an emissions figure, a footprint measurement or a reduction claim.

Attribute Case baseline

Topic What it covers Why separate

8. Green IT levers (new design)

Lever Idea Link to other demos Owner (proposed)
