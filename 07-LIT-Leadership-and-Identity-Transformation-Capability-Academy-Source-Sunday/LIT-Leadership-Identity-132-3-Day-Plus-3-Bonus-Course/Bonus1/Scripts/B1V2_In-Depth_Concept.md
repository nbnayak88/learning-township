# Bonus 1 V2: In-Depth Concept: Write Your Identity and Purpose Blueprint
*Duration: 20-30 min*

**Hook:** Identity Blueprint is where theory becomes income and impact.

**Take notes.** Keep a page open. Write three things as we go and share them towards the end. [pause]

**1. What is the concept?** (about 3 min)
An identity blueprint is a one-page statement of who you are, what you value, what you are here to do and whom you serve, written so clearly that you can use it to make a decision this week. [show slide]

**2. Why is it important?** (about 4 min)
Without a written identity, every pressure, title and opinion gets a vote on who you are. A blueprint gives you a standard to check your choices against, and a story you can share with confidence. Here is what I have seen. A mid-career engineer wrote his blueprint on a train and realised his last three job moves had all violated his top value. He turned down a bigger title and joined a smaller team, and said it was the first decision that felt like his own.

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way** (about 3 min)
When this is done well: Faster, calmer decisions under pressure; Clear answers to who you are and what you stand for; Consistent behaviour that builds trust; A foundation for every later leadership choice. [on screen: benefits]

**4. How to do it? Tactical** (about 10-12 min)
Follow these steps in order. Pause the video at each step and do it. [screen share]
1. Set a 45-minute timer and switch off notifications.
2. List five moments when you felt most alive and proud; note what you were doing.
3. Circle the values that repeat and pick your top three.
4. Write one sentence for each: who I am, what I stand for, whom I serve.
5. Add one non-negotiable: a line you will not cross even if it costs you.
6. Pick one sector and course ID, for example Energy (LIT01-IDT1-ENE) or Health Care (LIT04-IDT4-HC), and write how your blueprint shows up there.
7. Read it to a trusted peer and refine one line from their reaction.
