# Bonus 1 Quiz

**Q1. What does an identity blueprint contain?**
*Answer: Who you are, what you value, what you are here to do and whom you serve*

**Q2. Why write a non-negotiable?**
*Answer: It gives you a line to hold when pressure rises*

**Q3. How do you refine it?**
*Answer: Read it to a trusted peer and adjust one line*
