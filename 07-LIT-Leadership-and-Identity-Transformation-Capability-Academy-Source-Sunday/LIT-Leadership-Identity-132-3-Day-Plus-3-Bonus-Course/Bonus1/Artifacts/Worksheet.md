# Bonus 1 Worksheet: Write Your Identity and Purpose Blueprint

- [ ] Set a 45-minute timer and switch off notifications.
- [ ] List five moments when you felt most alive and proud; note what you were doing.
- [ ] Circle the values that repeat and pick your top three.
- [ ] Write one sentence for each: who I am, what I stand for, whom I serve.
- [ ] Add one non-negotiable: a line you will not cross even if it costs you.
- [ ] Pick one sector and course ID, for example Energy (LIT01-IDT1-ENE) or Health Care (LIT04-IDT4-HC), and write how your blueprint shows up there.
- [ ] Read it to a trusted peer and refine one line from their reaction.
