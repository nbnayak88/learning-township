# Bonus 1 Codex: Write Your Identity and Purpose Blueprint

**Tool:** Identity Blueprint One-Pager (Codex template)

## What
An identity blueprint is a one-page statement of who you are, what you value, what you are here to do and whom you serve, written so clearly that you can use it to make a decision this week.

## Why
Without a written identity, every pressure, title and opinion gets a vote on who you are. A blueprint gives you a standard to check your choices against, and a story you can share with confidence.

## Benefits
- Faster, calmer decisions under pressure
- Clear answers to who you are and what you stand for
- Consistent behaviour that builds trust
- A foundation for every later leadership choice

## Tactical steps
1. Set a 45-minute timer and switch off notifications.
2. List five moments when you felt most alive and proud; note what you were doing.
3. Circle the values that repeat and pick your top three.
4. Write one sentence for each: who I am, what I stand for, whom I serve.
5. Add one non-negotiable: a line you will not cross even if it costs you.
6. Pick one sector and course ID, for example Energy (LIT01-IDT1-ENE) or Health Care (LIT04-IDT4-HC), and write how your blueprint shows up there.
7. Read it to a trusted peer and refine one line from their reaction.
