# Bonus 2 Codex: Build Your Inner Operating System: Daily Practice and Weekly Review

**Tool:** Daily Practice Tracker and Weekly Review sheet (Codex)

## What
An inner operating system is a small set of daily practices (reflection, a focus block, a digital boundary, a learning and sharing habit) tracked on one sheet and reviewed once a week, so that change happens by routine and not by willpower.

## Why
Insight fades within days if nothing in your routine carries it. Tiny repeatable practices, with a weekly review, are what turn a good intention into a lasting identity.

## Benefits
- Change that lasts beyond the motivation
- Calmer emotions through daily reflection
- More focused time and fewer digital distractions
- Visible proof of progress every week

## Tactical steps
1. Choose four practices: reflect, focus, disconnect, learn and share (one line each).
2. Shrink each to a two-minute minimum version.
3. Attach each to an existing habit (after coffee, before sleep).
4. Set one digital boundary, such as no phone for the first hour.
5. Tick the Daily Practice Tracker every day for seven days.
6. Run a ten-minute Weekly Review: what worked, what blocked, one adjustment.
7. Share your review with your accountability partner.
