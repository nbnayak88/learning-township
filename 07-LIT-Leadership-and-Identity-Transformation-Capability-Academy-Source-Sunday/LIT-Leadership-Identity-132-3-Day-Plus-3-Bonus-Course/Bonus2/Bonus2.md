### B2 V2: In-Depth Concept: Build Your Inner Operating System: Daily Practice and Weekly Review
*Duration: 20-30 min*

**Hook:** Daily Practice System is where theory becomes income and impact.

**Take notes.** Keep a page open. Write three things as we go and share them towards the end. [pause]

**1. What is the concept?** (about 3 min)
An inner operating system is a small set of daily practices (reflection, a focus block, a digital boundary, a learning and sharing habit) tracked on one sheet and reviewed once a week, so that change happens by routine and not by willpower. [show slide]

**2. Why is it important?** (about 4 min)
Insight fades within days if nothing in your routine carries it. Tiny repeatable practices, with a weekly review, are what turn a good intention into a lasting identity. Here is what I have seen. A founder who believed she had no time tried a two-minute version of each practice. After six weeks the habits had grown on their own, and her team noticed she was calmer in meetings before she told anyone why.

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way** (about 3 min)
When this is done well: Change that lasts beyond the motivation; Calmer emotions through daily reflection; More focused time and fewer digital distractions; Visible proof of progress every week. [on screen: benefits]

**4. How to do it? Tactical** (about 10-12 min)
Follow these steps in order. Pause the video at each step and do it. [screen share]
1. Choose four practices: reflect, focus, disconnect, learn and share (one line each).
2. Shrink each to a two-minute minimum version.
3. Attach each to an existing habit (after coffee, before sleep).
4. Set one digital boundary, such as no phone for the first hour.
5. Tick the Daily Practice Tracker every day for seven days.
6. Run a ten-minute Weekly Review: what worked, what blocked, one adjustment.
7. Share your review with your accountability partner.

**5. Resources and tool** (about 3 min)
Your tool for this bonus: **Daily Practice Tracker and Weekly Review sheet (Codex)**. Open it from the Codex linked below. [on screen: tool walkthrough] Assignment: run the tracker for seven days, complete one Weekly Review and share the three-line result with your partner.

**CTA.** Share your notes below: three lines on what you learned, what surprised you and what you will do first. Next video: the outro.
