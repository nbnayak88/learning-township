## Concept check (short answer)
- What do values do in daily life?  
  *Answer: They turn identity into choices, especially when it costs something*
- Which programs anchor Concept 1?  
  *Answer: LIT01 Purpose & Identity and LIT09 Integrity & Values*
- What happens without a clear identity?  
  *Answer: You borrow one from your title or the crowd and drift under pressure*
- What is a growth mindset?  
  *Answer: The belief, backed by practice, that ability grows through effort and feedback*
- Which programs anchor Concept 2?  
  *Answer: LIT02 Growth Mindset and LIT03 Emotional Freedom*
- What is emotional freedom?  
  *Answer: Noticing and naming emotions so they inform choices without controlling them*
- What does authentic leadership rest on?  
  *Answer: Real values, consistency, honesty and self-awareness*
- Which programs anchor Concept 3?  
  *Answer: LIT04 Authentic Leadership and LIT08 Meaningful Relationships*
- Why do relationships matter to leaders?  
  *Answer: They are the channel through which leadership actually works*
