**1. What is the concept?**
Purpose and identity answer who you are, what you are here to do and who you serve. Integrity and values turn that answer into daily choices: the few principles you will not trade, even when it costs you. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Without a clear identity, you borrow one from your title, your employer or the crowd, and your decisions drift with the pressure of the moment. Values are the compass that works when no one is watching.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A senior manager was promoted three times and felt emptier each time. Writing down her three non-negotiable values showed that she had been trading one of them for every promotion. She changed how she chose her next role, and her energy returned. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: A clear, written statement of purpose and identity; Faster, calmer decisions under pressure; Trust from others because your words and actions match; Direction when titles, roles and employers change. [on screen: four benefits]
