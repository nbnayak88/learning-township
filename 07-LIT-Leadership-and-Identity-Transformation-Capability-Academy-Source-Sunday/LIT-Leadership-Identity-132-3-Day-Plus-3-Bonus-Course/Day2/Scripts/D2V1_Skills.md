# Day 2 V1: Skills
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Ten skills. That is all that stands between you and the leader you want to become.

Welcome to Day 2, and congratulations on completing Day 1! [pause]

Your goal from the Goal Card needs skills. Here are the top ten. [show slide]

1. **Self-awareness and honest reflection**: See yourself clearly without judgment.
2. **Purpose and values clarity**: Know what you stand for and why.
3. **Growth mindset practice**: Turn effort, feedback and failure into progress.
4. **Emotional regulation**: Name feelings and choose your response.
5. **Authentic communication**: Say what is true with care.
6. **Listening and relationship building**: Build trust that lasts.
7. **Intentional time and attention design**: Spend life on what matters, including your digital life.
8. **Continuous learning and sharing**: Learn weekly and teach what you learn.
9. **Ethical decision-making**: Choose well in grey areas.
10. **Sector translation across 11 GICS sectors**: Apply one inner core to any industry.

Rate yourself one to five on each. Circle your lowest two; we will build them through the six concepts.
