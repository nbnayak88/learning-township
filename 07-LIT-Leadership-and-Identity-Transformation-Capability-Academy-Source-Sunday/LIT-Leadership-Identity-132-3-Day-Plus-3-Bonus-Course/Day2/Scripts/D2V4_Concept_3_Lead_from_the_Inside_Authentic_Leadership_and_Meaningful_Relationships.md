**1. What is the concept?**
Authentic leadership means leading from your real values, being consistent, honest and self-aware, and inviting others to do the same. Meaningful relationships are built through listening, reliability, candour and care, and they are the channel through which leadership actually works. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Authority gets compliance, while trust gets commitment. Leaders who hide behind a role create fear and silence, and relationships that are only transactional collapse the first time they are tested.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A plant manager started his weekly meeting by sharing one mistake of his own. Within two months, near-miss reports tripled, because his people finally felt safe to speak. Accidents fell the next year. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Higher trust and psychological safety on your team; Honest feedback reaching you early; Stronger, longer-lasting professional and personal bonds; Influence that survives a change of role. [on screen: four benefits]
