# Bonus 3 V2: In-Depth Concept: Lead Change in Others: Influence, Contribution and Your Capstone Portfolio
*Duration: 20-30 min*

**Hook:** Influence and Portfolio is where theory becomes income and impact.

**Take notes.** Keep a page open. Write three things as we go and share them towards the end. [pause]

**1. What is the concept?** (about 3 min)
Leading change in others means turning your own growth into service: a small contribution project (a mentoring circle, a team ritual, a community talk) and a capstone portfolio of three or four artifacts that prove your journey in a real sector. [show slide]

**2. Why is it important?** (about 4 min)
Growth that stays private stays fragile. Serving others tests what you have learned, extends your influence beyond your title, and gives you visible proof of the person you are becoming. Here is what I have seen. A utilities supervisor ran one lunch session on handling stress, using his own practice sheet. It became a monthly circle, and the portfolio he built from it opened a leadership development opportunity.

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way** (about 3 min)
When this is done well: Deeper learning through service; Influence that does not depend on a title; A shareable portfolio for interviews and promotions; A natural path to Pro and Star stages. [on screen: benefits]

**4. How to do it? Tactical** (about 10-12 min)
Follow these steps in order. Pause the video at each step and do it. [screen share]
1. Pick one group you can serve in 30 days (team, family, community).
2. Name the single change you want to help them make.
3. Design the smallest useful contribution: one session, one ritual or one resource.
4. Invite two champions who will help you.
5. Run it once and gather feedback with two questions.
6. Assemble your portfolio: identity blueprint, practice tracker results, contribution canvas, sector reflection.
7. Publish it on one page and share the link in the community.
