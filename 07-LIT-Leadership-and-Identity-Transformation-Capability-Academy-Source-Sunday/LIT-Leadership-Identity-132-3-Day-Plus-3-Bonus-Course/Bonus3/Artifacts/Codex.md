# Bonus 3 Codex: Lead Change in Others: Influence, Contribution and Your Capstone Portfolio

**Tool:** Contribution Project Canvas and Capstone Portfolio Codex

## What
Leading change in others means turning your own growth into service: a small contribution project (a mentoring circle, a team ritual, a community talk) and a capstone portfolio of three or four artifacts that prove your journey in a real sector.

## Why
Growth that stays private stays fragile. Serving others tests what you have learned, extends your influence beyond your title, and gives you visible proof of the person you are becoming.

## Benefits
- Deeper learning through service
- Influence that does not depend on a title
- A shareable portfolio for interviews and promotions
- A natural path to Pro and Star stages

## Tactical steps
1. Pick one group you can serve in 30 days (team, family, community).
2. Name the single change you want to help them make.
3. Design the smallest useful contribution: one session, one ritual or one resource.
4. Invite two champions who will help you.
5. Run it once and gather feedback with two questions.
6. Assemble your portfolio: identity blueprint, practice tracker results, contribution canvas, sector reflection.
7. Publish it on one page and share the link in the community.
