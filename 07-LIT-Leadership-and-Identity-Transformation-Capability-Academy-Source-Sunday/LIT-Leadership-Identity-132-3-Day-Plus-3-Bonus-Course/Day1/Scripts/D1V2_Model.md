# Day 1 V2: Model
*Duration: 10-12 min*

**Hook:** Every organisation I have worked with had the same problem: lots of advice, no map. [pause]

Before I teach you anything, let me earn the right to. I have spent my career leading transformation for global organisations and coaching people through their own, and I have seen leaders rise and stall at close range. What you will learn here comes from real experience, not theory.

**Story and mission.** Early in my career I watched a brilliant leader stall, not because of skill, but because nobody had helped them see who they were and what they stood for. That day I decided to build a map. My mission: Architecting for a Better World, one inner transformation at a time.

**The model.** Here is the map. [show slide] Twelve programs: Purpose and Identity, Growth Mindset, Emotional Freedom, Authentic Leadership, an Intentional Life, Continuous Learning, a Balanced Digital Life, Meaningful Relationships, Integrity and Values, a Fulfilling Life, Inner Transformation, and a Better World. Eleven GICS sectors: Energy, Materials, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary, Consumer Staples, Health Care, Financials, Information Technology, Communication Services, Utilities, and Real Estate. Each course ID follows one rule: MDM code, Wisdom code and sector, like LIT01-IDT1-ENE. 12 x 11 = 132.

We compress the twelve into six concepts of two programs each, so every concept covers 22 courses. Learn six concepts, translate through one sector lens, and you have covered all 132.

**Take notes** now. Presence matters: if you are not writing, you are not learning. [pause] Reinforcement: say the formula once more out loud.

**CTA.** Take two minutes now and share your notes below. Three lines is enough: what you learned, what surprised you, what you will do. Next video: Self Discovery.
