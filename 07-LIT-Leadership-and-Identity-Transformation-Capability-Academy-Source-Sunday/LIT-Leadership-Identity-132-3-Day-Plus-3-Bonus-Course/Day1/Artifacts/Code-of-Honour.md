# Code of Honour

1. I honour time: I show up and I finish what I start.
2. I honour truth: I share what I actually learned, not what sounds good.
3. I honour each other: I help at least one person every day.
4. I share my notes after every video.
5. I credit my sources and never share confidential client or employer data.
6. I surrender to the system: I follow it fully before I judge it.
