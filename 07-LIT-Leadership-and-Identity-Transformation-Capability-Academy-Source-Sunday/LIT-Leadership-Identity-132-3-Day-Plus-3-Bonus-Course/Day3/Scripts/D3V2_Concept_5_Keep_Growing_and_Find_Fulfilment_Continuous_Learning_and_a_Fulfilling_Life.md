**1. What is the concept?**
Continuous learning is a repeatable system for acquiring, applying and sharing new skills and ideas across your life. A fulfilling life is the sense that your work, relationships, health, learning and contribution fit together and mean something to you. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Skills and roles expire faster than ever, and success without fulfilment feels hollow. Learning keeps you relevant, and fulfilment tells you what the learning is for.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. An engineer committed to one hour of learning and one act of sharing every week. Two years later she was leading the training program in her division, and said the sharing mattered more than the learning. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Skills that stay current as industries change; A weekly learning habit that compounds; A clear view of what fulfilment means to you across the five dimensions of life; Less regret and more sense of progress. [on screen: four benefits]
