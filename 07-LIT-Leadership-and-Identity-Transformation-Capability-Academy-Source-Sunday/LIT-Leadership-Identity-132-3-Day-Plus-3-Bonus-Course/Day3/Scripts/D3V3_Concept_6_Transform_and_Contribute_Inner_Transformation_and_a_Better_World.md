**1. What is the concept?**
Inner transformation is the lasting shift in beliefs, habits and identity that comes from reflection, practice and honest feedback, not from information alone. A better world is the outward result: using your growth to serve people, organisations and communities beyond yourself. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Knowledge without change is entertainment, and growth without contribution is self-absorption. Transformation proves itself when it changes what you do for others.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A utilities executive used what he had learned about himself to start a mentoring circle for young engineers in his city. Two years later, the circle had grown to sixty members, and he said it changed him more than them. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: Lasting change, not a weekend of motivation; A personal contribution project with real impact; Role-model influence on your team and family; A legacy that outlasts your job title. [on screen: four benefits]
