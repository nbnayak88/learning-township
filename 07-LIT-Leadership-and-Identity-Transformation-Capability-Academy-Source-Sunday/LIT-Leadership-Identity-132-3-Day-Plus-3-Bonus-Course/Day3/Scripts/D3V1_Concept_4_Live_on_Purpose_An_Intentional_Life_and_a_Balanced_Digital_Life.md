**1. What is the concept?**
An intentional life means deciding what matters, then arranging your time, energy and attention around it. A balanced digital life means choosing how, when and why you use technology, so that devices serve your goals instead of harvesting your attention. [pause]

**2. Why is it important?**
Attention is the raw material of a life. Without a deliberate design, urgent noise displaces important work, relationships and rest, and years pass without the things you said mattered.
Here is a real pattern I have seen. A founder tracked one week of screen time and found nearly four hours of unplanned scrolling. She replaced the first hour of every day with a deliberate priority, and finished a book she had postponed for three years. [pause]

**3. Benefits of implementing it the right way**
When you get this right, here is what changes: More hours spent on what you decided matters; Deeper focus and better work quality; Better sleep, presence and relationships; Less anxiety from constant comparison and noise. [on screen: four benefits]
