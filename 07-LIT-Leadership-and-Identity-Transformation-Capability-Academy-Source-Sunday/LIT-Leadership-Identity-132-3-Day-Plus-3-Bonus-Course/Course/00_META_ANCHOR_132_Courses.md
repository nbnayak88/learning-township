## Sector lens: same architecture, 11 translations

- **C1**: Sector lens: Health Care and Financials test integrity daily through patient and client trust; Energy and Materials test it through safety and environmental trade-offs.
- **C2**: Sector lens: Information Technology and Communication Services reward fast learning and public failure; Utilities and Industrials reward steady calm under operational risk.
- **C3**: Sector lens: Industrials and Consumer Staples lead large frontline workforces; Financials and Real Estate lead through long client relationships.
- **C4**: Sector lens: Communication Services and Information Technology live inside the attention economy; Health Care and Energy run shifts, where rest design matters most.
- **C5**: Sector lens: Information Technology and Health Care change knowledge fastest; Utilities and Real Estate reward deep, long-cycle expertise.
- **C6**: Sector lens: Energy and Utilities shape communities directly; Financials and Real Estate shape how people save and where they live.
