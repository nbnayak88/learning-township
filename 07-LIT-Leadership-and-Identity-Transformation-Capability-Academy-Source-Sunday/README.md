## Free 7-Day Leadership & Identity Origin Story Challenge

[TagMango Level Up — The Origin Story: Leadership & Identity Transformation](challenges/the-origin-story/leadership-identity-7-day-tagmango-level-up.md) includes a welcome post, seven beginner-friendly daily missions and a final celebration. It connects values, self-reflection, listening, commitments, leadership practice, social impact and 1970–2050 context through short text submissions. A coach-only appendix distinguishes voluntary personal reflection from formal project traceability across requirements, testing, go-live readiness and outcomes.
